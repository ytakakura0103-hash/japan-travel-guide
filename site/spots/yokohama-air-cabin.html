Yokohama Air Cabin

Yokohama

Opened in 2021 as an urban cable car rather than a mountain ropeway, this line strings glass-walled gondolas across a stretch of Minato Mirai between Sakuragicho Station and Unga Park near Cosmo World. The short ride gives a low-altitude, slow-moving view of the district that is different from the Landmark Tower's straight-down perspective, taking in the canal, the Ferris wheel, and the Red Brick Warehouse roofline as the cabin swings past.

Because each cabin only seats a handful of people, queues can build up on weekend afternoons and during evening illumination events, so a weekday visit or an early evening ride just after sunset, when the city lights start to come on but the wait is shorter, tends to work best. Round-trip tickets cost more than one-way, so it is worth deciding in advance whether you plan to walk back.

The ride works well as a scenic shortcut between Sakuragicho Station and the Cosmo World and Cup Noodles Museum area, saving a fifteen-minute walk while adding a photo opportunity most first-time visitors to Yokohama do not expect. It is at its most striking after dark, when the gondolas cross above a fully lit Minato Mirai skyline.
