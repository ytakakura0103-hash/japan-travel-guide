Arima-Rokko Ropeway

Kobe

This ropeway connects Arima Onsen at the base of the mountain with the Rokko summit area near Rokko Garden Terrace, making it possible to combine a hot-spring town visit with a mountaintop viewpoint in a single outing without backtracking through central Kobe. The ride itself takes around 10 to 15 minutes and climbs over forested terrain that turns vivid with autumn color in November, which is generally considered the most scenic time to ride.

Because it links two attractions that many visitors treat as separate day trips, using the ropeway as a through-route, arriving in Arima from Kobe, soaking in a public bathhouse, then riding up and over to Rokko Garden Terrace for the evening view, is a popular way to see both without much wasted travel time. Service can be seasonal or weather-dependent at times, so checking current operating days before planning a one-way trip through it is worth doing.

Autumn foliage weekends bring the heaviest demand for the ropeway specifically, since the ride itself becomes a scenic attraction rather than just transport; midweek travel in that season avoids most of the wait.
