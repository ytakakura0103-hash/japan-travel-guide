Unkai Terrace

Sapporo

Unkai Terrace sits atop a ropeway ride from the Tomamu resort, purpose-built as a viewing platform for unkai, the "sea of clouds" phenomenon that forms when temperature inversions trap low cloud in the valley below while the mountaintop stays clear. When conditions align, generally most likely in the early morning hours between late May and mid-October, the effect genuinely looks like the surrounding mountains are islands floating on a white ocean.

Because the phenomenon depends entirely on weather and temperature conditions that can't be guaranteed, the resort publishes rough probability forecasts, but there's always a real chance of arriving to an ordinary cloudy or clear view rather than the cloud sea itself; it's worth setting expectations accordingly rather than treating a visit as a sure thing.

Several small platforms and a cloud-shaped swing structure are built into the mountaintop specifically for photos, and a cafe near the top sells hot drinks, useful given how early and cold the best viewing window tends to be even in summer.

Tomamu sits roughly midway between Sapporo and the Furano/Biei area, which makes it a workable stopover for travelers already road-tripping that route, though a dedicated early-morning visit generally means staying overnight at or near the resort the night before.

Tomamu, Shimukappu, Yufutsu District, Hokkaido 079-2204

About 2 hours from Sapporo: JR Limited Express Okhotsk or Super Ozora to Tomamu Station (about 1 hour 40 minutes), or roughly 1.5-2 hours by car via the Doto Expressway; the ropeway to the terrace departs from the resort base.
