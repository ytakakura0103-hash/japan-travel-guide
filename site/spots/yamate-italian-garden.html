Yamate Italian Garden

Yokohama

The garden takes its name from its Italian-style formal layout rather than any direct historical link to Italy, and its centerpiece is a Western-style house, one of several original foreign residences the city relocated here after the Great Kanto Earthquake scattered the original Yamate settlement. The building's interior is open to visitors and furnished to evoke the domestic life of a foreign resident in the early 20th century.

The surrounding beds are planted for rotating seasonal color, with tulips in spring and roses later in the year among the more reliable highlights, and the layout gives clear views out over the bluff toward the harbor below, a quieter vantage point than the more famous Harbor View Park nearby. Because it sits slightly off the main Yamate walking route between Motomachi and the Foreign Cemetery, it draws fewer visitors than the marquee stops.

It pairs naturally with a visit to Berrick Hall and the Ehrismann Residence, since all three are part of the same scattered collection of preserved Yamate houses, and treating them as a loop rather than isolated stops makes for a fuller sense of what the foreign settlement here once looked like.

Yamate-cho, Naka-ku, Yokohama
