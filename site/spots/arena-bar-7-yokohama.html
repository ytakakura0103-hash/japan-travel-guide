Arena Bar 7

Yokohama

Arena Bar 7 occupies the seventh floor of K-Arena Yokohama, the large concert and events venue that opened in 2024, and offers panoramic night views over Yokohama Bay from roughly 120 seats, a scale that keeps it from feeling as cramped as many smaller hotel or rooftop bars. Rather than a cover charge, it runs on a drink-minimum system, and unlike many arena-attached bars, it stays open to visitors even on nights without a scheduled event, making it accessible well beyond concert-goers.

That accessibility is a real point in its favor: most arena bars only operate around event schedules, but Arena Bar 7 functions as a standing nightlife option in its own right, giving visitors a reason to visit even if nothing is playing at the arena that evening. Its Minatomirai-adjacent location puts it within a short walk of the bay's other major night-view spots, offering a genuine alternative vantage point over familiar scenery.

Because it's tied to a major events venue, checking whether a concert or event is scheduled on a planned visit night is worth doing, since crowd levels and atmosphere shift considerably between event and non-event nights. Either way, it's one of the more spacious and view-forward bar options in the Minatomirai area.

K-Arena Yokohama, 7F, Nishi Ward, Yokohama
