V2 TOKYO

Tokyo

V2 TOKYO opened in 2013 and has since positioned itself as Roppongi's largest and most production-heavy nightclub, built around a large main floor with an elaborate lighting and stage setup designed for big-name touring DJs and hip-hop and EDM-focused events. It fits the general character of Roppongi's nightlife district described elsewhere in this guide, international, English-friendly, and unapologetically club-scaled, but at a level of scale and production value that sets it apart from the smaller bars and lounges nearby.

Doors open late, from 9pm, and the club runs until 5am, so it functions as a genuine late-night destination rather than an early-evening stop; most of the real activity picks up well after midnight. Table and VIP bottle service is a significant part of the business model here, common at Tokyo's larger clubs, so budget-conscious visitors should expect general admission and standing areas to be the more affordable way to experience it.

As with Roppongi's nightlife district generally, the same caution about street touts applies on the walk there: V2 TOKYO doesn't need or use touts itself, so anyone approaching you on the street claiming to represent the club or offering a shortcut in is not affiliated with it.

Tower of Vabel 1F/B1F, 7-13-7 Roppongi, Minato City, Tokyo 106-0032

About 3 minutes on foot from Roppongi Station (Tokyo Metro Hibiya Line), or 5 minutes from the Toei Oedo Line exit.
