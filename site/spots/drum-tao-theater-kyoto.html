DRUM TAO Theater Kyoto

Kyoto

DRUM TAO built its reputation on stages well beyond Japan, touring internationally with a high-energy blend of taiko drumming, choreography, and theatrical staging that draws more on rock-concert production values than a traditional cultural performance. Its dedicated Kyoto theater, which opened in April 2026 on the ninth floor of the Avanti building just outside Kyoto Station's south exit, gives the group a permanent home base for the first time in the city, running regular evening performances rather than occasional touring dates.

The theater's location makes it one of the most convenient evening entertainment options in Kyoto for visitors without a car or a long train ride, sitting almost directly above the Hachijo exit that most Shinkansen travelers pass through. Two performances typically run each evening, giving visitors staying near the station flexibility to fit a show in around dinner plans.

Because the group's style leans toward spectacle and physical performance rather than the quieter, more contemplative traditional arts covered elsewhere in this guide, such as Gion Corner's rotating traditional-arts showcase, it suits visitors looking for a livelier, more high-energy evening. Given how recently the theater opened, checking the current schedule and ticket availability directly is worth doing before planning an evening around it.

31 Higashikujo Nishisannocho, Minami Ward, Kyoto (Avanti Building, 9F)
