Umi no Koen (Sea Park)

Yokohama

Built on reclaimed land in Kanazawa Ward, Umi no Koen gave Yokohama something the city's industrial harbor otherwise lacks: an actual sandy beach open to the public, backed by lawns and pine trees rather than concrete piers. Shellfish digging at low tide, particularly for asari clams, is a popular family activity here in the warmer months, and windsurfers and small sailboats regularly use the sheltered bay on breezier days.

The view across the water toward the Hakkeijima bridge and Sea Paradise amusement park gives the beach a distinctive skyline, especially at sunset, when the sky over the bay turns a deep orange that photographers favor. Swimming areas are seasonal and clearly marked, so it is worth checking current safety and swimming-season notices before planning to get in the water rather than just walk the sand.

Unlike Japan's famous natural beaches further along the coast, this is a deliberately engineered urban beach, and it draws mostly Yokohama and Kanagawa families rather than international beach tourists, giving it a relaxed, unpretentious local character. It pairs naturally with a visit to neighboring Nojima Park for a fuller half-day along this stretch of Kanazawa Ward's coastline.

6 Namiki, Kanazawa-ku, Yokohama
