Ichibancho (evening)

Sendai

Ichibancho is best known during the day as Sendai's main covered shopping arcade, but after dark it takes on a second identity as a calmer counterpart to Kokubuncho a few blocks north. Where Kokubuncho is built for a loud, late night out across thousands of bars and clubs, Ichibancho's evening scene leans toward quieter lounges, hideaway izakaya, and considered bars aimed at a crowd looking for a good drink and an earlier finish rather than a full night of bar-hopping.

Locals draw a fairly clear line between the two districts: Kokubuncho for a flashy, extended night out, Ichibancho for a solid drink and done. That distinction is worth knowing before deciding which side of central Sendai's nightlife to explore on a given evening, particularly for visitors who'd rather avoid Kokubuncho's louder, more crowded stretches.

Because it runs along the same arcades covered elsewhere in this guide for daytime shopping, Ichibancho's evening scene is easy to fold into a day that starts with shopping and ends with a quiet drink, without needing to change location. Prices and quality here tend to run a notch higher on average than in Kokubuncho's densest budget-bar stretches.
