Nijo Fish Market

Sapporo

Nijo Market is far smaller than Sapporo's central wholesale market at Chuo Oroshiuri, but that scale is part of the appeal: it's walkable in ten minutes, its stalls sell direct to visitors, and many will cook what they sell on the spot, from grilled scallops to sea urchin over rice. It has operated in some form since the early 20th century, originally serving the fishing boats that once docked closer to the city center.\n\nBecause it's a working market as much as a tourist stop, quality and prices vary stall to stall, and it rewards a little browsing before committing to a meal, though English menus and card payment are not universal, so having some cash on hand helps. Crab, uni and salmon roe are the standout items most visitors come for, particularly outside peak summer when Hokkaido's cold-water seafood is at its best.\n\nMorning is genuinely the right time to visit, both because that's when the freshest stock is out and because a late-morning tour-group rush can make the narrow aisles feel crowded; arriving at opening avoids both issues. Afternoon visits will find some stalls already closed or picked over. It's an easy stop to combine with a walk through Odori Park, given the short distance between the two.

Minami 3 Jo Higashi 1-chome, Chuo-ku, Sapporo, Hokkaido
