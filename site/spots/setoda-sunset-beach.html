Setoda Sunset Beach

Hiroshima

Unlike the rocky coastlines common elsewhere on the Shimanami Kaido islands, Setoda's beach is a genuine sand strip long enough for a proper walk, with calm, shallow water that makes it popular with families in summer despite being far less crowded than mainland beaches. Facilities are simple — showers and a snack stand in season — which keeps it feeling more like a local beach than a resort.

The name isn't just marketing: the beach faces almost directly west, and on clear evenings the sun drops straight into the sea between the silhouettes of neighboring islands, a view locals rate as one of the better sunset spots along the whole cycling route.

It's an easy add-on to a Setoda visit built around Kosanji Temple or the Hirayama Ikuo Museum, and cyclists doing the Shimanami Kaido often time their day to pass through around dusk specifically for this stretch of coast.
