Shirogane Blue Pond

Sapporo

The pond formed somewhat by accident, as part of erosion-control dam work following a nearby volcanic eruption, and its unusual color comes from naturally occurring aluminum hydroxide in the water scattering light in a way that produces a striking, almost artificial-looking blue. Dead tree trunks standing in the shallow water add to the pond's slightly eerie, photogenic quality, and its international fame grew substantially after an image of it was used as a default Windows computer wallpaper.\n\nThe pond is free to visit and open year-round, with a parking area a short walk away; hours for the parking lot itself shift seasonally, running later into the evening in the colder months when winter illuminations sometimes light the pond after dark. Its color also shifts somewhat with the seasons and weather, tending toward its most vivid blue on clear days.\n\nMidday in peak summer brings the heaviest crowds, including tour buses; visiting early morning or during the winter illumination period tends to be calmer and, in winter's case, adds a genuinely different visual experience with the pond partially frozen. The pond sits along the same road connecting Biei to Shirogane Onsen and the Tokachidake hot spring area, making it easy to combine with a wider loop through the region.

Shirogane, Biei-cho, Kamikawa-gun, Hokkaido

Buses run from Biei Station toward Shirogane Onsen (about 20 minutes), stopping near the pond entrance; there is also a parking lot about a 2-minute walk away for those driving.
