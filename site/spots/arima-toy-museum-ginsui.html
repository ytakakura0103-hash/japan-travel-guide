Arima Toy Museum (Ginsui)

Kobe

Occupying a multi-story building a short walk from Arima's main hot spring street, this museum gathers an eclectic private collection built up over decades, ranging from early 20th-century European and Japanese tin toys to a dedicated teddy bear room and a hall of antique music boxes and automata that still perform on demand. It leans more toward adult collectors and design enthusiasts than a typical children's museum, though kids generally enjoy it too.

Because Arima's main draw is its baths, this museum tends to get overlooked by visitors focused purely on the onsen experience, which keeps it comfortably uncrowded even when the town's main street is busy. It makes a good midday break between bathhouse visits, particularly if the weather turns.

The building itself, a former ryokan, adds atmosphere with creaky wooden stairs and multiple small rooms rather than open gallery halls, so budget more time than the modest floor space might suggest. Admission is separate from the town's bathhouses, and a small gift shop near the entrance sells reproduction tin toys and music boxes.

Arima-cho, Kita Ward, Kobe

Open in Google Maps

From Sannomiya, take the subway to Tanigami then the Shintetsu line to Arima-Onsen Station; the museum is a short walk from the station within the town center.
