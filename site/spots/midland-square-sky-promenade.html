Midland Square Sky Promenade

Nagoya

Midland Square, home to Toyota's headquarters offices among other tenants, rises close to the tallest points in the Nagoya Station skyline, and its Sky Promenade observation deck is unusual for being genuinely open-air rather than sealed behind glass, so the city noise, wind, and temperature are all part of the experience. The 360-degree views take in the station towers, the sprawl of the city, and on clear days the mountains beyond.

Admission is charged separately from the shopping floors below, and a visit to the deck itself takes well under an hour, though it's easy to linger circling the perimeter for different angles. The lower floors house upscale boutiques and restaurants if you want to extend the stop.

Sunset is by far the most popular and most rewarding time to visit, which means lines build in the hour beforehand; arriving a little earlier secures a good spot along the railing before the crowd peaks. Windy or rainy days can close the deck entirely, so it's worth checking conditions before making it the centerpiece of a visit.
