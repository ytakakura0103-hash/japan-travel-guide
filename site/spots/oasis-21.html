Oasis 21

Nagoya

Nicknamed the 'Spaceship Aqua' locally, Oasis 21's elliptical glass roof holds a shallow layer of water that catches the sky and city lights, suspended above a sunken plaza and an underground bus terminal that keeps the whole structure genuinely functional rather than purely decorative. It sits directly across from the Nagoya TV Tower, so the two make an easy combined photo stop in central Sakae.

Walking across the glass roof and through the plaza is free, and the site works well as a quick fifteen-minute stop or a longer one if you linger at the shops and food stalls in the sunken plaza below.

Evening illumination is when the water roof looks its best, reflecting the tower and surrounding lights, and this draws a steady stream of visitors and photographers after dark, especially on weekends. A daytime visit trades some of that atmosphere for a much quieter, less crowded experience, particularly on weekday mornings.
