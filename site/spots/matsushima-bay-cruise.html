Matsushima Bay Sightseeing Cruise

Sendai

Several operators run sightseeing boats out of Matsushima's main pier, threading a course between the bay's small islands that's genuinely hard to appreciate fully from shore alone. From the water, the layered arrangement of pine-topped islets becomes much clearer, and the boats generally pass close enough to some formations to make out individual wind-shaped pine trees and eroded rock textures.\n\nA popular, slightly silly ritual on these cruises involves buying a bag of senbei crackers before boarding and feeding the seagulls that trail persistently behind the boat the entire ride, snapping crackers out of the air a few feet from passengers, which tends to be a hit with kids and a source of some chaos for anyone holding food loosely.\n\nStandard cruises run around fifty minutes and cost a moderate, set fare; some operators offer shorter or longer routes and covered versus open-deck seating. Midday departures tend to be the busiest and most crowded on the boat itself; an early morning or later afternoon sailing usually means more space to move around the deck and better light for photos of the islands.
