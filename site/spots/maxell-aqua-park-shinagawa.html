Maxell Aqua Park Shinagawa

Tokyo

Maxell Aqua Park Shinagawa is an aquarium built directly into a building at Shinagawa Station, combining standard fish and marine life tanks with projection mapping, changing lighting, and music to create a more theatrical, nightclub-like atmosphere than a typical aquarium, particularly after dark. A central jellyfish tank and a dolphin performance pool are among the main draws, with the dolphin shows lit up in shifting colors and synced to music rather than staged as a conventional daytime show.

Because it stays open into the evening, later than most aquariums, it works well as an after-dinner or date-night activity rather than only a daytime family outing, and the lighting design shifts noticeably between afternoon and evening visits, so the same tanks can look quite different depending on when you go. A seasonal outdoor area with rides operates during warmer months, adding a small amusement-park element alongside the aquarium itself.

Its location directly inside Shinagawa Station makes it one of the more convenient aquariums in Tokyo to reach, useful for visitors with limited time or connecting between train lines. A visit through the main tanks and a dolphin show takes a couple of hours. Weekday evenings tend to be calmer than weekend and holiday visits, when both families in the daytime and couples in the evening push attendance higher.
