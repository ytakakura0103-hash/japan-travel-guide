Senkoji Park

Hiroshima

Senkoji Park occupies the summit area around Senkoji Temple, reached either via the Onomichi Ropeway or a walking trail up through the temple district below. Its main draw is the observation platform, which looks out across Onomichi's densely packed rooftops, the narrow Onomichi Strait separating the mainland from Mukaishima island, and the wider Seto Inland Sea beyond, with the various bridges of the Shimanami Kaido sometimes visible in clear weather.

The park is planted with a large number of cherry trees and has long been recognized as one of Japan's notable cherry blossom viewing spots, drawing crowds in early April when the trees bloom against the backdrop of the temple's vermillion halls and the sea view below. Large, naturally weathered rock formations are scattered through the grounds, some associated with local folklore, adding texture to what is otherwise a fairly compact hilltop park.

Because the park sits directly around Senkoji Temple, most visitors treat the two as a single stop, and the walking paths down from the park connect into the broader Onomichi Temple Walk route through the hillside's other temples and narrow lanes.

Higashitsuchido-cho, Onomichi, Hiroshima Prefecture
