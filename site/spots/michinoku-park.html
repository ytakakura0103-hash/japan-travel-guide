National Michinoku Park (Michinoku Koen)

Sendai

Located just north of Sendai in Rifu, this large government-run park is one of only a handful of "national" parks in Japan, spread across wide lawns, forested walking paths, and seasonal flower fields that rotate through tulips, cosmos, and other blooms depending on the time of year. Play structures and open green space make it a natural fit for families with children.\n\nAdmission is modest, and bicycles can be rented to cover more ground, since the park is large enough that walking the whole thing in one visit takes a good half day. It's an easy drive or bus ride from central Sendai.\n\nBecause it caters mainly to local families rather than tourists, weekday mornings are especially quiet, even during peak flower season. Each season brings a different floral highlight, so the best time to visit depends on which bloom you're after, with some evening illumination events held in winter.
