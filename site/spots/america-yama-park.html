America Yama Park

Yokohama

Opened in 2009 on land that once housed American consular and military facilities after the war, giving the park its name, this green space was constructed on a deck built over the below-ground train station, an engineering quirk that puts you standing directly above the trains rumbling beneath your feet. The elevated position offers a different angle on the neighborhood than the street-level views most visitors get, looking down over Chinatown's roof tiles toward the bay.

Seasonal flower beds rotate through tulips, roses, and other plantings depending on the time of year, and because the park is small and slightly hidden behind the station structure, it rarely gets crowded even when Chinatown below is packed with visitors. A short escalator connects the park directly to the station platforms, which is a genuinely unusual piece of urban infrastructure worth experiencing on its own.

It works well as a five-minute breather between Chinatown and the Yamate heritage-house walk, letting you step out of the crowds for a quiet overlook before continuing uphill. Late afternoon light works nicely for photos looking out toward the harbor.

97-1 Yamate-cho, Naka-ku, Yokohama
