THE BEACH YOKOHAMA

Yokohama

THE BEACH YOKOHAMA transplants a distinctly California-inspired aesthetic into a residential pocket of Shin-Yamashita, combining an artificial sand beach, a wedding venue, and a cafe into a single complex of white buildings and palm trees that reads as genuinely out of place against the surrounding Japanese neighborhood. That contrast is precisely the point: the complex has built a following on social media specifically for how convincingly it recreates a West Coast beach setting without actually being anywhere near open ocean.

Because the site functions primarily as a wedding venue, the beach and cafe areas are generally accessible to visitors outside of active ceremonies, though it's worth checking current access policies before a visit, since wedding-venue schedules can affect what's open to the general public on a given day. The cafe offers a reasonable way to spend time on-site without needing any connection to a wedding taking place there.

Its slightly out-of-the-way Shin-Yamashita location, away from the main Yamashita Park and Chinatown tourist core, means it draws a more deliberate visitor than a passerby, making it best suited for anyone specifically seeking out its distinctive look for photography.

Shin-Yamashita, Naka Ward, Yokohama
