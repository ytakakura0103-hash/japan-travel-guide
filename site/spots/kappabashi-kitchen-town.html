Kappabashi Kitchen Town

Tokyo

Kappabashi Kitchen Town is a working supply street rather than a tourist attraction dressed up as one — its shops exist primarily to outfit restaurants across Tokyo with knives, tableware, uniforms, signage, and equipment, and that practical purpose shows in the no-frills storefronts and wholesale pricing found throughout the street. The strip is best known internationally for its shokuhin sampuru shops, which produce the strikingly realistic plastic food models displayed in restaurant windows across Japan; several stores here sell finished pieces as souvenirs and some offer hands-on workshops where visitors can make their own miniature plastic food replica.

Beyond the novelty of the food models, the knife shops along Kappabashi are a serious draw for cooking enthusiasts, with a range of Japanese kitchen knives spanning entry-level to professional-grade, and some shops offering engraving or sharpening services on the spot. A casual walk through the main street takes about thirty minutes, though browsing multiple knife or tableware shops in detail can extend a visit to two hours or more.

Most shops keep standard daytime retail hours and are closed or quieter on Sundays, so a weekday visit gives the best chance of finding shops open and staff available for questions. The street sits an easy walk from Asakusa, making it simple to combine with a visit to Senso-ji and the surrounding old town.

Nishiasakusa area, Taito City, Tokyo
