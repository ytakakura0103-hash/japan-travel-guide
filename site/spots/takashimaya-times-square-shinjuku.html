Takashimaya Times Square

Tokyo

Takashimaya's Shinjuku store, branded Times Square, opened in 1996 on the station's then-underdeveloped south side and has since anchored the growth of that whole area into a major retail district in its own right. Unlike the historic Nihombashi flagship, this is a fully modern complex, built as a cluster of connected buildings rather than a single historic structure, with the main department store as the core.

A distinguishing feature is the adjacent Times Square building, home to a large Kinokuniya bookstore (one of the biggest in Tokyo), a dedicated home and interior goods tower, and an IMAX movie theater, giving the complex a broader range of things to do than a typical department store visit. The scale makes it a practical stop for a half-day rather than a quick pass-through, especially with dining options spread across multiple floors and buildings.

Because it sits right at Shinjuku Station's south side, it connects naturally with NEWoMan and the rest of the newer south-exit development, making this stretch of Shinjuku a reasonable full-afternoon shopping circuit distinct from the more famous east-side Kabukicho-adjacent shopping streets.

5-24-2 Sendagaya, Shibuya City, Tokyo 151-8580
