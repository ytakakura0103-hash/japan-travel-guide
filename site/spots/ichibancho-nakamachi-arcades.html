Ichibancho & Chuo-dori Covered Arcades

Sendai

Sendai's downtown is threaded with covered pedestrian arcades, of which Ichibancho and Chuo-dori are the main spines, lined with everything from department stores and chain shops to small family-run confectioners and standing soba counters that have operated for generations. Branch off the main strip into the smaller side arcades and the shops get noticeably more local and less polished.

There's no cost to wander, and meals or snacks along the way can be as cheap as a bowl of noodles or as substantial as a full izakaya dinner. Being fully covered, it's also a reliable option on rainy or snowy days.

The main Ichibancho strip can get busy on weekends, but the smaller connecting arcades a block or two off the main path tend to be quieter and are where you'll find the more distinctly local shops. It's a good any-season stop, though covered shelter is especially appreciated during Sendai's snowy winter months.
