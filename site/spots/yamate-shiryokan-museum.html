Yamate Museum (Yamate Shiryokan)

Yokohama

Small enough to see in fifteen minutes, this independently operated museum occupies a modest wooden house that predates most of the grander Western-style residences nearby, giving it a claim to being the oldest building of its kind still standing in Yamate. Where the larger heritage houses have been restored into polished period showcases, this one keeps a cluttered, personal-collection feel, furniture, tableware, photographs, and daily-life objects gathered by the family that has run the museum for generations.\n\nThere is a modest entrance fee, and it is easy to walk right past the unassuming building if you are not specifically looking for it, tucked among the more prominent stops on the Yamate heritage trail. That low profile is exactly why it rewards visitors willing to seek it out: no crowds, no roped-off rooms, just close-up access to genuine period objects.\n\nPair it with the nearby Yamate 111 Bankan or Harbor View Park for a fuller sense of the neighborhood, since this museum works best as one stop among several rather than a standalone destination. Cash payment is the safest assumption for the entrance fee.

247 Yamate-cho, Naka-ku, Yokohama

Open in Google Maps

About 8 minutes on foot from Minatomirai Line Motomachi-Chukagai Station (exit 6).
