JMSDF Kure Museum (Iron Whale)

Hiroshima

Nicknamed 'Tetsu no Kujira-kan,' or Iron Whale Museum, after its centerpiece exhibit, this free museum is centered on the Akishio, a 76-meter diesel-electric submarine decommissioned in 2004 and preserved close to how it looked in active service. Visitors can walk through much of the interior, seeing the cramped berths, control stations, and equipment that defined submarine life.\n\nThe rest of the museum, spread over three floors, covers the JMSDF's history and its close relationship with Kure as a naval city stretching back over a century, plus a floor dedicated to minesweeping operations, an area where Japan's postwar navy has played an outsized international role.\n\nOn the ground floor, the JMSDF Café serves navy curry from the actual mess-hall recipe used aboard the Akishio — a nod to the old imperial and current self-defense force tradition of serving curry every Friday to help sailors track the days at sea. It's a small, specific detail that makes the stop feel less like a static museum and more like an extension of naval life.

5-32 Takaramachi, Kure, Hiroshima
