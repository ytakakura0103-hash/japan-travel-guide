Hakata Riverain Mall

Fukuoka

Hakata Riverain Mall sits directly above Nakasu-Kawabata Station and combines retail with a genuinely notable cultural anchor: the Fukuoka Asian Art Museum, the only museum in the world dedicated specifically to modern and contemporary Asian art. That pairing gives the complex a cultural weight beyond its roughly seventy stores spread across basement two to the sixth floor, making it worth a visit even for travelers whose primary interest is the museum rather than shopping.

The complex also houses Hotel Okura Fukuoka and the Hakataza Theater, a venue for traditional Japanese performing arts, rounding out a mix of shopping, art, hospitality, and performance under one roof. Retail positioning has shifted over the years from an initial luxury-mall concept toward a more family-friendly tenant mix, reflecting broader changes in how the complex serves its Nakasu-area customer base.

The Fukuoka Asian Art Museum has periods of temporary closure for refurbishment, so checking its current status before planning a visit specifically around it is worthwhile. Its location directly by Nakasu-Kawabata Station makes it an easy stop when crossing between Nakasu's nightlife and Kawabata's shopping arcade, both covered elsewhere in this guide.
