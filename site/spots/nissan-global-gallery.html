Nissan Global Headquarters Gallery

Yokohama

Occupying the ground floor of Nissan's headquarters tower, this gallery gives car enthusiasts, and plenty of casual visitors, a chance to see current production models, performance cars like the GT-R, and occasional concept or motorsport vehicles up close without any admission charge. Displays rotate periodically, so what is on the floor during any given visit can vary, sometimes leaning toward electric vehicle technology, other times spotlighting Nissan's racing heritage.

Compared to the more elaborate corporate museums some manufacturers run, this is a relatively compact, straightforward showroom rather than a full interactive experience, which makes it an easy twenty-to-thirty-minute stop rather than a half-day outing. A small merchandise corner sells Nissan-branded goods for anyone wanting a souvenir beyond the usual Yokohama trinkets.

Because it sits right by Yokohama Station rather than out among the harbor attractions, it is a convenient stop to slot in right before catching a train, whether you are arriving in the city or heading out. It appeals particularly to visitors with any interest in Japanese car culture, and it costs nothing to walk through even if you only have a few spare minutes between trains.

1-1 Takashima, Nishi-ku, Yokohama
