Katta Gorge (Katta Kyokoku)

Sendai

Cut by volcanic activity on the Miyagi side of Mount Zao, Katta Gorge is a steep-walled ravine visible from a roadside viewing platform called Takimidai, which looks out over several waterfalls tumbling through the gorge at once. It sits along the lower stretch of the Zao Echo Line, the scenic toll road that climbs toward the famous Okama crater lake further up the mountain.\n\nThe Takimidai lookout is free to visit with nearby parking, and most people spend fifteen to twenty minutes taking in the view before continuing up or down the mountain road.\n\nBecause most visitors are focused on reaching Okama crater higher up, this lower gorge viewpoint gets comparatively little attention despite offering some of the most reliable early-season foliage color on the whole route. Peak color generally falls in late September into October, arriving here noticeably earlier than at the mountain's base.
