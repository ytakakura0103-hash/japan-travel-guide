Zou-no-hana Park and Terrace

Yokohama

The original curved pier here, built when the port first opened in 1859, was nicknamed the elephant's trunk for its distinctive shape, and although the historic structure is long gone, the city recreated its curving form when it opened this park in 2009 to mark the port's 150th anniversary. It sits between Osanbashi Pier and the Red Brick Warehouse but attracts a fraction of the foot traffic either of those draws, making it a genuinely calmer stretch of waterfront to sit and look at the harbor.\n\nA small cafe and gallery space called Zou-no-hana Terrace hosts rotating art exhibitions and events, giving the park a cultural layer beyond just scenery, and it is worth a look inside if something happens to be on. At night, panels along the pier are lit in colors that shift through the evening, a subtler lighting display than the big illuminations elsewhere on the waterfront.\n\nBecause it connects directly to both Osanbashi and the Red Brick Warehouse, it is easy to fold into a longer harbor walk rather than visiting on its own, and it rewards visitors who are willing to slow down between the two bigger attractions on either side.

Kaigan-dori, Naka-ku, Yokohama
