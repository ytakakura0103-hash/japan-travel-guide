Nakanoshima Museum of Art, Osaka

Osaka

Opened in 2022 after a long and often-delayed planning process, this museum's most immediately striking feature is its building: a black cube-shaped structure that appears to hover above an open plaza at its base, supported by columns set well back from the edges. The collection inside spans modern and contemporary art, with a particular strength in Japanese and international works from the 20th century onward, displayed across special exhibition galleries as well as rotating selections from the permanent collection.

A visit focused on the permanent collection can be done in an hour or two, while major temporary exhibitions, which often carry their own separate admission on top of general entry, can extend a visit considerably longer depending on how much of the show you want to see. The museum's location on Nakanoshima puts it within easy walking distance of several other cultural institutions, including the Osaka City Museum of Modern Art and the Osaka City Central Public Hall, making it straightforward to combine into a single museum-focused day along the island.

Weekday mornings tend to be the quietest time, particularly outside of headline touring exhibitions, which can draw longer queues on weekends. The building's ground-level plaza and exterior are worth a look even without going inside, and the riverside walk along Nakanoshima nearby makes a pleasant addition before or after a visit.

4-3-1 Nakanoshima, Kita Ward, Osaka
