Isetan Shinjuku Main Store

Tokyo

Isetan traces back to a kimono shop founded in 1886, and its Shinjuku flagship, opened at the current site in 1933, is the company's main store. The building itself is a well-preserved example of Art Deco department store architecture, recognized as a Tokyo Selected Historical Building, distinct from the glass-and-steel towers that dominate most of Shinjuku's skyline today.

Within Japanese fashion circles, Isetan Shinjuku has a reputation as one of the country's trend-setting stores, particularly for womenswear, regularly credited with shaping what becomes popular elsewhere in Tokyo rather than simply following trends. The main building is supplemented by a separate Men's Building and the Isetan Kaikan annex nearby, together spanning nine floors from basement to roof in the main building alone.

The basement food floor draws a dedicated following for its dessert selection in particular, and the rooftop garden offers a family-friendly, unhurried space to rest that's easy to miss if you don't specifically seek it out. Even for visitors not planning to shop, the building's Art Deco details are worth a slow look on the way in or out.
