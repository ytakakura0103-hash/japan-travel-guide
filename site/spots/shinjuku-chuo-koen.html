Shinjuku Central Park

Tokyo

Shinjuku Central Park sits directly in front of the Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building, surrounded by some of the tallest buildings in the Nishi-Shinjuku skyscraper district, including the Hyatt Regency and Park Hyatt Tokyo. Despite that setting, the park itself feels deliberately low-key: open lawns, mature trees, and a small artificial waterfall called Jusho-no-Taki give it a quieter, more residential feel than the office towers around it would suggest.

It works well as a short break between skyscraper-district sightseeing, close enough to the Metropolitan Government Building's free observation decks to combine into the same outing, but with none of the crowds. Office workers use it for lunch breaks on weekdays, and it hosts occasional food events and markets on weekends, so its atmosphere shifts noticeably depending on when you visit.

It isn't a destination park in the way Shinjuku Gyoen or Yoyogi Park are, and most visitors treat it as a convenient stopover rather than a planned outing, but for anyone already in the area for the Metropolitan Government Building or a hotel nearby, it's a pleasant, unhurried green space that most itineraries otherwise skip entirely.

2-11 Nishi-Shinjuku, Shinjuku City, Tokyo 160-0023
