Sagano Scenic Railway

Kyoto

The Sagano Scenic Railway, often called the Torokko train, runs a repurposed section of railway track along the Hozugawa River gorge between Saga (next to Arashiyama) and Kameoka, using open-window carriages that let the sound and air of the valley in as the train moves at a deliberately slow pace. The ride passes cliffs, seasonal foliage, and the same river later used by the Hozugawa boat rides, offering a different, elevated vantage point on the same gorge.

Trains run on a fixed timetable rather than continuously, and seats are reserved, so tickets are worth securing in advance, especially during the autumn foliage season in November and cherry blossom season in spring, both of which sell out quickly and push demand for seats to its peak. Outside of those windows, tickets can often still be bought on the day, though weekends stay busier year-round. One open-air carriage without windows offers the most direct views and air but fills up fastest.

The ride itself is the main activity, and most visitors treat it as one leg of a loop, riding out from Saga to Kameoka and returning to Arashiyama by the Hozugawa river boat or a regular train, rather than riding round-trip on the Torokko alone. The full outing, including transfers, typically takes a few hours.
