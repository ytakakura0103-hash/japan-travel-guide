Takashimaya Yokohama

Yokohama

Takashimaya Yokohama traces back to 1956, taking on its current name in 1959, and played a significant role in transforming Yokohama Station's west side, once considered little more than a back entrance compared to the busier east side, into the major shopping district it is today. Spread across basement two to the eighth floor, it carries international luxury apparel and cosmetics that have made it a multi-generational favorite among local shoppers, alongside the largest food floor in Kanagawa Prefecture.

That food floor is a particular highlight, including a dedicated Sweets Market gathering around twenty different sweets brands and a Bakery Square section, giving it a scale and variety that goes well beyond a typical department store depachika. As one of Takashimaya's most significant stores outside its home base in Kansai and its Tokyo flagship in Nihombashi, it carries real weight within the broader Takashimaya group.

As the anchor tenant of Sotetsu JOINUS, it's directly integrated with the surrounding underground shopping arcade, making it easy to move between department store shopping and JOINUS's broader retail and dining without stepping outside. Its west-exit location puts it within easy reach of Yokohama Station's other shopping complexes on the same side of the station.
