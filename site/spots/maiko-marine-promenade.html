Maiko Marine Promenade

Kobe

Built into the underside structure of the Akashi Kaikyo Bridge, the Maiko Marine Promenade lets visitors walk out over the strait along a deck fixed roughly under the roadway, offering views of the bridge's massive support cables and towers from an angle almost no other vantage point provides. Its best-known feature is a glass floor panel partway along the walkway, where visitors can look straight down through the transparent flooring to the water far below, a genuinely vertigo-inducing moment for anyone not used to heights.

An eighth-floor observation lounge with a cafe rounds out the facility, giving a more relaxed indoor option to take in the same views while seated. Admission is charged, generally at a modest rate that is a bit higher on weekends and holidays than on weekdays, and a full visit, including time on the glass floor and the lounge, takes roughly 30 to 45 minutes.

Weekend and holiday crowds are noticeably heavier here than on weekdays, particularly around the glass floor section where people tend to linger for photos, so a weekday visit or an early arrival makes the walkway considerably less congested.

2051 Higashi Maiko-cho, Tarumi-ku, Kobe

About a 5-minute walk from JR Maiko Station or Sanyo Maiko-koen Station.
