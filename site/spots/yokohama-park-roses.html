Yokohama Park Rose Garden

Yokohama

Before it became a park, this ground served as a racecourse for the foreign settlement in the early Meiji era, and later a public gathering space, before eventually being redeveloped into the leafy park that today wraps around Yokohama Stadium, home field of the DeNA BayStars professional baseball team. The rose beds along the park's perimeter bloom across a long stretch of spring and again in autumn, planted in curated blocks by color and variety rather than as a single dense bed.

On game days the area around the stadium fills with baseball fans in team colors, giving the park a completely different energy than its quiet flower-garden reputation suggests, so timing matters if you are after either a peaceful stroll or a taste of local sports culture. Checking the team's schedule beforehand helps you pick the experience you actually want.

Because it sits directly between Kannai Station and the Bashamichi and Nihon Odori historic district, it functions as much as a walking corridor as a destination, and most visitors pass through without realizing the rose plantings are curated as seriously as they are. Late May tends to be the fullest bloom for the spring rose season.

1 Yokohama Park, Naka-ku, Yokohama
