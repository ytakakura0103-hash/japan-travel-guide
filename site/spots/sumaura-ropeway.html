Sumaura Ropeway

Kobe

Rising from the hills just behind Suma Beach, this ropeway carries visitors up to Sumaura-yama Park in only a few minutes, delivering a view that stretches along the coast toward Akashi Kaikyo Bridge and Awaji Island on a clear day. From the ropeway's upper station, a connecting chairlift continues even higher to an observation platform, and on the way back down there is an option to ride a long slide built into the hillside instead of taking the lift back, a small but popular novelty for kids and adults alike.

Combining the ropeway, chairlift, and slide into a single loop generally takes a bit over an hour, making it an easy half-outing to pair with time at Suma Beach or the aquarium below. The hilltop area also has a small observation deck and modest facilities for a short break.

As a secondary attraction to the beach and aquarium rather than a headline destination, the ropeway tends to see manageable crowds even in summer, though clear-weather weekends bring more visitors up for the view than hazy or overcast days.
