CLUB JOULE

Osaka

CLUB JOULE traces its roots back to 1999, when it opened near Triangle Park in Amerikamura, before relocating to its current Nishi-Shinsaibashi address in 2009 and undergoing a full renovation in 2016. That kind of longevity is unusual in Osaka's club scene, where venues routinely close within a few years, and it's earned JOULE a reputation as one of the city's more serious electronic-music venues rather than a general night-out spot.

The club spans three floors of a dedicated building, giving it more room than most Nishi-Shinsaibashi venues to run different rooms or events simultaneously, and its booking policy covers a broad range of electronic genres rather than sticking to one narrow lane, which keeps its calendar varied from week to week. Nishi-Shinsaibashi itself sits just west of the main Amerikamura shopping district, an easy walk from both Shinsaibashi and Yotsubashi stations.

Because JOULE leans toward genuine electronic music programming, it tends to draw a crowd more interested in the music than in bottle service or a see-and-be-seen scene, worth knowing for visitors deciding between it and the bigger, more mainstream clubs closer to Shinsaibashi Station. As always with Japanese clubs, checking the current event schedule before heading over is worthwhile, since the lineup and door charge vary by night.

Minami-Suminoyacho Building 2F/3F/4F, 2-11-7 Nishi-Shinsaibashi, Chuo Ward, Osaka

Open in Google Maps

About 5 minutes on foot from Yotsubashi Station (Osaka Metro Yotsubashi Line), or 8 minutes from Shinsaibashi Station.
