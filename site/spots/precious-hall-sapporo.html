Precious Hall

Hokkaido

Precious Hall opened in 1993 and stands as Sapporo's oldest operating nightclub, a genuine rarity given how quickly Japanese club venues typically turn over. Its house and techno booking policy has stayed remarkably consistent across more than three decades, building a reputation among electronic music fans well beyond Hokkaido and helping cement Susukino's place in Japan's club scene alongside the country's larger cities.

The club underwent a relocation in 2014, merging with its sister venue Fillmore North, but has otherwise operated continuously from its Susukino basement location, a stability that's rare enough in Japan's club industry to be worth noting in its own right. Longevity like this tends to attract a knowledgeable, dedicated crowd rather than a casual night-out audience, and the booking policy reflects that, favoring serious electronic music programming over mainstream chart hits.

Its basement setting within Susukino puts it in easy reach of the district's other bars and restaurants, making it simple to fold into a broader evening in Sapporo's main entertainment district. Checking the current event calendar before visiting is worthwhile, since Precious Hall's lineup skews toward specific electronic subgenres that vary night to night.
