MARK IS Minatomirai

Yokohama

This mall covers several floors of fashion, lifestyle, and homeware shops along with a substantial food court and restaurant floor, and it is directly connected to Minatomirai Station, making it a convenient rainy-day option in a district otherwise built around outdoor waterfront walking. Its scale and mix of both international and Japanese brands make it a reasonable one-stop option if you are short on shopping time elsewhere in the city.

The rooftop garden on the upper level is the more distinctive feature: a relatively quiet lawn and walking path with views toward the Landmark Tower and Grand Mall Park below, and it costs nothing to visit even if you are not shopping. It is a good spot to rest between museum visits without needing to buy anything.

Weekends and evenings get busy with the restaurant crowd, especially around dinnertime, but the shopping floors themselves are manageable outside of holiday sales periods. Because it sits at one end of Grand Mall Park, it pairs naturally with a walk down toward the Landmark Tower and Yokohama Museum of Art, and it is an easy place to duck into if Minato Mirai's outdoor spaces get too hot or cold for comfortable walking.

3-5-1 Minatomirai, Nishi-ku, Yokohama
