Yamate 234 Bankan

Yokohama

Unlike the grand single-family villas nearby, Yamate 234 Bankan was originally built as multi-unit rental housing for foreign residents, a reminder that not every Westerner in prewar Yokohama lived in a mansion. Four separate households once shared this Spanish-tile-roofed building, and the layout, smaller rooms, shared stairwells, feels distinctly more domestic and less showcase-like than Berrick Hall or the Ehrismann Residence.\n\nToday the ground floor hosts small community exhibitions, sometimes local art or historical photo displays that rotate periodically, while the general structure and some furnished rooms give a sense of everyday foreign-resident life in interwar Yokohama rather than diplomatic grandeur. Admission is free.\n\nIt sits at the edge of the main Yamate heritage-house cluster, which means it gets a fraction of the foot traffic that Harbor View Park and Berrick Hall attract, even though it is only a short walk from both. If your interest runs more toward social history than architectural showpieces, this is the house to prioritize, and you will likely have entire rooms to yourself on a weekday visit.

234 Yamate-cho, Naka-ku, Yokohama

About 7 minutes on foot from Minatomirai Line Motomachi-Chukagai Station (exit 6).
