Local Pick

Kanawa Floating Oyster Restaurant

Hiroshima

Hiroshima Prefecture is one of Japan's largest producers of farmed oysters, and the calm, nutrient-rich waters of the Seto Inland Sea around Hiroshima Bay have supported oyster cultivation for centuries. Kanawa takes that local specialty and serves it aboard a converted boat permanently moored on the Motoyasu River, within easy walking distance of Peace Memorial Park, giving diners a dedicated setting built entirely around the ingredient.

The restaurant offers set courses that typically move through several preparations of oyster in one sitting, such as raw, grilled, fried, and simmered in a hot pot, letting first-time visitors sample the range of textures and flavors a single ingredient can take on rather than committing to just one style. Seasonal availability affects the menu, since Japanese oysters are generally at their best from autumn through early spring.

Because it operates from a boat with limited seating, reservations are strongly recommended, particularly for dinner or during peak oyster season, and the combination of a distinctive setting with a genuinely well-regarded kitchen has made it a long-standing favorite among Hiroshima diners rather than a purely tourist-oriented novelty.

Ote-machi, Naka Ward, Hiroshima, Hiroshima Prefecture

About 25-30 minutes on foot from Hiroshima Station (a tram is recommended), or 5-minute walk from Genbaku Dome-mae tram stop, about 15 minutes by tram from Hiroshima Station; moored on the Motoyasu River at the foot of Motoyasu-bashi bridge, near Peace Memorial Park.
