Sendai Mitsukoshi

Sendai

Sendai Mitsukoshi represents the Tohoku outpost of Mitsukoshi Isetan, one of Japan's most prestigious national department store groups, giving it a different character from Fujisaki, its homegrown neighbor a short walk away. Where Fujisaki draws on more than two centuries of specifically Sendai-rooted history, Sendai Mitsukoshi carries the national Mitsukoshi brand's reputation for luxury goods and internationally recognized fashion labels, a useful contrast for visitors deciding which store better suits what they're shopping for.

The store operates across two connected buildings, the Main Building and the Jozenji-dori Building, giving it a footprint that spreads along the tree-lined Jozenji-dori corridor rather than concentrating in a single tower. Standard department store amenities, including a basement food hall and multiple fashion and cosmetics floors, round out the offering.

Sitting directly beside Fujisaki, it's easy to visit both stores in a single outing and compare Sendai's two major department stores directly against each other. Its Jozenji-dori-facing side also puts it close to one of Sendai's most scenic tree-lined boulevards, worth a walk regardless of shopping plans.
