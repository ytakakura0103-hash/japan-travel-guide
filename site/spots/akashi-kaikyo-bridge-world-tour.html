Bridge World Tour, Akashi Kaikyo Bridge

Kobe

This guided program takes small groups through maintenance walkways inside the Akashi Kaikyo Bridge's girder, eventually reaching an open platform near the top of one of the main towers, roughly 300 meters above the strait, for a 360-degree view over Kobe, Awaji Island and the shipping lanes below. It's operated by the bridge's management organization rather than a general tour company, and involves a genuine engineering-focused briefing before the walk begins.

Because it requires advance reservation, a language capability check for non-Japanese speakers in some cases, and a fixed group schedule, it draws far fewer participants than the freely accessible viewpoints nearby, and tends to attract visitors specifically interested in engineering or a genuine adrenaline experience rather than casual sightseers.

Tours typically run only in warmer months due to the exposed, high-elevation walkway, and are weather-dependent, cancelled in high winds or rain, so building flexibility into your schedule is worth it if this is a priority stop. Comfortable closed-toe shoes are required, and personal items like phones or cameras may be restricted or secured during the walkway sections for safety.

Maiko, Tarumi Ward, Kobe

Open in Google Maps

Tours depart from the Maiko side of the bridge, reachable from Sannomiya via the JR Kobe Line or Sanyo Dentetsu Line to Maiko Station (about 25-30 minutes).
