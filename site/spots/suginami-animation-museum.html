Suginami Animation Museum

Tokyo

The Suginami Animation Museum is a small, free museum dedicated to the history and craft of Japanese animation, fittingly located in Suginami, a residential ward long associated with animation studios going back to some of the industry's earliest postwar productions. Exhibits trace the technical evolution of anime from hand-drawn cel animation through to modern digital production, and the museum includes hands-on stations where visitors can flip through a simple animation flipbook, try recording dialogue in a voice-acting booth, or experiment with basic animation techniques rather than only reading wall text.

Because the museum is compact, most visits take about an hour, making it an easy addition to a day exploring Suginami's residential streets rather than a destination requiring significant time on its own. Admission is free, funded by the ward rather than ticket sales, which makes it a good low-commitment stop for anyone with a general interest in anime who doesn't want to invest a full afternoon or the entry fee of a larger attraction.

The museum tends to be genuinely quiet on weekdays, with occasional school groups the main source of crowding; weekends bring a modest increase in visitors but rarely anything close to a wait. Its location a short walk from Ogikubo Station makes it a low-effort stop for travelers interested in anime history away from the more commercial, merchandise-focused options in Akihabara.
