Nanzoin Temple's Reclining Buddha

Fukuoka

Nanzoin is a Shingon-sect temple set into wooded hills in Sasaguri, a short train ride from central Fukuoka, and its main draw is impossible to miss once you round the final bend in the path: a reclining bronze statue of the Buddha at the moment of entering nirvana, reportedly among the largest bronze statues of its kind anywhere. The scale only becomes clear up close, when a single foot towers well above head height.

The temple grounds slope steeply through forest, with smaller shrines, statues, and a columbarium tucked along the paths leading up to the main statue, so the walk itself is part of the experience rather than a formality before the highlight. Incense smoke and quiet chanting from worship halls add to an atmosphere that feels distinctly different from the more commercial temples closer to the city.

Despite the scale of the statue, Nanzoin remains relatively under-visited by international tourists compared to Dazaifu, in part because it requires a dedicated train trip rather than sitting on a well-worn tourist loop. Weekday visits are notably calm; weekends can bring more visitors, particularly around Japanese holidays. Comfortable shoes are worth it given the sloped, sometimes uneven paths.

1035 Sasaguri, Sasaguri-machi, Kasuya-gun, Fukuoka

Open in Google Maps

About 20-25 minutes by JR Fukuhoku Yutaka (Sasaguri) Line rapid train from Hakata Station to Kido Nanzoinmae Station, then about a 3-minute walk to the temple.
