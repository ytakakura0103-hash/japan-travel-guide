COCONO SUSUKINO

Hokkaido

COCONO SUSUKINO occupies the site of the former Robinson's Sapporo department store, redeveloped into a mixed shopping and dining complex that anchors the northern edge of Susukino, Sapporo's main entertainment district. Its tenant mix spans fashion retail and a range of restaurants, giving it a dual identity as both a daytime shopping stop and an evening dining destination for visitors already in the area for Susukino's nightlife.

Its direct connection to Susukino Station makes it one of the most accessible shopping stops in the district, useful for anyone navigating Sapporo's extensive underground and station-connected retail network without needing to surface at street level. That convenience has helped it become a natural meeting point and starting stop for evenings that continue on into Susukino's bars and clubs nearby.

Being built specifically for the Susukino context rather than transplanted from elsewhere, its restaurant selection leans into dishes and formats popular with the district's evening crowd, worth checking directly for current tenant offerings given how retail and dining lineups in redeveloped complexes tend to shift in their first few years.

Susukino, Chuo Ward, Sapporo
