Sakurai Shrine

Fukuoka

According to shrine tradition, a violent thunderstorm in 1610 split open a rock face on this hillside, revealing a natural cave from which the shrine's origins are said to trace; the local feudal lord, moved by reports of the event, had the main shrine hall built in 1632. Today the shrine complex includes the main hall partway up a wooded approach and a smaller inner shrine, Amanoiwato, near the cave itself, reached by a further short climb.

Sakurai Shrine has become known specifically as a matchmaking and relationship shrine, drawing visitors hoping for good fortune in love, and it enshrines guardian spirits linked to both the inner and outer shrines of Ise. Once a year, in early July, a predawn ritual opens the inner cave shrine to visitors, drawing a notable crowd that contrasts with the shrine's otherwise quiet daily rhythm.

Outside that one ritual date, the wooded grounds and stone steps are peaceful enough for an unhurried visit, and the shrine sits close enough to Sakurai Futamigaura to combine both in one coastal outing. The forest setting keeps the shrine noticeably cooler in summer than the open coastal road nearby.

3777 Sakurai, Itoshima, Fukuoka

By car or taxi from JR Chikuzen-Maebaru Station, about 15-20 minutes' drive, close to Sakurai Futamigaura.
