Onomichi Ropeway

Hiroshima

The ropeway climbs the hill behind central Onomichi from a lower station near Nagaeguchi, just a few minutes from JR Onomichi Station, up to Senkoji Park at the summit in a ride of only a few minutes. It has operated in some form since the 1950s, built specifically to give easier access to Senkoji Temple and the panoramic viewpoint above it without requiring visitors to climb the hillside's many stone stairways on foot.

From the upper station, a short walk leads to the main observation deck at Senkoji Park, which looks out over Onomichi's dense, tightly packed rooftops, the narrow strait separating Honshu from Mukaishima, and the wider Seto Inland Sea dotted with small islands and passing ferries. The view is considered one of the better easily accessible cityscape-and-sea views in the Setouchi region, and it is popular at both midday and sunset.

Many visitors ride the ropeway up and then walk back down through the temple district on foot, following the Onomichi Temple Walk route past Senkoji and several other temples, alleys, and the Path of Literature lined with stone monuments inscribed with lines from writers connected to the city.

20-1 Higashitsuchido-cho, Onomichi, Hiroshima Prefecture
