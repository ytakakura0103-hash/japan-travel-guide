Mukaishima Shipyard Skyline and Backstreets

Hiroshima

Mukaishima sits directly across the narrow Onomichi Channel from the mainland, close enough that a small ferry crossing takes only a few minutes, and its skyline of shipyard cranes has become part of Onomichi's own visual identity — visible in the background of countless photos taken from the hillside temples on the other side. Rather than viewing it only from Onomichi, crossing over lets you walk the residential backstreets that run alongside the working docks, where the scale of the ships under construction is much more apparent up close.

At night, several of the crane structures are lit in slowly changing colors, a low-key light display that's easy to miss unless you know to look for it, best seen either from the Mukaishima side itself or from the Onomichi waterfront after dark.

This isn't a polished sightseeing route — it's a genuine industrial neighborhood — but that's exactly its appeal for visitors who've already covered Onomichi's temple hill and want a sense of the town's other economic backbone.
