Nokonoshima Island Park

Fukuoka

Nokonoshima sits in Hakata Bay a short ferry crossing from the mainland, and its island park spans a large, gently sloped area planted with seasonal flower fields that change dramatically through the year, yellow rapeseed and cherry blossoms in spring, sunflowers and scarlet sage in summer, tens of thousands of cosmos in autumn, and daffodils through winter. The combination of flowers, sea views, and rolling hills makes it a popular half-day escape from the city.

Beyond the flower fields, the park has cafes, souvenir shops, and simple outdoor activities suited to families, and overnight cottages with ocean views are available for anyone wanting to extend the visit. The island itself is small enough to explore comfortably in an afternoon.

Ferries run frequently from Meinohama Ferry Terminal and the crossing itself takes only about ten minutes, so the main time investment is the bus or subway ride out to the terminal rather than the boat trip. Weekends during peak flower seasons, especially the autumn cosmos field, draw the largest crowds; a weekday visit or an early ferry gives noticeably more breathing room among the flowers.

1233 Nokonoshima, Nishi Ward, Fukuoka
