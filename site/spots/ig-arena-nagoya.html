IG Arena

Nagoya

IG Arena opened in July 2025 as Aichi Prefecture's new flagship arena, built beside Meijo Park with a capacity of roughly 17,000, placing it among the largest multi-purpose venues in Japan. Its most distinctive feature is a roof built from an intricate wooden lattice structure, nicknamed a "tree-form arch," designed by architect Kengo Kuma, whose wood-forward style has become internationally recognizable through projects like the Japan National Stadium. The result is a building that reads as much as a piece of architecture as a sports and concert venue.

Beyond hosting concerts and sporting events, the arena has taken on a notable new role as a sumo venue, hosting the Nagoya Grand Sumo Tournament from 2026 onward, giving it a place in Japan's traditional sporting calendar alongside its modern architectural profile. That combination, cutting-edge design paired with one of Japan's oldest sporting traditions, has helped drive its growing visibility on social media since opening.

Its location beside Meijo Park and within reach of Nagoya Castle makes it easy to combine with a visit to either, particularly for anyone specifically drawn by the building's architecture rather than an event happening inside it. Checking the current events calendar is worthwhile if the goal is to see a show or match rather than just view the exterior.
