NEWoMan Shinjuku

Tokyo

NEWoMan sits in the newer south side of Shinjuku Station, built as part of the Miraina Tower development and positioned deliberately differently from the station's other big retail draws: rather than youth fashion or electronics, it's curated around lifestyle, beauty, and design brands aimed at working adults, with a noticeably calmer, more polished atmosphere than the crowds around the east side exits.

The roughly 100 stores mix Japanese and international brands across fashion, homeware, and beauty, but the food and cafe floors are what draw the most consistent foot traffic, including a well-regarded selection of bakeries and dessert counters that regularly appear on Tokyo food-recommendation lists independent of the mall itself. It's a practical stop for a coffee or light meal while catching a train, given its direct station access, rather than requiring a special trip.

Because it connects straight into both JR Shinjuku Station and Shinjuku-sanchome Station underground, NEWoMan works well as a weatherproof shortcut between the two areas as much as a shopping destination, useful to know about on a rainy day or when connecting to Shinjuku Sanchome or Ni-chome without wanting to walk the street-level route.

4-1-6 Shinjuku, Shinjuku City, Tokyo 160-0022

Open in Google Maps

About 5 minutes on foot from Shinjuku Station (via the Miraina Tower, Koshu Kaido, or Shinminami gates); about 3 minutes from Shinjuku-sanchome Station, with a direct underground connection to the B1 level.
