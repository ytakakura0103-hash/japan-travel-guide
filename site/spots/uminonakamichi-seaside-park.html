Uminonakamichi Seaside Park

Fukuoka

Uminonakamichi occupies a long, narrow peninsula across Hakata Bay from central Fukuoka, and its scale sets it apart from the city's smaller downtown parks: flower gardens, a water park, a small zoo, sports fields, and long stretches of open lawn are all spread across the site, with cycling trails connecting the different areas. Roughly 2,000 cherry trees line the lawns and paths, forming blossom tunnels that make it one of the city's better-regarded hanami spots in late March and early April.

Seasonal events run through the year, including spring flower festivals, rose displays in early summer and autumn, and a cosmos festival in autumn, so the specific flowers on show change significantly depending on when you visit. Renting a bicycle is a popular way to cover the park's considerable size rather than walking the whole thing.

Marine World Uminonakamichi, a separate aquarium, sits just across the train tracks from the park and requires its own ticket, worth factoring into planning if visiting both in one day. Weekends and cherry blossom season bring the heaviest crowds; weekday visits outside peak flower periods are considerably calmer.

18-25 Saitozaki, Higashi Ward, Fukuoka
