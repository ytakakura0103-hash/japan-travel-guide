Osaka Castle Nishinomaru Garden

Osaka

This lawned garden occupies part of the western bailey of Osaka Castle's grounds, offering one of the clearest unobstructed views of the main keep from ground level, framed by roughly 300 cherry trees planted across the open grass, a combination that makes it one of the most photographed cherry blossom scenes in the Kansai region each spring. Outside the blossom season, the garden remains a pleasant, quieter space than the more heavily trafficked areas immediately around the keep, with open lawns suitable for sitting and a former Western-style guest house near the entrance adding a small architectural point of interest.

A visit takes thirty minutes to an hour depending on whether you simply walk through or settle in on the grass. Admission to Nishinomaru Garden is charged separately from the castle keep itself, a modest fee that helps keep the space less crowded than the free areas of the wider park.

During peak cherry blossom season, typically late March into early April, the garden becomes one of the busiest spots in the city, and a picnic spot with a keep view can require arriving early in the day to secure; outside that narrow window, crowds thin considerably and a visit can be enjoyed at a relaxed pace at almost any time. It sits within Osaka Castle Park, a short walk from Osakajokoen Station, and combines naturally with a visit to the castle keep itself.

2 Osakajo, Chuo Ward, Osaka
