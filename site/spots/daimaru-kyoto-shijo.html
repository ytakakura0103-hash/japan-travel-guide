Daimaru Kyoto

Kyoto

Daimaru's history in Kyoto stretches back to a kimono shop called Daimonjiya, founded in Fushimi in 1717, though the store's current Shijo location dates to 1912, when it opened in a building with Indo-Saracenic styling. That original building burned down in 1921 and was rebuilt in reinforced concrete, expanding to six floors across east and west wings by 1928, and a 2014 renovation gave the exterior its current three-layer facade combining marble, brick tile, and plaster.

Locals affectionately call it "Daimaru-san," a nickname that reflects its standing as a community-rooted institution rather than a purely aspirational luxury destination, even as it carries a full range of luxury brands, cosmetics, jewelry, and kids' and sportswear across floors B2 to 8F. In September 2023, the store reopened a full restaurant floor for the first time in roughly fifty years, restoring a dining dimension that had been absent from the building for decades.

Its location at Shijo-Karasuma puts it near Kyoto's business district rather than directly in the tourist core, giving it a slightly different customer mix than Takashimaya a short walk east. It's a reasonable stop to combine with a walk down Shijo-dori toward Kawaramachi, taking in both of Kyoto's major department stores along the way.
