Sapporo Art Park

Sapporo

Set into wooded hills on Sapporo's southern edge, Sapporo Art Park spreads dozens of outdoor sculptures, many by Japanese and international artists, across walking trails that wind through genuine forest rather than a manicured lawn. An indoor art museum handles rotating exhibitions, while a separate craft studio building lets visitors watch, and sometimes try, glassblowing and pottery.\n\nThe combination of art and hiking-adjacent scenery makes it a distinctive stop for visitors who want something beyond a standard museum visit, and its distance from downtown, roughly a bus ride from the nearest subway station, keeps it well off the standard first-time itinerary, so crowds are rarely an issue even on a nice day.\n\nAutumn foliage through the sculpture trails in October is a strong reason to time a visit then, while summer offers the most comfortable walking conditions given how exposed some of the outdoor sections are. Because the outdoor sculpture areas can close or become harder to navigate under heavy snow, winter visits are better suited to the indoor museum and craft studio rather than the full trail experience. Comfortable shoes are worth it regardless of season, given how spread out the grounds are.

3 Chome, Geijutsu-no-Mori, Minami-ku, Sapporo, Hokkaido

Open in Google Maps

Bus from Makomanai subway station (Namboku Line), about 15 minutes.
