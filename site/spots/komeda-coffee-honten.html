Komeda Coffee Original Store

Nagoya

Komeda Coffee started in Nagoya and grew into a nationwide chain, but its distinctive habit of throwing in a free thick-cut toast and a boiled or savory egg with any morning coffee order traces directly back to a local custom known as 'morning service,' a Nagoya-area tradition that turned ordinary kissaten coffee shops into unofficial breakfast spots decades ago. Visiting the brand's original store offers a chance to experience that custom at its source, in an interior styled with the same red vinyl booths and wood paneling found across the chain today.

A coffee with the morning set costs about the same as a regular cup elsewhere, making it one of the better value breakfasts in the city, and the service generally runs only during set morning hours before switching to a standard menu.

Weekday mornings draw a loyal crowd of regulars reading newspapers rather than tourists, and arriving right at opening secures a booth before the modest morning rush; the shop empties out again by mid-morning once the free-toast window closes.
