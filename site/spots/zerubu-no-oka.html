Zerubu-no-Oka

Hokkaido

Zerubu-no-Oka is a modest hillside flower garden in Biei operated by Rokkatei, the Hokkaido confectionery brand, and it trades scale for calm compared to Shikisai-no-Oka or Farm Tomita. The garden covers rows of seasonal flowers, sunflowers and various annuals depending on the month, arranged so that the classic Biei patchwork-hill backdrop is visible from several points on the grounds.

Because Rokkatei runs the site, there's a cafe serving the company's confections alongside soft-serve and coffee, a reasonable rest stop given how much walking a typical Biei day involves. The garden is small enough to see thoroughly in 30-45 minutes, which makes it easy to fit in alongside a couple of Biei's other hill viewpoints in a single day.

It's within a roughly 30-minute walk of Biei Station, or a short taxi ride, which puts it in reach for visitors without a rental car, unlike some of Biei's more scattered farm viewpoints that effectively require driving.

Summer brings the fullest bloom, but the hill views themselves, the patchwork fields and distant Tokachi mountains, are worth seeing across seasons, including a snow-covered version in winter when the garden itself is dormant.

Biei-cho, Kamikawa-gun, Hokkaido 071-0200
