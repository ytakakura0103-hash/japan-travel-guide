teamLab: Field of Wind, Rain and Sun

Osaka

Opened in October 2024 inside a repurposed can-manufacturing factory in Higashi-Osaka, this installation takes a different approach from teamLab's better-known Osaka venue in Nagai Park's botanical garden: rather than working with an existing natural setting, it builds an entirely artificial environment of wind, rain, and shifting light inside the industrial shell of the old factory, using the building's raw, unfinished character as a backdrop for the digital projections rather than disguising it.

The site includes an attached cafe styled as a meadow, split between a daytime session and a separately ticketed evening session under illuminated lighting, giving visitors a reason to treat the stop as a full outing rather than a quick installation walkthrough. Its location outside the central tourist core, in an industrial pocket of Higashi-Osaka, means it draws a smaller, more deliberate crowd than teamLab's more famous venues elsewhere in Japan, and getting there generally takes more planning than a stop in central Osaka.

Because the venue repurposes a working factory building rather than a custom-built gallery, the visitor experience leans more raw and atmospheric than polished, which is part of its appeal for anyone who has already seen teamLab's more conventional exhibitions. Checking current session times and transit access on the official site before setting out is worthwhile given the location.
