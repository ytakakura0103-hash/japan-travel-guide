Nanzoin Temple

Fukuoka

The reclining Buddha at Nanzoin is enormous by any measure, reportedly around 41 meters long and 11 meters tall, cast in bronze and set into a landscaped valley just outside the temple's main buildings. It's often described as the largest bronze reclining Buddha statue in the world, and the scale is genuinely hard to appreciate from photos alone.

Beyond the statue itself, the temple grounds include a Shichifukujin tunnel dedicated to the Seven Gods of Good Fortune and a small tea shop for a break partway through exploring. Entry to the temple grounds is free, though there's a small fee to enter the prayer room built beneath the statue.

Because it sits outside central Fukuoka in the town of Sasaguri, reached by a slower suburban train line, it draws a noticeably different, often more local crowd than the downtown shrines, and rarely feels overrun even on weekends. The temple opens at 9am and closes in the late afternoon, so a morning departure from Hakata Station leaves comfortable time to explore without rushing back for the return train.

Sasaguri, Kasuya District, Fukuoka Prefecture

Open in Google Maps

About 26 minutes on the JR Sasaguri Line from Hakata Station to Kido Nanzoin-mae, then a 5-minute walk
