ekie

Hiroshima

ekie opened in phases between 2017 and 2019 on the Shinkansen side of JR Hiroshima Station, building out a roughly 130-store shopping and dining complex directly integrated into the station itself. Its tenant lineup leans heavily into Hiroshima's distinctive food culture, with a strong concentration of restaurants and shops specializing in okonomiyaki, oysters, momiji manju, and lemon-based sweets, giving travelers passing through the station a genuine taste of the region rather than generic transit food.

That focus makes it one of the most convenient places in the city for last-minute souvenir shopping or a final regional meal before catching a train out of Hiroshima, without needing to venture into the downtown shopping districts. Its direct Shinkansen-side location means it's essentially unavoidable for anyone arriving or departing by bullet train, making it worth planning around rather than rushing through.

Given how central it is to a Hiroshima visit's beginning or end, checking ekie's current tenant lineup before a planned souvenir shopping trip is worthwhile, since specific shops can rotate, though the overall focus on Hiroshima specialty foods has remained consistent since opening.
