Furano Cheese Factory

Sapporo

Furano Cheese Factory processes local milk into a range of cheeses on-site, and observation windows let visitors watch parts of the production process without needing to book a formal tour. It's a low-key, mostly self-guided stop: wander the production viewing area, browse the shop for cheese and dairy products, then head next door for food.

An adjoining building, sometimes called the Pikanpakan workshop, runs hands-on sessions for making butter, ice cream, or pizza using the factory's own dairy, which makes this a more interactive stop than most single-product food factories and a reasonable activity for families traveling with kids. The complex sits on a low hill outside central Furano, giving a view over surrounding farmland while you eat.

It draws a fairly steady stream of visitors given its family-friendly reputation, but the site is spread out enough, and far enough from the station, that it rarely feels as packed as more centrally located Furano stops.

Summer is the liveliest season given the ice-cream and outdoor seating appeal, but the shop and viewing windows operate through the colder months too, with the farmland backdrop simply swapping green for snow.

Nashigoryo, Furano, Hokkaido 076-0034

About 2.5 hours from Sapporo to JR Furano Station, then about a 10-minute taxi ride, roughly a 15-minute walk, or seasonal shuttle bus to the factory.
