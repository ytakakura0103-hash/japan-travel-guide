Gekko NAKASU

Fukuoka

Gekko NAKASU brings a genuine dance-club presence to Nakasu, a district otherwise better known for its dense concentration of yatai food stalls and hostess bars than for nightclubs in the conventional sense. Its hip-hop-focused booking policy and notable LED lighting installation, covered in Japanese trade press when the club opened, give it a distinct visual identity among Nakasu's more traditional nightlife venues.

Running from 9pm to 5am on weekends, it's built for a genuine late-night out rather than an early-evening stop, positioning it alongside Tenjin's larger clubs as an option for visitors who want to dance rather than eat their way through Nakasu's yatai row. Its presence in Nakasu specifically, rather than the more club-dense Tenjin area, makes it a convenient option for anyone already spending an evening among the yatai stalls who wants to extend the night into something more music-focused.

Given that it's a newer addition to Nakasu's nightlife scene, checking current hours and event programming before visiting is a reasonable precaution, as with most of Fukuoka's smaller, independent clubs.

Nakasu, Hakata Ward, Fukuoka
