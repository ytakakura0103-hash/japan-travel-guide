MIZUHO PayPay Dome

Fukuoka

This all-weather dome is home to the SoftBank Hawks, one of Japan's most successful professional baseball franchises, and its retractable roof was reportedly the first of its kind built in the country. On game days it's a lively, high-energy venue with the kind of coordinated cheering and chanting Japanese baseball crowds are known for, quite different from the more subdued atmosphere at many Western stadiums.

When there isn't a game scheduled, guided tours are available that take visitors through areas normally off-limits, including locker rooms, dugouts, and sometimes close-up views of the roof mechanism itself, giving a sense of the stadium even outside the baseball season or on off days. Tour offerings can vary, so checking availability in advance is worth doing if a tour is the main draw.

Game-day tickets are relatively easy for visitors to buy compared with some other Japanese sports, and even without deep baseball knowledge, the atmosphere alone can be worth attending for. The surrounding complex includes restaurants, shops, and a hotel connected directly to the dome.

2-2-2 Jigyohama, Chuo Ward, Fukuoka
