Mount Kurikoma

Sendai

Mount Kurikoma rises at the meeting point of Miyagi, Iwate, and Akita prefectures, protected as a quasi-national park and known nationally for autumn foliage that some rankings place among the very best in the country, a mix of vivid reds, oranges, and yellows sweeping down the mountainside in waves as the season progresses. Hiking trails of varying difficulty lead toward the summit, with a few hot spring facilities, including Sukawa Onsen, near the trailheads for a post-hike soak.

Trailhead facilities generally charge a small parking or usage fee, and a full hiking day trip requires most of a day given the distance from Sendai and the time needed on the mountain itself. Public transport options are limited, so a car is the most practical way to visit.

Because it takes noticeably longer to reach than Zao, Kurikoma sees a fraction of the tourist traffic despite arguably rivaling it for foliage quality, so a weekday visit during peak color in late September to early October avoids most of what crowding does occur. Higher trails close with the first heavy snowfall, so autumn and summer are the realistic hiking windows.
