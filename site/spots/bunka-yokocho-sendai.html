Local Pick

Bunka Yokocho

Sendai

Bunka Yokocho, nicknamed "Bunyoko" by regulars, takes its name from the Bunka Kinema, a Taisho-era movie theater that once stood on the site, and the alley has held onto a distinctly retro, Showa-era atmosphere ever since, a contrast to the flashier bars and clubs elsewhere in Kokubuncho. Around fifty long-established eateries line the narrow lane, many built around simple counter seating that puts solo diners in easy conversation range of the owner and other regulars.

That counter-seating culture is central to the alley's appeal: it's one of the more welcoming spots in Kokubuncho for a solo visitor, since ordering a drink and a small plate at a counter carries none of the awkwardness that a table-service restaurant built for groups might. A recently completed addition to the alley, a small redeveloped building called Bunyoko ROAST housing a butcher shop and several new tenants, has brought a contemporary edge to the otherwise nostalgic streetscape.

Combined with neighboring Ichibansan Yokocho, Bunka Yokocho gives visitors a genuine sense of Kokubuncho's older, more lived-in character, distinct from the newer nightclubs and high-turnover bars closer to the district's main streets. Early evening is generally the best time to see the alley at its most atmospheric, before the later crowds arrive.

Kokubuncho, Aoba Ward, Sendai

Open in Google Maps

Within Kokubuncho, a short walk from Sendai Station or Kotodai-koen Station (Sendai Subway Namboku Line).
