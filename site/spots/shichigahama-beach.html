Shichigahama Beach

Sendai

Shichigahama is a small coastal town just northeast of Sendai made up of a string of coves and beaches, popular with the city's small but dedicated surfing community as well as families looking for an easy seaside outing without traveling all the way to Matsushima or beyond. A hill called Hiyoriyama offers a modest lookout point over the coastline nearby.

Beach access is free, and surfboard or wetsuit rental is available through local shops for those who want to try the water. It's an easy trip by train or bus from central Sendai, making it feasible even as a half-day outing.

Being more of a local weekend spot than a tourist destination, it stays relaxed even in summer compared to more famous beaches further afield. Surfing continues through much of the year for the dedicated, but swimming is really a summer-only activity, and sunset views over the water are a quiet highlight any time of year.
