Fukuoka Asian Art Museum

Fukuoka

Set on the seventh and eighth floors of the Hakata Riverain building, this museum built its collection specifically around modern and contemporary art from across Asia, a focus that sets it apart from Japan's more Japan- or Western-centric art institutions. Rotating exhibitions sit alongside a smaller permanent collection, and workshop spaces sometimes let visitors try simple art activities related to the current show.

Because it sits directly above a subway station rather than in a standalone gallery district, it's an easy add-on to a Nakasu or Kawabata Arcade visit rather than a dedicated trip. The views from the upper-floor windows over the Naka River and Nakasu skyline are a small bonus even for visitors who aren't especially drawn to the art itself.

It draws far fewer visitors than Fukuoka's shrines and food streets, so it's a reliably calm stop even on a busy Saturday. Note that opening hours and closure days shift periodically for maintenance and exhibition changeovers, so it's worth a quick check before visiting if the museum is central to your plans.

3-1 Shimokawabatamachi, Hakata Ward, Fukuoka (Hakata Riverain, 7th-8th floors)

Open in Google Maps

1-minute walk from Nakasu-Kawabata Station, Exit 6, via the elevator inside Hakata Riverain
