Urato Islands

Sendai

Departing from the Marine Gate ferry terminal in Shiogama, a municipal ferry loops daily around the Urato Islands, a scattering of small, mostly car-free islands at the mouth of Matsushima Bay. Katsurashima has a small public beach that opens for swimming during a brief calm stretch each summer, Nonoshima is known for a camellia tunnel and seasonal lavender fields, and Sabusawa, the largest of the group, holds historical sites including unusual rope-tied jizo statues.\n\nA one-way ferry ticket costs a modest, fixed amount depending on destination, and because the ferry runs on a set loop schedule, it's worth checking timetables in advance so you're not stranded on an island longer than planned.\n\nThe islands function primarily as a commuter ferry route for the small resident population, which means visitors are a genuine rarity outside of hiking groups following the Michinoku Coastal Trail. Spring brings flowering nanohana and camellias, while the brief July swimming window at Katsurashima is a distinctly local secret.
