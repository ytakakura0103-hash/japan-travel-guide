Ningle Terrace

Sapporo

Ningle Terrace is a small collection of log-cabin workshops connected by a raised wooden boardwalk through a patch of forest near the Shin-Furano Prince Hotel. Each cabin houses an independent craftsperson selling handmade goods, glasswork, woodcraft, leather, and similar items, made largely on-site rather than imported or mass-produced, which gives it a different feel from a typical souvenir strip.

The name and concept reference the Ningle, small forest-dwelling characters from novelist Jun Ishikawa's fiction that are closely tied to Furano's regional identity, and the wooded, lantern-lit setting is clearly designed to evoke that atmosphere rather than just house shops. It's most effective in the evening, when the path lighting comes on and the surrounding forest fades into shadow.

Because it's attached to a hotel resort rather than sitting in central Furano, and because hours run into the evening (typically until around 8:45pm, with earlier opening in July and August), it stays relatively uncrowded outside peak summer holiday periods, especially on weeknights.

Summer evenings are the classic visit, warm enough to walk the boardwalk comfortably, though the cabins operate across other seasons too, and a snow-covered version of the same lantern-lit path has its own quieter appeal in winter.

Shin Furano Prince Hotel grounds, Nakagoryo, Furano, Hokkaido 076-0034

About 2.5 hours from Sapporo: JR Limited Express Furano Biei Norokko or a transfer via Takikawa/Furano Line to JR Furano Station (roughly 2-2.5 hours total), then about a 10-minute taxi or hotel shuttle to the Shin-Furano Prince Hotel area.
