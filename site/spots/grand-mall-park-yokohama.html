Grand Mall Park

Yokohama

This park is really a long green spine rather than a single destination, running from near Minatomirai Station toward the Landmark Tower and connecting several of the district's malls and public buildings along the way. Fountains, benches, and rotating sculpture installations line the route, and because it was built as the connective tissue of Minato Mirai's master plan, it tends to be far less crowded than the attractions on either end of it.

It is at its best in spring and autumn, when seasonal plantings are in bloom or turning color along the walkway, and in the evening when the surrounding office and hotel towers light up above the greenery. Because much of the park is shaded and open, it is also a comfortable place to rest during Yokohama's humid summer afternoons between museum visits.

Most visitors pass through it without stopping, moving between MARK IS, the Landmark Tower, and the Museum of Art, but treating it as a short standalone walk rather than just a corridor reveals small details, benches, and quiet corners that are easy to miss when rushing between bigger sights.

Minatomirai, Nishi-ku, Yokohama
