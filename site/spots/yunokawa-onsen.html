Yunokawa Onsen

Hokkaido

Yunokawa's hot spring was discovered in the 17th century and grew into one of Hokkaido's older established onsen towns, distinguished today mainly by its location directly on Hakodate Bay, which gives many of its hotel baths sea views that inland onsen towns can't match. It sits close enough to Hakodate Airport that some visitors treat it as a convenient last stop before an evening flight, or a first stop after landing.

Several free or low-cost public foot baths are scattered around the town center, offering a taste of the hot spring experience without committing to a full hotel day-use bath, which makes it accessible even for visitors with limited time between flights or trains. A small aquarium and a modest amusement facility nearby add a family-friendly option beyond the baths themselves.

Because it functions as a genuine residential onsen town rather than a purely tourist destination, it rarely feels crowded even during Hakodate's busier seasons; weekday visits in particular are quiet. Winter brings the appeal of watching snow fall over the bay from a hot outdoor bath, a fairly classic Hokkaido onsen experience, while summer evenings offer a milder version of the same sea view.
