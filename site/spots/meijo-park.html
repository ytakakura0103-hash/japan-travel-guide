Meijo Park

Nagoya

Wrapping around the outer moats of Nagoya Castle, Meijo Park is really a working city park rather than a castle-viewing garden: sports grounds, wide lawns, and a loop path draw joggers, dog walkers, and families well beyond the castle's opening hours. It offers a different, more relaxed angle on the castle than paying to enter the grounds does, since parts of the outer moat and walls are visible for free from the park paths.

There's no cost to enter the park itself, and a loop around the moat takes roughly forty minutes to an hour depending on pace.

Early morning brings out the local exercise crowd rather than tourists, and this is genuinely the best time to see the moat's cherry blossoms in spring without competing for space, since the trees along the park side of the moat are less crowded than those directly inside the castle grounds. Evening light on the stone walls, reflected in the moat, is another underrated time to visit.
