Daimaru Umeda

Osaka

Daimaru's Umeda branch shares the same three-century company history as the Shinsaibashi flagship, but the current building is a modern one, completely rebuilt and reopened in 2011 with fourteen floors spanning B2 to 15F directly above and connected to JR Osaka Station. Rather than leaning on architectural heritage the way the Shinsaibashi store does, Umeda distinguishes itself through its floor lineup, most notably the largest Sanrio store in western Japan on the 5th floor and a cluster of pop-culture and hobby shops covering Nintendo, One Piece, Pokemon, and Capcom merchandise, alongside a full Tokyu Hands.

That mix makes it a useful stop for visitors interested in character goods and Japanese pop culture without traveling to Den Den Town or a dedicated specialty store, while still offering the full range of fashion, cosmetics, and gourmet floors expected of a major department store. Its direct connection to Osaka Station also makes it one of the most convenient department stores in the city to fold into a travel day, with no need for a separate trip.

The basement and lower floors get busy with commuters during rush hour, so visiting slightly outside peak times makes for a more relaxed shopping experience. Combine a stop here naturally with Hankyu Umeda Main Store and Hanshin Umeda Main Store, both within a short walk, for a single outing covering three of Umeda's major department stores.

3-1-1 Umeda, Kita Ward, Osaka
