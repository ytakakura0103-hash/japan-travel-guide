Weathercock House (Kazamidori no Yakata)

Kobe

Built around 1909 for a German trading merchant, this is the only brick-built ijinkan in the Kitano district, which makes it stand out from the wooden and half-timbered houses around it. The rooster-shaped weathervane on the roof gave the house its name and eventually lent its silhouette to postcards and souvenirs across the whole neighborhood. Inside, the rooms are furnished in a heavy, formal German style with dark wood furniture, stained glass, and a study that survived largely intact.

A visit through the house takes roughly 20 to 30 minutes, and it is usually sold as a joint ticket with the neighboring Moegi House, which sits just across a small plaza. That plaza, framed by both houses, is one of the most reliably photographed spots in Kitano, especially with the weathercock silhouette against the sky.

Because this is the flagship building of the district, it draws the heaviest crowds of any single ijinkan, particularly around midday and on weekends. Visiting right when the house opens, before tour groups arrive from Sannomiya, is the most realistic way to get a quiet look inside.

3-13-3 Kitanocho, Chuo-ku, Kobe

Open in Google Maps

About a 15-minute walk uphill from Sannomiya Station, at the top of the Kitano Ijinkan district.
