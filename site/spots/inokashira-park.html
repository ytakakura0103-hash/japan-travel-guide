Inokashira Park

Tokyo

Inokashira Park centers on a long, narrow pond in Kichijoji, ringed by a walking path shaded by cherry and other trees that make it one of the more relaxed green spaces in western Tokyo. Rowboats and swan-shaped pedal boats are available to rent on the pond, and a long-running local superstition claims that couples who ride together will eventually break up — a piece of folklore locals bring up often, half-joking, half-serious, and one that has done surprisingly little to stop couples from renting the boats anyway.

Beyond the pond, the park includes a small zoo, a pond-side shrine dedicated to Benzaiten, and Inokashira Park's own community of street performers and craft vendors, who set up along the paths especially on weekends. A relaxed walk around the pond takes about thirty minutes, while a fuller visit including the zoo or a boat ride can stretch to two hours or more.

Cherry blossom season, typically late March to early April, is the park's most popular period, when the pondside trees draw large weekend crowds for hanami picnics and boat rides; a weekday visit or early morning walk offers a much calmer alternative during that window. The park sits an easy walk from Kichijoji Station and connects naturally to a visit to the Ghibli Museum, which borders the park's western edge.
