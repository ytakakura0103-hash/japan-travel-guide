Yokohama Timber Wharf

Yokohama

Yokohama Timber Wharf opened in October 2025 within Rinko Park, immediately standing out for a timber-truss exterior that gives the building a warmer, more architecturally distinctive presence than the glass-and-steel towers typical of Minatomirai's waterfront developments. Its location directly on the park puts it right on the water, giving ground-floor tenants an ocean view that's become a significant part of the complex's appeal.

The building drew immediate attention for its opening tenant lineup, including Yokohama's first location of a popular fresh-donut specialist and a bakery cafe, both of which drew lines from the day they opened, a fairly reliable sign of a spot that's landed on the current wave of Japanese food trends. That kind of tenant curation, prioritizing buzzy, photogenic openings over generic retail, has helped the building establish itself quickly on social media.

Its position within Rinko Park makes it an easy stop to combine with a walk along the waterfront or a visit to nearby Minatomirai landmarks, adding a genuine dining and shopping destination to a stretch of park that was previously more about the view than anything to do once there.

Rinko Park, Nishi Ward, Yokohama
