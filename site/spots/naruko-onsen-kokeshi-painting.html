Naruko Onsen Town

Sendai

Naruko is one of the region's classic hot spring towns, its sulfuric waters drawing bathers for generations, but what sets it apart is how visibly tied it is to the kokeshi doll craft: small workshops with wood-turning lathes sit right along the main streets, many open for visitors to watch craftsmen at work or try painting a doll themselves. The town sits at the center of a wider hot spring area that includes several distinct spring sources nearby, each with a slightly different mineral character.

Day-bathing at participating ryokan typically costs a modest fee, and a kokeshi painting or turning experience is usually available separately at workshops around town.

Compared to Zao or Akiu, Naruko draws a noticeably smaller crowd of foreign visitors despite its long onsen pedigree, which keeps both the baths and the craft workshops feeling unhurried. The milky, sulfuric waters feel especially good against cold air during the snow season, when the town takes on a quieter, more contemplative atmosphere.
