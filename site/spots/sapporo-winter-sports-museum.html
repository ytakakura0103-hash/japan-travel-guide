Sapporo Winter Sports Museum

Sapporo

Located at the base of Okurayama Ski Jump Stadium, the Sapporo Winter Sports Museum focuses on the city's 1972 Winter Olympics legacy through interactive simulators rather than static displays. Visitors can try a ski-jump simulator that measures balance and reaction in a way modeled on real athlete data, along with cross-country skiing, speed skating, and bobsled simulations.

The exhibits work well for kids and adults with limited or no skiing background, since the simulators translate winter-sport mechanics into something approachable without requiring any real snow experience. It's a fairly compact museum, generally manageable in under an hour, which makes it easy to pair with the observation-deck lift ride at Okurayama itself.

Because it's bundled with the ski jump stadium rather than marketed as a standalone attraction, it draws a modest crowd even in peak season, and simulator wait times are usually short outside of school holiday periods.

It's a genuinely useful indoor option on a rainy or heavily snowing day, and combined with the Okurayama lift, gives a fuller sense of what Sapporo's Olympic history looked like beyond the downtown clock tower and museum plaques.

1274 Miyanomori, Chuo-ku, Sapporo, Hokkaido 064-0959

Open in Google Maps

Same access as Okurayama Ski Jump Stadium: about 30-40 minutes from Sapporo Station via subway to Maruyama-koen Station, then a bus to Okurayama.
