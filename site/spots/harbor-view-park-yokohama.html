Harbor View Park

Yokohama

Sitting at the point where the Yamate bluff drops down toward the port, this park has one of the more commanding views in central Yokohama, taking in the harbor mouth, passing ships, and on a clear day the Yokohama Bay Bridge in the distance. It marks the historic edge of the foreign settlement, and a portion of the park occupies the former site of a French consulate and garrison.\n\nRose gardens here bloom in both spring and autumn, and the park is a popular, if fairly well-known, photography spot during those windows, so weekday mornings are the more comfortable time to visit if avoiding crowds matters more than golden-hour light. Outside of rose season, it is considerably quieter and makes a pleasant, uncrowded rest stop on a Yamate walking route.\n\nIts position beside the Foreign General Cemetery and within walking distance of Berrick Hall and other preserved houses makes it a natural hub for exploring the bluff district, and the elevated view here offers a useful orientation point for visitors trying to get their bearings on how the port, Chinatown, and Yamate relate to each other geographically.
