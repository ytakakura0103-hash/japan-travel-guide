Onomichi U2

Hiroshima

Built inside a former Japan Transport Ministry maritime warehouse right on the Shimanami Kaido cycling route, Onomichi U2 was designed from the start around cyclists rather than treating them as an afterthought: rooms in its hotel section allow bikes indoors, there's an on-site rental and repair shop, and the open-plan building holds a bakery, a craft beer taproom, a restaurant, and a general store selling regional goods.

Even if you're not cycling the Shimanami Kaido, it's worth a stop for the building itself — long sightlines through raw concrete and steel trusses with the Seto Inland Sea visible through the end windows — and for a coffee or beer overlooking the water where riders set off toward Shikoku.

It sits at the actual starting point of the Shimanami Kaido route on the Onomichi side, so it works well as either a first stop before a ride or a reward stop after finishing one.

5-11 Nishigosho-cho, Onomichi, Hiroshima

Open in Google Maps

About a 5-minute walk from JR Onomichi Station, along the waterfront.
