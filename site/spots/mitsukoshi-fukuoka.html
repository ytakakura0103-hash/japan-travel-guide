Mitsukoshi Fukuoka

Fukuoka

Mitsukoshi Fukuoka opened in 1997 and quickly became a Tenjin landmark for a detail as much as for its shopping floors: Lion Square, a bronze lion statue at the store's entrance that's served as one of central Fukuoka's most recognizable meeting points for decades, the kind of "meet me at the lion" spot locals default to without needing further explanation. Now part of the Iwataya Mitsukoshi group under Isetan Mitsukoshi Holdings, the store sits a short walk from its sister store Iwataya.

As of 2026, the store is mid-transition: floors five through nine are converting from standard department store space into specialty retail under the Lashic brand, part of a broader remodel plan with a full reopening targeted for autumn 2027. The store continues operating normally throughout this process on its remaining floors, so it remains a functioning shopping destination rather than a closed or inaccessible building, though visitors should expect some floors to look different from a typical department store layout during the transition.

Lion Square out front remains a reliable, easy-to-describe meeting point regardless of what's happening with the store's internal floor plan, useful for anyone coordinating a group meetup in central Tenjin. Checking current floor offerings before a specific shopping trip is worth doing given the ongoing changes.
