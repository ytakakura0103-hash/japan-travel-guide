Kobe Municipal Oji Zoo

Kobe

Oji Zoo has operated since the 1950s and remains one of Kobe's most visited family attractions, largely thanks to its giant pandas, which draw visitors from well beyond the city since so few zoos in Japan keep the species. Beyond the panda enclosure, the zoo houses a broad range of animals typical of a mid-sized municipal zoo, including big cats, elephants, and a reptile house, spread across grounds that also include a small amusement park section with rides aimed at younger children.

A relaxed visit covering most of the grounds takes a few hours, and combining it with the small on-site amusement rides can easily fill most of a day for families. The zoo sits inland from the harbor in the Nada district, making it an easy add-on for anyone also visiting the Nada sake breweries nearby.

Panda viewing areas can get crowded, particularly around midday and on weekends, since they are the zoo's main draw; going in the first hour after opening tends to give calmer viewing conditions. The zoo has periodically undergone facility renewals, so checking current opening areas before visiting is worthwhile.
