Itoshima Sunset Road Cafe Hopping

Fukuoka

Itoshima's roughly 30-kilometer Sunset Road hugs the coastline past Keya-no-Oto, Sakurai Futamigaura, and a string of surf beaches, and over the past decade or two it has become known almost as much for its cafes as for its scenery. Former farmhouses and beachside plots have been converted into airy, wood-and-glass cafes serving pancakes, specialty coffee, and locally grown produce, many with ocean or rice-field views from the seating area.

The area's relaxed, slightly bohemian identity has grown alongside its surf culture, and it draws a steady stream of Fukuoka residents on weekend day trips as much as visitors from further afield, meaning popular spots can have a genuine wait around lunchtime on weekends. A car is by far the easiest way to explore, since cafes are spread along kilometers of coastal road rather than clustered in one walkable area.

Going on a weekday, or timing a visit for a late afternoon coffee rather than peak lunch hours, avoids most of the crowding. Because the specific roster of cafes changes over time as businesses open and close, it's worth checking current recommendations locally rather than chasing one specific name; the drive and the atmosphere are the constant, more than any single shop.
