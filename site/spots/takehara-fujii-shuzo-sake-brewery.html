Fujii Shuzo Sake Brewery

Hiroshima

Tucked into one of the narrower side alleys of Takehara's preserved merchant district, Fujii Shuzo has been brewing sake since 1863, making it one of the few producers still operating from within the historic townscape rather than a modern facility outside it. The brewery won a top prize at Japan's first national sake competition in 1907 and continues to use rice grown locally along with mountain spring water for its junmai-shu.\n\nVisitors can taste a rotating selection of the brewery's sake on-site, and an attached soba restaurant makes it possible to turn a quick tasting stop into a proper light meal, pairing the sake with simple noodle dishes rather than rushing straight back out to the street.\n\nBecause it sits inside the historic district itself, a visit here doubles as a way to see the interior of one of the old merchant buildings up close, with worn wooden beams and brewing equipment that make the connection between Takehara's Edo-era wealth and its sake tradition much more concrete than reading about it.

3-4-14 Honmachi, Takehara, Hiroshima

Open in Google Maps

Within Takehara's historic townscape district, about a 10-minute walk from JR Takehara Station.
