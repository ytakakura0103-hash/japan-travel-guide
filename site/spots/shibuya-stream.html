Shibuya Stream

Tokyo

Shibuya Stream runs along a stretch of the Shibuya River that had been paved over and hidden beneath the city for decades before this redevelopment opened the water back up to daylight. A landscaped walkway now follows the river from Shibuya Station toward Ebisu, lined with restaurant terraces, cafes, and small event spaces that spill outdoor seating right up to the water's edge. It is one of the few spots in central Shibuya where the pace slows down noticeably, and in the evening the terraces stay busy well after the department stores and boutiques nearby have pulled their shutters down.

There is no admission fee to walk the promenade itself, and most visitors treat it as a place to eat, drink, or simply stroll rather than a sightseeing stop with a fixed itinerary — twenty to thirty minutes covers a walk from end to end, longer if you stop for a meal. Lunchtime on weekdays and early evening on weekends are the busiest windows around the restaurant terraces, while a walk along the river in the late morning or right after opening tends to be much quieter.

Because it connects directly into Shibuya Station, Shibuya Stream works well as a link between the station and Ebisu Garden Place a bit further down the river, making it easy to combine both stops into a single relaxed walk rather than treating either as a dedicated destination.
