Higashiyama Zoo and Botanical Gardens

Nagoya

Higashiyama's animal collection is genuinely extensive, and the park has drawn national attention over the years for individual animals, including an orangutan once famous for learning to walk upright like a human. Beyond the zoo enclosures, the grounds continue into a large botanical garden with greenhouses, seasonal flower beds, and a Sky Tower offering panoramic views over the eastern hills of the city, all connected by shuttle or a fairly long walk.

Admission is inexpensive relative to the size of the site, and covering both the zoo and garden properly takes most of a day; visitors short on time often pick one section and save the other for a return trip. A monorail-style ride and several rest areas make it manageable for younger children over a long visit.

Weekends bring families in large numbers, particularly around the popular animal enclosures, while the botanical garden stays comparatively calm even then. Cherry blossoms in spring and autumn foliage in the botanical section each draw their own smaller seasonal crowds, worth timing a visit around if seasonal scenery matters to you.

3-70 Higashiyama-motomachi, Chikusa-ku, Nagoya, Aichi
