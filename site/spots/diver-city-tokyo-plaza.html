DiverCity Tokyo Plaza

Tokyo

DiverCity Tokyo Plaza is a large shopping mall on the Odaiba waterfront, but its main claim to fame is what stands outside it: a full-scale statue of a Gundam robot from the long-running anime and model franchise, built at a height matching the fictional robot's canonical size. At set intervals, the statue puts on a short show with lighting, sound, and limited movement, drawing a crowd of phones and cameras even from people who have no particular interest in the franchise; show times are posted at the site and change periodically, so it's worth checking the current schedule on arrival rather than assuming a fixed time.

Inside, the mall has a dedicated Gundam Base store selling model kits and franchise merchandise across multiple floors, alongside a broader mix of standard mall shops and restaurants that make it a reasonable stop even for visitors who aren't specifically Gundam fans. A separate cafe area themed around the franchise is also on site for anyone who wants to extend the visit.

The statue is lit and visible at night as well as during the day, and evening visits pair well with the Rainbow Bridge and skyline views elsewhere in Odaiba. Seeing the statue and one of its shows takes well under an hour; browsing the mall and the Gundam store can extend a visit considerably longer depending on interest.

1-1-10 Aomi, Koto City, Tokyo
