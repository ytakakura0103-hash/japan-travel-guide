Happy Cock

Fukuoka

Happy Cock has operated in Tenjin since 1995, giving it more than three decades of continuous history in a nightlife scene where clubs typically last only a few years before closing or rebranding. That longevity has made it something of an institution among Fukuoka's nightlife regulars, a reliable fixture that's outlasted countless newer venues that came and went around it.

Its location near Oyafuko-dori and Daimyo puts it in easy reach of Tenjin's densest concentration of bars and clubs, and its established reputation means it draws a loyal following alongside newer visitors curious about one of the area's oldest surviving clubs. The exact programming has evolved over its history, as is typical for a venue that's stayed open this long, so current genre focus is worth checking directly.

For visitors interested in Fukuoka's nightlife history as much as a night out itself, Happy Cock offers a rare chance to experience a club that predates most of what's currently operating around it. As with any long-running venue, current hours and event details are best confirmed close to a planned visit.
