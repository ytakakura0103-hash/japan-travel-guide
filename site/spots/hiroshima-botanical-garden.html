Hiroshima Botanical Garden

Hiroshima

Opened in 1976 on a hillside in the Saeki Ward suburbs, the Hiroshima Botanical Garden covers a large landscaped area organized into sections by plant type and region, including a rose garden, a Japanese garden, a fruit and vegetable area, and a large tropical greenhouse that stays warm year-round regardless of season outside. The rose garden is generally the headline attraction, with a bloom that peaks around late May and a smaller second bloom in autumn.\n\nBecause the grounds are extensive and largely flat or gently sloped, it is a popular spot for families and for visitors who want an unhurried outdoor walk rather than a dense museum circuit, and it includes playground areas and open lawns alongside the more curated plant beds. The greenhouse, which holds a large orchid collection among other tropical species, is a reliable option on rainy or cold days when the rest of the garden is less comfortable to walk.\n\nIt sits well outside the main tourist core of central Hiroshima, so it draws a mostly local crowd rather than international visitors, which gives it a noticeably quieter, more everyday atmosphere than the city-center parks.

3491 Kurose-cho, Saeki Ward, Hiroshima, Hiroshima Prefecture

Open in Google Maps

About 15 minutes by bus from JR Itsukaichi Station or JR Hiroshima Station; roughly 40 minutes total from central Hiroshima by train and bus.
