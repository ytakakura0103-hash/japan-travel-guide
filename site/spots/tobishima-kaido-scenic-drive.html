Tobishima Kaido Scenic Drive

Hiroshima

While the Shimanami Kaido gets most of the international attention, the Tobishima Kaido, connecting seven islands south of Kure — including Shimokamagarijima, Kamikamagarijima, Toyoshima, and Osakishimojima — offers a similar experience of island-hopping over water on a much smaller, quieter scale, with far less traffic and almost no organized tour groups.

The road is popular with local cyclists and drivers for its clear water, small citrus-farming communities, and short bridges that rarely involve the long climbs of the Shimanami Kaido's larger crossings, making it a more relaxed ride for cyclists who want scenery without the crowds or the distance.

The route culminates at Mitarai, the historic port town on Osakishimojima, giving the drive or ride a genuine destination rather than just a loop back to the mainland, and the whole stretch can be done as a day trip by car from Kure or as a longer, more leisurely cycling route for those with more time.
