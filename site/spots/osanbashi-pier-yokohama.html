Osanbashi Pier

Yokohama

Osanbashi is a functioning international cruise terminal, but its architectural claim to fame is the rooftop: a continuous wave-like deck of wooden planking and lawn, designed so the building itself reads as an extension of the harbor rather than a conventional structure. It is free to access at any hour, which is unusual for a harbor viewpoint in Yokohama and part of why it has become popular for wedding photography and casual evening visits alike.

From the deck, the view lines up the Landmark Tower, Red Brick Warehouse, and Marine Tower in a single panorama, making it one of the more efficient photo stops in the city for capturing several landmarks at once. Because a large cruise ship might be docked on any given day, the exact view can vary, and it is worth checking cruise schedules if you specifically want an unobstructed water view.

Evenings bring a steady but not overwhelming stream of visitors, since it draws locals as much as tourists, and the deck's scale means it rarely feels as packed as Yamashita Park nearby. Night views here are considered among the better free ones in the city, particularly after the harbor lights come on.

1-1-4 Kaigan-dori, Naka-ku, Yokohama
