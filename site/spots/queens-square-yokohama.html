Queen's Square Yokohama

Yokohama

Queen's Square is less a single building than a small vertical city, joining office towers, a hotel, and a concert hall around a shopping and dining podium at street level. For visitors it functions mainly as a climate-controlled shortcut and shopping stop between Minatomirai Station, Pacifico Yokohama, and the InterContinental hotel's distinctive sail-shaped building nearby.

The shops lean toward mid-range fashion and homeware, with a basement food floor that is useful for a quick meal without committing to a sit-down restaurant. It rarely feels crowded compared to MARK IS or the Red Brick Warehouse, since it draws more office workers on weekdays and event-goers on weekends when the concert hall or Pacifico Yokohama convention center has something scheduled.

Because the complex connects underground and at ground level to Minatomirai Station and the bayfront promenade toward Pacifico Yokohama, it is a practical stop if you are heading to or from an event at the convention center, or simply want a covered route through Minato Mirai on a rainy day. The open plaza facing the bay also offers an unobstructed, less-photographed view back toward the Landmark Tower.

2-3 Minatomirai, Nishi-ku, Yokohama
