Arima Onsen Gin-no-yu (Silver Spring public bathhouse)

Kobe

Gin-no-yu draws from Arima's other characteristic spring type, a clear, naturally carbonated radium spring quite different in character from the iron-rich reddish water at the nearby Kin-no-yu. The gently effervescent water is said locally to be good for the skin and for easing minor aches, and because the two bathhouses sit only a short uphill walk apart, some visitors try to fit in both during the same trip.

The building is smaller and less architecturally showcased than Kin-no-yu, and the visit itself follows the same basic pattern: pay admission, change, and soak, generally for around an hour when including changing time. There is no equivalent free foot bath directly outside, unlike its sister bathhouse, so it draws a more purposeful crowd of people specifically there to bathe rather than casual passersby.

Being set slightly back from Arima's main tourist street and overshadowed by the more famous Kin-no-yu, Gin-no-yu consistently sees a lighter crowd, making it the better choice for visitors who want a calmer, more local soak without a wait.

1039-1 Arimacho, Kita-ku, Kobe
