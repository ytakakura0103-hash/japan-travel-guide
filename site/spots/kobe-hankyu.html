Kobe Hankyu

Kobe

Kobe Hankyu occupies a landmark building directly beside Sannomiya Station that operated for eighty-five years as Sogo Kobe before closing under that name in September 2019 and reopening days later, on October 5, as Kobe Hankyu under the Hankyu Hanshin department store group. The transition was a rebrand and operator change rather than a genuine closure, and the store has continued operating in the same prominent location ever since, carrying forward its role as one of Sannomiya's most recognizable retail landmarks.

Under its current branding, the store leans into trend-forward fashion and luxury goods, positioning itself as a more contemporary shopping destination than its Sogo-era history might suggest. Its basement food hall carries a particularly strong reputation for European-style sweets, worth a stop even for visitors not otherwise planning to shop the store's fashion floors.

Given its location directly at Sannomiya Station, it functions as one of the most convenient department store stops in the city, and its proximity to Kobe Marui, Sannomiya OPA, and the Sannomiya Center-gai shopping arcade makes it easy to combine with a broader shopping day across the district's cluster of retail options.
