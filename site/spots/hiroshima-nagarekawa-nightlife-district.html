Nagarekawa Nightlife District

Hiroshima

Nagarekawa is Hiroshima's largest entertainment district, a dense grid of narrow streets holding well over a thousand bars, izakaya, snack bars, and karaoke spots within a few walking-distance blocks east of the central Hondori and Parco shopping area. It began as a haunt for local artists and writers before shifting decades ago into the city's primary nightlife zone, and today it draws everyone from salarymen on after-work drinks to visitors chasing a late-night bowl of Hiroshima-style tsukemen.

Unlike a single curated food street, Nagarekawa rewards wandering — small standing bars seating six people sit beside multi-floor karaoke complexes and casual yakitori counters, and the mix changes block by block. Prices skew reasonable compared to similar districts in Osaka or Tokyo, and English menus are less common here, which keeps the area feeling distinctly local rather than tourist-oriented.

It gets going after dark and stays lively well past midnight on weekends, making it a natural evening counterpart to a daytime spent at the Peace Park or Hiroshima Castle a short walk away.
