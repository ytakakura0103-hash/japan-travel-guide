Takeshima Aquarium

Nagoya

Takeshima Aquarium has built its reputation less on scale, it's reportedly the fourth-smallest aquarium in Japan, than on personality, filling its modest exhibit space with handwritten signage that describes each animal in blunt, often self-deprecating terms rather than the polished educational copy typical of larger institutions. That combination of a tiny budget and genuinely funny, low-key charm has made it a recurring subject of social media rediscovery for years, the kind of place people share specifically because it undersells itself so completely.

A major renovation completed in October 2024 doubled the aquarium's floor space with a new wing, giving it room to expand its exhibits without abandoning the scrappy, handmade character that made it popular in the first place. It remains a small, easily walkable aquarium rather than a full-day destination, closer in spirit to a well-loved local institution than a major tourist attraction.

Its location near Gamagori Station and the causeway out to Takeshima Island, already covered elsewhere in this guide, makes it easy to combine both into a single coastal day trip from Nagoya. Families with young children in particular tend to find its scale and pace more manageable than a larger aquarium, without sacrificing the appeal of seeing marine life up close.

Gamagori, Aichi Prefecture
