World Porters

Yokohama

World Porters leans more casual and youth-oriented than the malls closer to Minatomirai Station, with a broad mix of Japanese fashion brands, an imported goods and variety store popular for browsing rather than serious shopping, and record and hobby shops that give it a slightly different character from the more upscale MARK IS. A multiplex cinema and food court on the upper floors make it a practical stop for a meal or a movie as well as shopping.

Its location puts it within easy walking distance of Cosmo World, the Cup Noodles Museum, and the Red Brick Warehouse, so it works naturally as a shopping break in the middle of a Shinko waterfront day rather than a standalone destination. Weekend afternoons, especially when a new movie release is drawing crowds to the cinema, are the busiest times.

A rooftop area offers a quieter, less-visited view over the surrounding waterfront district, worth a quick look for visitors who want a break from the shop floors below without leaving the building. It is generally less crowded than the Red Brick Warehouse a short walk away, making it a reasonable fallback shopping stop when that plaza is packed.

2-2-1 Shinko, Naka-ku, Yokohama
