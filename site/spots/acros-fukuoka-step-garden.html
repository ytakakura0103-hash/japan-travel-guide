ACROS Fukuoka Step Garden

Fukuoka

ACROS Fukuoka is a working concert hall and conference center, but its most photographed feature is the exterior: a series of stepped terraces on the Tenjin Central Park side, planted with a dense mix of trees and shrubs that has matured into something closer to a hillside than a rooftop garden. Climbing the terraces gives a gradually rising view over Tenjin Central Park below and the surrounding city skyline.

Access is only from the park side; the garden cannot be reached from inside the building itself, so look for the staircases along the park-facing facade. Entry is free, but hours are seasonal, and the very top rooftop deck is generally only open on weekends and national holidays, meaning weekday visitors may only be able to reach the upper terrace levels rather than the true summit.

The climb involves a fair number of stairs and inclines with only occasional benches, so it isn't the easiest option for anyone with mobility concerns. Weekday late afternoons tend to be quiet, while weekend rooftop access naturally draws more visitors chasing the full view.

1-1-1 Tenjin, Chuo Ward, Fukuoka
