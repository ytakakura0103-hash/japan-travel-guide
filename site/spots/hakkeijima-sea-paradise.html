Hakkeijima Sea Paradise

Yokohama

Built across its own reclaimed island, Hakkeijima combines several aquarium buildings, including large tanks for dolphins, belugas, and other marine life, with a conventional amusement park of roller coasters and family rides, plus shops and restaurants scattered across the site. The scale sets it apart from anything in central Yokohama, and it draws visitors specifically for a full day out rather than as a quick stop on a broader sightseeing route.

It sits noticeably further from Minato Mirai and Chinatown than the rest of this list, out in Kanazawa-ku, so it is worth budgeting real travel time and treating it as a separate half or full day rather than squeezing it in alongside central Yokohama sights. Dolphin and other animal shows run on a set schedule, and checking showtimes in advance helps in planning the day around the highlights.

Weekends and Japanese school holidays bring significant crowds, particularly for the more popular shows and rides, so a weekday visit makes a noticeable difference here. The aquarium sections tend to stay comfortable even when the amusement park side gets busy, making them a reasonable fallback if ride lines get long.
