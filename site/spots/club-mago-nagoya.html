CLUB MAGO

Nagoya

CLUB MAGO occupies a basement space in Shin-Sakae, just east of central Sakae, and has built a following over the years as one of the more community-rooted venues in Nagoya's club scene. Its survival through the pandemic wasn't guaranteed: alongside its sister venue in the same building, Live & Lounge Vio, it ran a public crowdfunding campaign in 2020 to stay afloat during the enforced closures, a period that shuttered a number of Nagoya clubs for good.

That the club came through the other side and has resumed a regular event schedule says something about the loyalty of its local following, and it continues to draw a crowd that values the venue's history alongside its current programming. Sharing a building with Vio gives visitors the option of moving between two different rooms and atmospheres without leaving, useful on a night when one room's music isn't quite the right fit.

Its Shin-Sakae location puts it a short walk east of the main Sakae nightlife cluster, slightly removed from the density of Joshidai and Nishiki, which gives it a marginally calmer approach even on a busy night. Checking the current event calendar before visiting is recommended, as with most independent Nagoya clubs.

Unryu Flex Building West, B2F, Shin-Sakae 2-1-9, Naka Ward, Nagoya
