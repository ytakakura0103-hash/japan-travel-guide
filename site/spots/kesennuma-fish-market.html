Kesennuma Fish Market

Sendai

Kesennuma is one of Japan's major fishing ports and is particularly known for shark and swordfish landings, with the market's catch processed so thoroughly that little goes to waste, turning up in everything from shark fin to leather goods and health supplements. Auctions typically run in the early morning hours on most days, with a long observation deck on the market's second floor letting visitors watch the process from above, since the auction floor itself is off-limits to the public.

The viewing deck is free, and the building also houses shops and restaurants selling freshly landed and processed seafood, along with Japan's only dedicated shark museum on the same floor as the observation area.

Because Kesennuma sits well north of the more typical Sendai-Matsushima-Ishinomaki route, relatively few travelers make it this far, keeping the market a genuinely working, unpolished stop rather than a tourist production. Catch volumes and variety shift with the season, so an early morning visit on a market day gives the best sense of the port in full swing.
