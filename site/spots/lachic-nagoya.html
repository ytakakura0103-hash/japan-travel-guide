Lachic

Nagoya

Lachic opened in March 2005 with a name coined from the Japanese phrase for "be yourself," and it's grown into what's widely considered Nagoya's most fashion-forward shopping building, curating cutting-edge select shops like Beams and United Arrows across eight floors rather than aiming for the broad, everything-under-one-roof approach of a traditional department store. An open atrium spanning the first three floors gives the interior a more open, gallery-like feel than the tighter floor plans typical of Japanese retail buildings.

The top two floors are given over entirely to dining, including options built around Nagoya's distinctive local cuisine, giving Lachic a reason to visit even outside of shopping hours. Its target customer skews younger and more trend-conscious than Mitsukoshi next door, and the passageway connecting the two buildings makes it easy to move between Lachic's curated boutiques and Mitsukoshi's broader department store offering without leaving the complex.

For visitors interested in current Japanese fashion trends specifically, Lachic is one of the more reliable single stops in the city, gathering a curated cross-section of what's currently popular rather than requiring a walk through multiple separate shopping streets.
