Misen (Taiwan Ramen Originator)

Nagoya

Despite the name, Taiwan ramen was invented in Nagoya, not Taiwan: a chef originally from Taiwan running this restaurant in the Imaike neighborhood adapted a spicy Taiwanese noodle dish for local tastes decades ago, and the result, topped with minced pork, chili, and garlic in a fiery broth, became popular enough to spread across the city under a name that still confuses first-time visitors. Misen is generally credited as the restaurant where the dish originated, making it a genuine culinary landmark rather than just another ramen shop.

A bowl is priced like a typical noodle-shop meal, and the heat level can be intense, so it's worth asking about spice options if you're not used to Sichuan-style chili oil. A meal takes about thirty minutes.

The restaurant sits in a fairly local, unassuming stretch of Imaike rather than a tourist zone, so it rarely feels overrun, though a lunch or dinner rush from neighborhood regulars is common at peak hours; timing a visit slightly off those peaks makes for an easier, unhurried meal.

Imaike area, Chikusa-ku, Nagoya, Aichi
