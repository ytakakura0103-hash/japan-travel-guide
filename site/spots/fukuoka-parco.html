Fukuoka PARCO

Fukuoka

Fukuoka PARCO spans a Main Building, basement to eighth floor, and a newer New Building, basement two to sixth floor, with direct access from Tenjin Station making it one of the most convenient shopping stops in central Fukuoka. Its tenant mix follows the PARCO brand's typical fashion-forward, youth-oriented approach, but its pop-culture offering stands out even by PARCO's usual standards.

A large official One Piece store draws fans of the globally popular manga and anime series, one of the more substantial dedicated pop-culture retail spaces in Kyushu, while the sixth floor "Tamariba" space functions as a combined arts, culture, and bookshop area rather than standard retail, giving the building a cultural dimension beyond shopping. A Hands Be outlet and attached cafe round out the lifestyle offering.

Its direct station access makes it an easy first or last stop on a Tenjin shopping trip, and its proximity to Iwataya, Daimaru Fukuoka, and Mitsukoshi Fukuoka means it's simple to combine with the district's other major department stores for a fuller day of browsing.
