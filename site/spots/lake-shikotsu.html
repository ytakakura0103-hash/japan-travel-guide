Lake Shikotsu

Hokkaido

Lake Shikotsu is regularly cited among Japan's clearest lakes, its deep, cold caldera water rarely freezing over even in Hokkaido's harsh winters, which makes it unusual among the region's larger lakes. It sits inside Shikotsu-Toya National Park, ringed by the conical volcanic peaks of Mount Eniwa and Mount Tarumae, the latter still occasionally active and climbable via a relatively accessible trail with views into its own crater.

Compared to Lake Toya, Shikotsu has developed less as a resort town, with a smaller onsen village on its shore and fewer large hotels, which gives it a noticeably quieter, more natural feel; boat cruises and kayaking on the exceptionally clear water are among the more popular ways to experience it directly rather than just viewing it from shore.

Public transport here is genuinely limited, with more direct bus service from Chitose or New Chitose Airport than from central Sapporo, so a rental car or organized tour is the practical way to see more than the immediate lakeside village. Autumn foliage around the crater lake, typically peaking in October, is a strong seasonal draw, while summer suits the kayaking and camping facilities around the shore better than the colder months.
