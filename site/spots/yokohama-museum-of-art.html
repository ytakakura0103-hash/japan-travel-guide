Yokohama Museum of Art

Yokohama

The museum's collection spans Japanese and Western modern and contemporary art, including works connected to Yokohama's own history as a port city open to foreign influence earlier than most of Japan. After closing for a lengthy renovation, it reopened with a brighter, more open entrance hall and an expanded free-to-enter Grand Gallery area, so even visitors who skip the paid exhibitions can experience part of the building.\n\nExhibition lineups change through the year and can include major touring shows alongside the permanent collection, so it is worth checking what is currently on before planning a visit, since ticket prices and crowd levels vary a lot depending on the show. The permanent collection galleries tend to be calmer than special exhibitions, especially on weekday mornings.\n\nSituated at the far end of Grand Mall Park from Minatomirai Station, the museum works well as a slower final stop on a Minato Mirai day, after the busier attractions like Cosmo World or the Landmark Tower. The building's severe, symmetrical stone facade is itself a notable piece of 1980s architecture worth a look even from outside.

3-4-1 Minatomirai, Nishi-ku, Yokohama
