Ebisu Yokocho

Tokyo

Ebisu Yokocho occupies the site of a former shopping arcade, the Yamashita Shopping Center, repurposed into a covered alley lined with roughly 20 stalls, each only a few square meters, serving everything from yakitori and oden to sushi and curry alongside drinks. It grew out of what used to be a fairly ordinary salaryman drinking spot into a younger, more mixed crowd today, though the cramped, communal-table layout still forces the kind of conversation with strangers that made the format popular in the first place.

Compared to similar alley formats elsewhere in Tokyo, Ebisu Yokocho leans noticeably more toward food than pure drinking, with a wider variety of small kitchens than a typical izakaya alley, which makes it a reasonable option for a full casual dinner rather than only a stop for drinks. Several stalls stay open very late, and it has become a common last stop for people who started their night elsewhere and want food before heading home.

The alley is compact enough to see in full within a few minutes of walking through, though finding a seat at a specific stall on a busy night can take some patience. Weeknights tend to be calmer and easier to get a seat than weekends, when the narrow aisles fill up quickly with both regulars and visitors drawn by the atmosphere alone.

1-7-4 Ebisu, Shibuya City, Tokyo 150-0013

About 2 minutes on foot from Ebisu Station, east exit (JR Yamanote, Saikyo, and Shonan-Shinjuku Lines, or Tokyo Metro Hibiya Line).
