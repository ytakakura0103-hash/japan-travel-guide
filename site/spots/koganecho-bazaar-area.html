Koganecho Art Underpass

Yokohama

Koganecho, just one stop from Yokohama Station on the Keikyu Line, had a rough reputation for illegal establishments packed under the elevated railway until a city-backed cleanup began in the mid-2000s. Rather than simply demolishing the strip, organizers invited artists to take over the small studio spaces, and the result is a slightly surreal walk beneath the tracks past glass-fronted galleries, a secondhand art-book shop, and rotating installations.\n\nThe area hosts the Koganecho Bazaar, a citywide art festival held over several weeks each autumn, when the studios open up with exhibitions and events. Outside festival dates, only some spaces are staffed, so the experience is more about the atmosphere of the repurposed underpass than a guaranteed gallery visit.\n\nThis is very much a locals'-pace neighborhood rather than a polished museum district, and that unfinished, still-transitioning feel is part of the appeal for visitors curious about how Japanese cities handle urban renewal. It pairs naturally with a walk along the Ooka River toward Noge. Weekday afternoons are quiet enough to wander without crowds; check the area management center's site beforehand if you want to catch a specific studio open.

Koganecho / Hinodecho area, Naka-ku, Yokohama

1 minute on foot from Keikyu Line Koganecho Station, or about 15 minutes on foot from JR Kannai Station.
