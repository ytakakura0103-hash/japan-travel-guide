Rusutsu Resort

Hokkaido

Rusutsu sits a short distance from the better-known Niseko ski area and shares much of the same reliably heavy powder snowfall, but distinguishes itself with an unusual pairing: a full ski resort spread across three connected peaks alongside a permanent amusement park at the base, complete with a Ferris wheel, roller coaster and other rides that keep running through winter, snow and all. It's a genuinely odd juxtaposition, riding a roller coaster bundled up against sub-zero temperatures, that has become something of a novelty draw in its own right.

Compared to Hirafu's dense, internationally oriented base village at Niseko, Rusutsu feels more self-contained and resort-style, built around a small number of large hotels rather than a sprawling town, which suits travelers who prefer an all-in-one base over Niseko's livelier, more scattered nightlife.

Winter, roughly December through March, is by far the resort's busiest and most defining season given its ski terrain and snowfall reputation; the amusement park itself typically operates on a more limited schedule outside summer and early autumn, so it's worth checking current operating dates if the rides specifically are the draw. Families with a mix of skiers and non-skiers may find the combination of slopes and rides a practical way to keep everyone entertained without splitting up for the day.

13 Izumikawa, Rusutsu-mura, Abuta-gun, Hokkaido
