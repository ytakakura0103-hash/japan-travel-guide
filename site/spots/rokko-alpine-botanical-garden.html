Rokko Alpine Botanical Garden

Kobe

Taking advantage of Mount Rokko's cooler summit climate, this garden specializes in alpine and subarctic plant species gathered from mountain regions around the world, arranged across sloped terrain that mimics the rocky, high-elevation conditions many of these plants need. It gives visitors a chance to see flora, including some rare or region-specific alpine flowers, that simply could not grow in Kobe's warmer lowland areas.

The garden is organized into themed sections by region and habitat, and a full walk through takes roughly an hour for anyone who wants to read the plant labels and take in each section, less for a quick pass-through. Bloom timing varies considerably by species and season, with different sections at their peak from spring through summer.

As one of the lesser-visited stops among Mount Rokko's cluster of attractions, which also include the more popular Garden Terrace and cable car views, this garden generally sees light foot traffic even during good weather weekends, appealing more to plant enthusiasts than typical sightseers passing through.
