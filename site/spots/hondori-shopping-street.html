Hondori Shopping Street

Hiroshima

Hondori has functioned as central Hiroshima's primary commercial street since well before the war, and it was rebuilt as a covered, car-free arcade in the postwar reconstruction of the city. Today it stretches for several blocks between the castle and Peace Memorial Park areas, lined with a mix of national chain stores, department stores, and smaller independent shops selling clothing, souvenirs, and local snacks.

Because it sits roughly between the castle, Shukkeien Garden, and Peace Memorial Park, it works well as a connecting route rather than a separate destination, letting visitors break up a day of sightseeing with a meal or a bit of shopping. Food options along and just off the street range from quick okonomiyaki counters to cafes and the seasonal fruit-based sweets Hiroshima is known for, such as fresh fruit parfaits made with lemons from the Setouchi islands.

The arcade is fully covered, so it stays a reliable option in bad weather, and it connects directly to smaller side streets and alleys, including some of the nightlife areas around Nagarekawa and Yagenbori just to its east.

Hondori, Naka Ward, Hiroshima, Hiroshima Prefecture
