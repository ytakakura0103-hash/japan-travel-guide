Otakamori Observatory

Sendai

Otakamori sits on Miyato Island in the Okumatsushima area and is counted, alongside Saigyo Modoshi no Matsu Park, among the traditionally recognized "four great views" of Matsushima Bay, offering a wide panorama over the islets from a different angle than the more visited viewpoints closer to central Matsushima.

Reaching the top requires a moderate uphill walk of roughly thirty to forty minutes through forest, and there's no admission fee. It combines naturally with a visit to Nobiru Beach or the wider Okumatsushima area.

Because it demands more effort to reach than the boat-cruise vantage points near Matsushima's main pier, Otakamori sees a fraction of the visitor numbers despite ranking among the bay's best-regarded views. Autumn foliage across the surrounding hillsides makes it an especially rewarding climb in October and November.

Via JR Senseki Line to Nobiru Station, then a taxi (about 10 minutes, roughly 2,000 yen) to the trailhead; walking from the station takes well over an hour and isn't the practical option.
