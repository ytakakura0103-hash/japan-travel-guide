Local Pick

Yakurai Garden

Sendai

Yakurai Garden spreads across roughly 150,000 square meters in Kami, a rural town northwest of Sendai, planting itself firmly in a different category from the city's more famous nature spots like Matsushima or Zao. Rather than a single dramatic landscape, its appeal is horticultural and seasonal: rapeseed flowers in spring, hydrangeas in early summer, sunflowers through the summer months, and cosmos in autumn keep the fields in near-constant color across most of the year.

A bright pink garden gate set among the flower beds has become the garden's signature photo spot, widely shared on social media as a simple, colorful frame for portraits against whatever's currently blooming. It's a straightforward, low-effort composition that's helped the garden build a following well beyond its rural location would otherwise suggest, particularly among visitors chasing a specific shot rather than just a scenic walk.

Because it sits about an hour from central Sendai with limited public transit access, a rental car or taxi is the practical way to get there, making it more of a deliberate half-day trip than a casual add-on. Checking which flowers are currently in bloom before visiting is worth doing, since the garden's appeal depends heavily on hitting the right seasonal window.

Kami, Kamimasu District, Miyagi Prefecture
