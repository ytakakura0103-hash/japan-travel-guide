ORCA NAGOYA

Nagoya

ORCA NAGOYA opened in February 2015 across the upper floors of a building on the edge of Nishiki and has grown into one of Sakae's largest and most established nightclubs, with a mainstream booking policy spanning EDM and hip-hop aimed at a broad night-out crowd rather than a niche electronic-music audience. Its upper-floor location gives it skyline views uncommon at most club venues, a detail that sets it apart from the basement-level clubs found in much of Nagoya's nightlife scene.

The club's production team also runs CLUB SANGO nearby, giving the two venues a shared identity and making it easy to move between them over the course of a night if one room's music isn't landing. Both draw from the same general Sakae crowd, a mix of locals and visitors out for a mainstream club night rather than underground electronic music specifically.

Given its scale and central Sakae location, ORCA NAGOYA works well as an anchor point for an evening that also takes in Nishiki's more upscale bars or the Joshidai club cluster nearby. Checking the current event calendar before visiting is worthwhile, since guest DJ nights and cover charges vary considerably.

Sakae Nanairo Building, 11F-12F, Naka Ward, Nagoya
