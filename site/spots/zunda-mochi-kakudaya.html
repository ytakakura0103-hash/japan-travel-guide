Kakudaya (Original Zunda Mochi Shop)

Sendai

Zunda, a bright green paste made from mashed sweet edamame, sugar, and a touch of salt, has become shorthand for Sendai cuisine, showing up on convenience-store shelves and souvenir shops citywide. Fewer visitors make it to the older, family-run confectioneries where the modern zunda mochi trend actually got its commercial start, of which Kakudaya is one of the most established, still making the paste in small batches rather than mass-producing it.\n\nThe difference from the packaged versions sold at the station is noticeable: the edamame flavor is fresher and less uniformly sweet, and the mochi itself has a softer, more delicate texture that doesn't survive long shelf storage, which is exactly why shops like this stay local rather than going national. Beyond the classic zunda mochi, some shops in this tradition also sell it as a filling for daifuku or as a topping for shaved ice in summer.\n\nPrices for a few pieces are inexpensive, closer to an everyday sweet than a tourist splurge. It's a quiet, unglamorous shop rather than a destination in itself, which is part of the appeal, most visitors who know about it stop in on the way to or from somewhere else downtown rather than making a special trip. Weekday afternoons are the calmest time to visit.

Chuo area, Aoba-ku, Sendai, Miyagi
