WORLD KYOTO

Kyoto

WORLD KYOTO opened in July 2001 just off Shijo Kawaramachi and has stood as one of Kyoto's largest nightclubs ever since, with a capacity well over a thousand that puts it in a different league from the city's smaller, more intimate club spaces. Its programming centers on techno, house, and broader electronica, and it has a track record of booking touring international DJs alongside domestic acts, giving it more scale and reach than most of Kyoto's nightlife scene.

The club suspended operations in March 2020 during the pandemic, backed at the time by a public fundraising campaign from supporters hoping to see it survive, and it has since resumed a regular schedule of events. That history is worth knowing as context for a club that's become something of a fixture in Kyoto's otherwise thin big-venue nightlife landscape, alongside Osaka's much larger club scene an hour away.

Its location just off Shijo Kawaramachi puts it within easy walking distance of Pontocho and Kiyamachi-dori, making it simple to fold into an evening that starts with dinner or drinks in either district. As with any club, checking the current event calendar in advance is worthwhile, since genre and guest DJ lineups vary considerably by night.
