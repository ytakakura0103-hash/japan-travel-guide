Sapporo TV Tower

Hokkaido

Built in 1957 at the eastern tip of Odori Park, this red lattice tower is one of Sapporo's most recognizable silhouettes even though, at roughly 90 meters, it is modest compared to observation towers in bigger cities. Its main draw is the angle: because it sits right at the end of the park, the observation deck gives a straight, elevated view down the length of Odori and out toward the mountains ringing the city, including Mount Moiwa on a clear day.

The deck is small enough that a visit rarely takes more than 30-45 minutes, which makes it an easy stop before or after a walk through the park rather than a destination in itself. At night, the tower itself is lit up and photographs well from the park below, which is part of why it appears in so many Sapporo postcards.

Because it sits directly above one of the park's subway exits, it can get a burst of visitors right after tour buses unload; going up in the first hour after opening or later in the evening tends to be calmer. During the Snow Festival the deck offers a bird's-eye view of the sculptures below, which is worth the ticket price in winter specifically, while in other seasons the view is pleasant but less dramatic.

Odori Nishi 1-chome, Chuo-ku, Sapporo, Hokkaido
