Yagenbori Backstreet Izakaya Row

Hiroshima

Running roughly parallel to Nagarekawa a couple of blocks over, Yagenbori is a narrower, less polished stretch that leans more heavily toward small, counter-style izakaya than the flashier bars and karaoke spots dominating its bigger neighbor. Many of the shops here seat under ten people, are run by a single owner-chef, and specialize in a narrow menu — grilled skewers, oden, or fresh seafood — done well rather than trying to cover everything.\n\nThe street has a reputation locally that includes some red-light-adjacent bars mixed in among the food spots, so it has a rougher, more unfiltered feel than the main Nagarekawa strip, which some visitors find more atmospheric and others prefer to skip. Either way, it's a useful contrast to the brighter, louder main drag a short walk away.\n\nFor visitors interested in Hiroshima's izakaya culture beyond the well-lit main streets, wandering Yagenbori after dark and picking a counter that looks busy with locals is a reasonably reliable way to eat well without a reservation or an English menu.

Yagenbori, Naka-ku, Hiroshima
