NEWoMan & CIAL Yokohama

Yokohama

NEWoMan and CIAL Yokohama opened together in 2020 inside the newly built JR Yokohama Tower at the station's west exit, splitting the tower's retail space by floor and focus. NEWoMan occupies the first through tenth floors with a fashion and lifestyle lineup aimed specifically at working women, a positioning shared with its better-known Tokyo counterpart at Shinjuku Station, while CIAL takes the basement levels, spanning three floors dedicated almost entirely to dining and food retail.

That division gives the complex a clear structure for visitors: head up for fashion and lifestyle shopping, head down for a meal or food shopping, without the two experiences blending into an undifferentiated mass of shops. Being purpose-built alongside the tower itself, rather than retrofitted into an older building, both sections have a more contemporary, polished feel than some of Yokohama Station's older shopping complexes.

Its location within JR Yokohama Tower puts it directly at the station's west exit, making it one of the most convenient shopping and dining stops for anyone traveling through Yokohama Station on the JR side specifically, complementing the Sotetsu-affiliated JOINUS and Takashimaya nearby on the same side of the station.
