EDION Peace Wing Hiroshima

Hiroshima

EDION Peace Wing Hiroshima opened in February 2024 as a genuinely city-center football stadium, built directly within Chuo Park rather than on the outskirts of town where most Japanese stadiums are located. That placement makes it walkable from Kamiyacho and Hatchobori, central Hiroshima's main commercial districts, an unusual level of accessibility for a stadium of its scale.

Its open-air design keeps the venue connected to the surrounding park rather than sealing it off as a self-contained structure, and a logo object outside the stadium has become a minor photo landmark in its own right. Inside, a collaboration mural themed around Captain Tsubasa, the internationally famous football manga and anime series, decorates part of the second-floor concourse, a distinctly Japanese pop-culture touch inside an otherwise modern sports venue.

Even without a match to attend, the stadium's exterior and surrounding park make for a worthwhile stop for anyone interested in contemporary architecture or football culture, and its connection to Hiroshima Castle Sannomaru and Hiroshima Gate Park via pedestrian deck makes it easy to fold into a broader walk through this part of the city.
