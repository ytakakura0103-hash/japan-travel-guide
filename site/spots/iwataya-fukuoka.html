Iwataya

Fukuoka

Iwataya's roots trace back to 1754, when it began as a kimono shop, and it grew into Kyushu's first true terminal department store when it opened its main Tenjin location in 1936, a milestone that helped establish Tenjin as the region's premier shopping and business district. Today it remains Kyushu's top-selling department store, spread across roughly 48,500 square meters and covering the full range of fashion, cosmetics, and gourmet food expected of a major flagship.

One detail that sets Iwataya apart from other department stores is its shopping bags: since 1959, the store has commissioned bag designs from painter Kenzo Okada, drawing on regional culture and motifs specific to Kyushu, turning a simple carrier bag into something closer to a small piece of design history that regular customers recognize instantly. Now part of Iwataya Mitsukoshi Ltd. under the Isetan Mitsukoshi Holdings group, it sits alongside its sister store Mitsukoshi Fukuoka nearby, though it retains its own distinct identity and clientele.

Its central Tenjin location puts it within easy walking distance of Daimaru Fukuoka, Mitsukoshi Fukuoka, and the rest of Tenjin's dense shopping district, making it simple to combine with a broader day of department store browsing. As the region's top-selling store, it's a reasonable benchmark for what a major Kyushu department store experience looks like.

Tenjin, Chuo Ward, Fukuoka
