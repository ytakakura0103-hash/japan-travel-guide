Kobe Marui

Kobe

Kobe Marui, part of the nationwide Marui (0101) fashion chain, gathers roughly seventy brands across five floors in central Sannomiya, with a tenant lineup skewing toward young women's fashion typical of the Marui brand's broader positioning across Japan. Its scale and central location make it a reliable stop for current Japanese fashion trends without needing to seek out individual boutiques scattered across the district.

Less obviously, the building's second floor houses ANCHOR, covered elsewhere in this guide as generally the only genuine dance nightclub operating in Kobe, giving the building an evening identity that most department stores don't share. That combination means a single building can serve both an afternoon shopping trip and a late-night club visit, worth knowing for anyone planning both in the same area.

Its Sannomiya-machi location puts it within easy walking distance of Kobe Hankyu, Sannomiya OPA, and the Center-gai shopping arcade, making it simple to fold into a broader shopping day, with the added option of returning after dark for the club upstairs.

Sannomiya-machi 1-7-2, Chuo Ward, Kobe
