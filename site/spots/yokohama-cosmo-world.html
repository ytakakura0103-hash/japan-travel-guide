Yokohama Cosmo World and Cosmo Clock 21

Yokohama

Cosmo World is unusual for an amusement park in that there is no admission fee at the gate; visitors pay per ride instead, which makes it easy to wander in just to look at the Cosmo Clock 21 wheel and its enormous digital clock face, then decide whether to actually ride it. At around 100 meters tall it was once among the largest Ferris wheels anywhere, and it remains one of the most recognizable landmarks in the bay, especially once its LED rim lights up after dark.

The park is split into two sections connected by a bridge over the old shipping canal, with a mix of family-friendly rides, a roller coaster that dips toward the water, and games stalls. Weekday afternoons are noticeably quieter than weekends, when the park fills with families and couples on Minato Mirai dates.

Because it sits between the Cup Noodles Museum, World Porters mall, and the Red Brick Warehouse, Cosmo World works well as a midpoint stop on a longer Minato Mirai walk rather than a destination in itself, unless you are traveling with kids who want the rides. The illuminated wheel is at its best on clear evenings, and it is also visible from Yokohama Air Cabin gondolas passing overhead.

2-8-1 Shinko, Naka-ku, Yokohama

Open in Google Maps

2-minute walk from Minatomirai Station, or 10-minute walk from Sakuragicho Station
