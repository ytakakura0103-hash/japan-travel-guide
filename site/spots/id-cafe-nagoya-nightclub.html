iD Cafe

Nagoya

iD Cafe operates out of multiple floors inside Sakae's Mitsukoshi department store building, an unusual setting that puts one of Japan's largest multi-floor nightclubs directly above a mainstream retail anchor. Its scale lets it run several different rooms and genres simultaneously, giving it more range across a single night than most Nagoya clubs, which typically commit to one sound per room.

Unusually for a Japanese club, it opens comparatively early, around 7pm, and runs into the early morning, making it more accessible for visitors who want to start a night out before midnight rather than waiting for the later opening hours typical of clubs in Sakae's Joshidai cluster. That earlier start has helped it build a broad, mixed crowd spanning different ages and music tastes rather than skewing toward one particular scene.

Its location directly in central Sakae, connected to one of the area's major department stores, makes it one of the most straightforward clubs in the city to find and reach. As with any large multi-room venue, checking which floor is running which genre on a given night is worth doing before arriving, since the lineup can vary significantly floor to floor.

Mitsukoshi Building, Sakae 3-1-15, Naka Ward, Nagoya
