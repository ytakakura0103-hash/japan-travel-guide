Sakunami Onsen

Sendai

Sakunami sits in a narrow valley along the Hirose River, a stop on the JR Senzan Line best known locally as a convenient hot spring escape rather than a headline tourist destination. A handful of ryokan line the riverbanks here, several of which open their baths to day visitors, offering mineral-rich waters in a genuinely quiet, forested setting.\n\nDay-bathing fees are generally modest, and a soak plus some time relaxing in a lounge area makes for an easy two-to-three-hour visit. It's reachable directly by train from central Sendai, without needing a car.\n\nBecause it sits in the shadow of better-known onsen towns like Akiu and Naruko, Sakunami sees far fewer foreign visitors, even though the experience is comparable. Snow season is particularly atmospheric here, with steam rising off the outdoor baths against a backdrop of snow-covered hills.

Sakunami area, Aoba-ku, Sendai, Miyagi
