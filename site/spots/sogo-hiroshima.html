Sogo Hiroshima

Hiroshima

Sogo Hiroshima opened in October 1974 at the Kamiyacho intersection, one of central Hiroshima's most recognizable landmark corners, and was built with an integrated bus terminal alongside the adjacent Aqua Hiroshima Center Street shopping arcade. An annex building added in 1994 has since closed as its lease expired, but the main department store building remains fully open and is currently in the middle of an active, multi-year renovation.

That renovation has moved through the store floor by floor, including relocating the cosmetics section to a temporary first-floor space in 2026, with an investment exceeding one billion yen in that year alone, signaling a serious long-term commitment to modernizing the store rather than a quick cosmetic refresh. Visitors during this transition period should expect some floors to look different from a typical finished department store layout, though the store continues normal operations throughout.

Its location directly at the Kamiyacho crossing puts it at one of the busiest intersections in central Hiroshima, within easy reach of Hondori, Hiroshima PARCO, and the Peace Memorial Park area, making it simple to combine with a broader day of sightseeing and shopping in the city center.
