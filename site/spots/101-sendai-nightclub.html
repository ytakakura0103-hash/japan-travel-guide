101 SENDAI

Sendai

101 SENDAI opened in Kokubuncho in July 2024 and is billed as the largest nightclub in the entire Tohoku region, a notable claim given how much smaller Sendai's club scene is compared to Tokyo, Osaka, or Nagoya. Its scale gives it room to run a broad, mainstream booking policy spanning EDM and current chart hits, aimed at a general night-out crowd rather than a niche electronic-music audience.

Being the region's largest venue of its kind, it draws visitors from across Tohoku as well as Sendai locals, giving it a slightly more regional pull than most of Kokubuncho's smaller, neighborhood-oriented bars and clubs. Its central Kokubuncho location puts it within easy walking distance of the district's yokocho alleys and its dense concentration of other bars and restaurants.

As Tohoku's largest club, it's a natural anchor point for a Kokubuncho night out built specifically around dancing and a big-venue atmosphere, rather than the smaller-scale bar-hopping that defines much of the rest of the district. Checking the current event calendar before visiting is worthwhile, since guest DJ nights and cover charges vary.

Kokubuncho, Aoba Ward, Sendai
