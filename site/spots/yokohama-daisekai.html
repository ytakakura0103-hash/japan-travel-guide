Yokohama Daisekai

Yokohama

Daisekai leans fully into a retro, theatrical version of old Shanghai, with several floors built around that theme rather than a single attraction. The ground floors are largely food courts and souvenir stalls selling Chinatown staples like steamed buns and sweets, while upper floors house a trick-art or optical-illusion exhibit and a costume photo studio where visitors can rent qipao or other period clothing for portraits against elaborate sets.

It is a more curated, indoor experience than wandering Chinatown's open streets, which makes it a useful option on a hot or rainy day, or for families with kids who want a slower, air-conditioned break from the crowds outside. The costume photo studio in particular draws a steady stream of visitors looking for a souvenir portrait, and booking or arriving earlier in the day tends to mean a shorter wait for a session.

Because it sits within Chinatown's main grid, it is easy to combine with temple visits and food stops nearby, though it is worth noting that some of the upper-floor attractions charge separate admission on top of any general entry, so budgeting for the specific activities you want in advance avoids surprises.
