Local Pick

Furano Wine Factory

Hokkaido

Furano Winery is a municipally operated wine facility built into the hillside overlooking the Furano basin, and unlike many tourist-facing wineries, it grew out of a genuine regional effort starting in the 1970s to diversify the area's agriculture beyond its more famous produce. Self-guided routes let visitors walk through parts of the production and aging cellar for free, ending at a tasting counter where a selection of the winery's own bottles is available to sample.

The surrounding grounds include vineyard rows visible from the building and a distillery section producing grape spirits alongside the wine operation, plus a shop selling bottles that, in many cases, aren't distributed much beyond Hokkaido. The hillside setting means there's also a decent view back over Furano town and the valley's farmland patchwork.

It sits a few kilometers from Furano Station, which keeps it out of the main pedestrian tourist flow that concentrates around the station and Ningle Terrace, so it tends to draw a calmer, slightly more wine-focused crowd rather than general sightseers.

Harvest season in early autumn is when the vineyard itself looks its best, but the tasting room and cellar tour operate year-round, making it a reasonable stop regardless of when you're passing through Furano.

Shimizuyama, Furano, Hokkaido 076-0048

About 2.5 hours from Sapporo to JR Furano Station, then about a 10-minute taxi ride or a seasonal town shuttle bus to the winery.
