Fufu no Yu

Kyoto

Arashiyama draws some of the heaviest foot traffic of any district in Kyoto, between the Bamboo Grove, Togetsukyo Bridge, and the temple circuit around Tenryuji, and Fufu no Yu opened as the district's first day-use hot spring facility specifically to give visitors a way to recover from that walking without leaving the area. Its open-air bath looks out over a landscaped garden setting, a smaller-scale, more intimate version of the onsen experience found at larger resort-style facilities elsewhere in the Kansai region.

As a day-use facility rather than a ryokan, there's no need to book an overnight stay to use it, making it a practical stop to slot into an afternoon or evening in Arashiyama rather than requiring a dedicated trip. Standard onsen etiquette applies, including washing thoroughly before entering the bath and any tattoo policies the facility may have in place, worth checking in advance if relevant.

Given its central Arashiyama location, it works well as a late-afternoon stop after the district's main sights, timed to catch the garden view as the light softens toward evening. Towels and basic toiletries are typically available for rent or purchase on-site, so it's not necessary to arrive with a full onsen kit.
