Rairaikyo Gorge

Sendai

This roughly one-kilometer trail hugs a cliff face about twenty meters above the Natori River, winding past unusually shaped, water-carved rock formations and small shaded groves. One naturally eroded rock along the path resembles a heart and has picked up a reputation among locals as a minor good-luck stop for couples, though most visitors are simply there for the scenery.\n\nThe trail is free, never closes, and has multiple entry and exit points, so a full there-and-back walk takes about forty to fifty minutes, though it's easy to shorten. It's an easy add-on to a visit to Akiu Otaki waterfall nearby.\n\nCompared to the falls, which sit right off the main road, this gorge trail is easy to miss and consequently draws far fewer visitors, even during Akiu Onsen's busier weekends. Autumn is the standout season, when the gorge's foliage reflects in the river below, but the trail is pleasant in any season given its constant shade.

Akiu-machi area, Taihaku-ku, Sendai, Miyagi

About 40 minutes by bus from Sendai Station West Exit toward Akiu Onsen, alighting at the Nozokibashi bus stop.
