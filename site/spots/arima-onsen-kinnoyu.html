Arima Onsen Kin-no-yu (Golden Spring public bathhouse)

Kobe

Kin-no-yu is the more famous of Arima's two public bathhouses, built specifically to give day visitors access to the town's distinctive iron-and-salt-rich spring water, which oxidizes to a deep reddish-brown color often described locally as gold-colored, hence the name. The water is considered good for relieving fatigue and improving circulation in the local tradition, and simply soaking here for a while is the easiest way to experience Arima's signature spring without booking an overnight ryokan stay.

The bathhouse is compact, with separate facilities for men and women, and a visit including changing and soaking generally takes somewhere around an hour depending on how long you linger in the water. There is also a small foot bath just outside the building, free to use, for anyone who wants a quick taste of the spring water without a full bath.

Because it is the more well-known of the two public bathhouses and sits right on Arima's main street, Kin-no-yu tends to be busier than its counterpart, particularly on weekend afternoons; a morning visit right after opening is the more comfortable option if avoiding a wait matters.

833 Arimacho, Kita-ku, Kobe
