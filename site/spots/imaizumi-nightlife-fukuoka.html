Local Pick

Imaizumi (evening)

Fukuoka

Imaizumi sits just beside Daimyo, already covered elsewhere in this guide for its dense backstreet bar scene, but has carved out its own identity as something of a "second bar" district, the kind of place locals move on to after starting an evening elsewhere. Its bars tend to be a notch more stylish and design-conscious than Daimyo's more casual backstreet spots, without tipping into the exclusivity of Fukuoka's more upscale nightlife pockets.

The district's standout venue is evoL, a hybrid livehouse and club with a capacity of around 460 that hosts a genuinely wide range of programming, from touring bands to dedicated club nights, including a recurring K-pop-focused event that's built a loyal following. That range gives Imaizumi more of a live-music dimension than most of central Fukuoka's nightlife areas, which lean more toward bars and yatai than live venues.

Its location between Daimyo, Yakuin, and Tenjin makes it easy to fold into a broader night out across several districts, moving from dinner in one area to drinks in Imaizumi and on to Tenjin's larger clubs if the night calls for it. It rewards a bit of wandering rather than a fixed plan, in keeping with its role as a stop along the way rather than a single destination.

Imaizumi, Chuo Ward, Fukuoka

Open in Google Maps

Adjacent to Daimyo and Yakuin, a short walk from Tenjin Station or Yakuin Station (Nishitetsu Tenjin Omuta Line).
