Sunshine Sakae Ferris Wheel

Nagoya

Rising from the roof of a shopping and entertainment complex in the middle of Sakae, this Ferris wheel is modest in scale compared with dedicated theme-park attractions, but that's part of its charm — a quick, inexpensive ride that puts you above the neon signage and traffic of downtown for a few minutes without requiring a special trip out of the city center. The complex below houses shops, an arcade, and dining options if you want to extend the stop.

A single ride is inexpensive and takes only a few minutes, making it an easy add-on rather than a planned centerpiece of a visit.

Evenings are clearly the best time to ride, when the surrounding Sakae skyline lights up, and this naturally means slightly longer queues on weekend nights than on a weekday evening. It's a good option for families with younger kids who might find Midland Square's open-air deck or a taller observation tower intimidating, given the shorter, gentler ride here.
