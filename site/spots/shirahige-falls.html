Shirahige Falls

Sapporo

Shirahige Falls drops through a forested gorge just outside Shirogane Onsen, feeding into the same mineral-laden river system that gives the nearby Blue Pond its color. The falls themselves carry that same pale turquoise tint at their base, especially visible from the roadside bridge that most visitors use as the viewing point, which makes the short stop worth combining with the Blue Pond rather than skipping.

There's no hiking involved; a small parking area and a bridge over the gorge put the falls within a couple of minutes' walk, which makes it one of the easiest scenic stops in the Biei area for visitors without much time or mobility for longer trails. A public footbath sits nearby using hot spring water from the Shirogane area, giving the option to rest for a few minutes with your feet warm even in colder months.

Since there's no train service to Shirogane Onsen, most visitors arrive by rental car or the limited seasonal Dohoku Bus service, which naturally caps the number of people passing through compared to more accessible Biei stops, and the falls rarely feel crowded even when the Blue Pond parking lot nearby is full.

Winter brings partial ice formation around the falls with illumination some evenings, while the color is most vivid in the clearer light of spring through autumn.

Shirogane, Biei-cho, Kamikawa-gun, Hokkaido 071-0235

About 2.5 hours from Sapporo, roughly 2 hours to Biei/Asahikawa by train plus a bus or taxi transfer; the Dohoku Bus toward Blue Pond/Shirogane Onsen runs from Biei Station and stops near the falls (about 30-35 minutes, limited daily departures).
