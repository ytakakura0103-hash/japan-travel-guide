Local Pick

BankART KAIKO

Yokohama

Part of a longer-running Yokohama art initiative that has moved between several converted industrial buildings over the years, this location occupies part of a former raw-silk warehouse, the 1926-built Teisan Soko, keeping much of the building's brick and structural character visible around the exhibitions. The result is a space that feels closer to an artist-run project than a conventional municipal museum, with a rotating program of contemporary art, performance, and design exhibitions that changes more frequently and unpredictably than the fixed collection at the Yokohama Museum of Art.

Because the space operates on a smaller budget and profile than the city's flagship museums, current exhibitions and opening days are worth checking before visiting, since programming can be intermittent between shows. Admission is often low-cost or free for certain exhibitions, which fits the informal, experimental character of what is shown.

It sits a short walk from Bashamichi Station in the historic Kannai district, close to the Minato Mirai waterfront rather than near Yokohama Station, making it an easy addition to a waterfront sightseeing loop, and it draws a crowd more interested in contemporary Japanese art and design than typical sightseers, which keeps it consistently uncrowded even during an active exhibition.

5-57-2 Kitanakadori, Naka-ku, Yokohama

Directly adjacent to Minatomirai Line Bashamichi Station (about a 1-minute walk), or about 7 minutes on foot from Sakuragicho Station
