Akiu Onsen

Sendai

Akiu was one of the hot spring towns favored by the Date lords as a retreat from castle life, and it has kept that lower-key, restorative character ever since, a cluster of ryokan inns and bathhouses set along the Natori River gorge rather than a dense resort strip. Its water is generally milder in mineral character than some of the more sulfurous springs elsewhere in Miyagi, which some bathers find more comfortable for longer soaks.

Most travelers who aren't staying overnight visit for a day-use bath, higaeri onsen, offered by several of the ryokan for a set time slot and a modest fee, a good way to experience a proper Japanese hot spring without booking a room. The town itself is small and easy to walk, with the winery, craft village, and Rairaikyo Gorge all within a short distance, making it easy to build a relaxed half or full day around the onsen as a base.

Because Akiu is closer to Sendai than Naruko or Zao, it can get moderate weekend traffic from city residents on a day trip; a weekday visit is noticeably quieter and gives more flexibility on day-bath time slots. The surrounding hills carry good autumn color in November, visible from some of the outdoor bath areas.

Akiu-machi Yumoto, Taihaku-ku, Sendai, Miyagi
