Yokohama Marine Tower

Yokohama

Built in the early 1960s to mark the centennial of the port's opening, this tower once held a claim to being among the tallest lighthouse structures in the world, though its lighthouse function has since been retired. It has closed for renovation more than once over the decades, most recently reopening after a multi-year closure, so its current operating hours and observation deck access are worth a quick check before visiting, since they have shifted with each reopening.

When open, the deck gives a lower, closer-in view of the harbor than the Landmark Tower, taking in Yamashita Park, the Hikawa Maru, and the bay bridge in one sweep. In the evening the tower itself is lit in changing colors, making it a recognizable part of the nighttime skyline even for visitors who do not go up.

Because it sits right at the edge of Yamashita Park, it is an easy add-on rather than a separate trip, and it tends to be quieter than the Landmark Tower's observation floor since fewer visitors specifically seek it out. Sunset is generally considered the best time for the view, catching both the daylight harbor and the transition into the evening lights.

15 Yamashita-cho, Naka-ku, Yokohama

Open in Google Maps

5-minute walk from Motomachi-Chukagai Station, adjacent to Yamashita Park
