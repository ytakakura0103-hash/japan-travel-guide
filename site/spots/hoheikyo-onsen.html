Hoheikyo Onsen

Sapporo

Hoheikyo Onsen sits further up the valley beyond Jozankei, in a spot that feels considerably more remote despite still being within Sapporo's municipal limits. The baths are fed directly from the source with a continuous flow rather than recirculated water, and the outdoor bath looks out over forested hillside rather than a manicured garden, giving it a rougher, more natural feel than many of the resort-style baths closer to town.

The facility's odd but well-loved detail is its restaurant, which serves a widely recommended Indian curry alongside the more typical onsen-facility food, a combination that has made the spot something of a local secret even among Sapporo residents who haven't made the trip out. Most transactions, including admission and food, are cash only, so it's worth bringing yen rather than relying on cards.

Because the direct bus service is limited and a round trip effectively takes most of an afternoon, it draws almost no casual tourist traffic, and the baths tend to be occupied mostly by locals rather than travelers, even on weekends.

Late autumn, when the surrounding hillside turns color and the air is cold enough to make the outdoor bath feel dramatic, is a particularly good time to visit, while summer keeps the forest green and the water pleasantly cool by contrast.

Jozankei area, Minami-ku, Sapporo, Hokkaido

About 60-70 minutes from Sapporo Station via the reservation-based Kappa Liner bus, or the Jotetsu bus bound for Hoheikyo Onsen from Sapporo Station Bus Terminal, which runs to the end of the line.
