Aichi Prefectural Government Office

Nagoya

Built a few years after its neighbor, City Hall, the prefectural office follows the same Imperial Crown Style approach of a Western-style building crowned with a Japanese tiled roof, and the two structures were clearly designed to relate to one another visually as well as functionally. The roofline is often read as a deliberate nod to nearby Nagoya Castle, tying the seat of prefectural government back to the historic symbol of the city.

Like City Hall, it remains an active government building, so viewing is limited to the exterior, which takes only a few minutes but rewards a slow look at the detailing around the entrance and roofline.

Because both buildings sit on the walk between Nagoya Castle and the surrounding park, they're easy to fold into a castle visit without needing a separate trip, and there's no particular time of day that matters much for viewing an exterior like this beyond avoiding harsh midday light for photos.

3-1-2 Sannomaru, Naka-ku, Nagoya, Aichi
