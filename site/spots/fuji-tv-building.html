Fuji TV Building

Tokyo

The Fuji TV Building, headquarters of the Fuji Television network, is one of Tokyo's more unusual pieces of architecture, designed by Kenzo Tange with a distinctive titanium sphere, roughly 32 meters across, suspended in the gap between two rectangular towers. The building's exposed structural framework and elevated walkways give it a deliberately industrial, almost mechanical look that stood out when it was completed in the 1990s and still does today against the more conventional towers around it in Odaiba.

Inside the sphere is an observation deck offering views out over Odaiba, Tokyo Bay, and the Rainbow Bridge, reached by a dedicated elevator from the building's lower levels; admission is charged for the observation deck, though it's generally inexpensive relative to Tokyo's other major viewpoints. Lower floors of the building also have exhibits and shop space related to the network's television programs and characters, of more interest to visitors familiar with Japanese TV than to first-time visitors.

The observation deck can be covered in well under an hour, making it an easy add-on to a broader day exploring Odaiba's waterfront attractions rather than a destination in its own right. It's a good indoor option if the weather turns, and the view is particularly pleasant in the late afternoon as the light over the bay softens ahead of sunset.

2-4-8 Daiba, Minato City, Tokyo
