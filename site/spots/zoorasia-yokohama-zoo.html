Zoorasia (Yokohama Zoological Gardens)

Yokohama

Zoorasia organizes its large grounds by world region rather than by animal type, so a walk through the park moves from a Japanese satoyama countryside zone into recreated savannas, subarctic forests, and Central Asian highlands, each with landscaping designed to evoke the actual habitat rather than a standard row of enclosures. The scale is considerably bigger than the free Nogeyama Zoo closer to central Yokohama, and an internal shuttle bus service helps visitors cover the distance between the farthest zones.

Recent animal arrivals have included species like Sumatran tigers and Siamese langurs as part of the zoo's active role in international breeding and conservation programs, giving it a somewhat more serious conservation identity than a typical city zoo. Some exhibits close periodically for animal health management or facility maintenance, so checking current exhibit status before a visit focused on a specific animal is worthwhile.

Because it sits out in Asahi Ward, well outside the central sightseeing zone most visitors stick to, Zoorasia sees relatively few foreign tourists despite being large and genuinely well designed, making it feel more like a full day out with Yokohama families than a stop on an international visitor's checklist. In summer, extended evening hours for a nighttime program let you see nocturnal animal behavior you would otherwise miss.

1175-1 Kamishirane-cho, Asahi-ku, Yokohama

About 18 minutes by bus from JR Yokohama Line/Yokohama Municipal Subway Nakayama Station, or about 15 minutes by bus from Sotetsu Line Mitsukyo Station or Tsurugamine Station.
