Solaniwa Onsen OSAKA BAY TOWER

Osaka

This bayside hot-spring facility packs nine different baths, including several outdoor tubs with views over Osaka's harbour, into a single building alongside saunas, a resting lounge and a food court, aiming to give visitors a full afternoon or evening's worth of relaxation rather than a quick soak. The baths draw on both natural hot-spring water and artificially heated mineral baths, so the mix includes options beyond a typical sento, from carbonated baths to cold plunge pools for sauna-goers practising the alternating hot-cold routine.

A visit here comfortably fills two to three hours, and the facility is designed for a half-day stay, with rental clothes, towels and lockers generally available for visitors arriving without their own gear. As with most Japanese bathing facilities, visible tattoos may be restricted in shared baths, so it's worth checking current policy before visiting if that applies.

Admission is charged per entry, often with different rates for weekday and weekend visits, and additional charges apply for towel and clothing rental if needed. Evenings and weekends tend to be busiest, particularly after work hours on weekdays and throughout the day on weekends, so a weekday afternoon visit offers a quieter experience with more space in the outdoor baths. The building sits near Osaka Bay, a short walk from Bentencho Station, and pairs well with a visit to the nearby aquarium and Tempozan area earlier in the day, saving the baths for a relaxing end to the itinerary.

1-2-3 Benten, Minato Ward, Osaka
