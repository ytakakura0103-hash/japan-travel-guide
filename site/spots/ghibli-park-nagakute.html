Ghibli Park

Nagoya

Ghibli Park opened its first sections on November 1, 2022, built not from scratch but woven into the existing grounds of Expo 2005 Aichi Commemorative Park, also known as Moricoro Park, the site of the 2005 World Expo. That decision shapes the entire experience: rather than a conventional theme park with rides queued behind gates, Ghibli Park spreads its five themed areas across genuine forest and open parkland, opened in phases through 2022, 2023 and 2024.\n\nDondoko Forest, one of the first areas to open, centers on Satsuki and Mei's House from My Neighbor Totoro, tucked into real greenery with walking paths leading up a hillside to a large Totoro-shaped play structure. Ghibli's Grand Warehouse and Hill of Youth followed as part of the same initial phase, with Mononoke Village arriving in November 2023 and the Valley of Witches, inspired by Kiki's Delivery Service, completing the park's five areas in March 2024.\n\nBecause the park sits within a much larger public park, and because most areas require timed tickets booked well in advance, visitors should plan their entry slots before arriving rather than expecting walk-up access.

1533-1 Iwasakicho, Nagakute, Aichi
