Tokyo Joypolis

Tokyo

Tokyo Joypolis is an indoor entertainment center in Odaiba run by Sega, blending arcade games with small-scale theme-park rides across several floors of a single building, an increasingly rare combination in a city where most large arcades have scaled back on physical rides. The lineup leans heavily on virtual reality attractions and motion-based rides built around Sega's own game properties, alongside rhythm games, prize-catcher machines, and more conventional arcade cabinets, giving it a broader range of activities than a standalone arcade.

Admission is typically structured as an entry fee plus either pay-per-ride tickets or an unlimited-ride pass, and which option makes sense depends on how many attractions you plan to try; for a short visit focused on just one or two headline rides, single tickets may work out cheaper than a pass. Because it's entirely indoor and multi-storey, it's a practical option on a rainy or very hot day in Odaiba when outdoor sightseeing loses its appeal.

The venue tends to draw a mix of teenagers and young adults, and lines for the most popular VR attractions can build up on weekend afternoons and during school holidays. A couple of hours is enough to try a handful of attractions; dedicated arcade and ride fans can easily spend half a day. It sits within walking distance of Odaiba's other attractions, making it easy to combine with a broader day in the area.
