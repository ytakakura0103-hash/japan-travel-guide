Kishamichi Promenade

Yokohama

Before it was a footpath, this elevated route carried freight trains along a branch of the Yokohama Rinko Line, a cargo railway that opened in 1911 to connect the freight yards near Sakuragicho with the piers of Yokohama Port. When the line was decommissioned in 1986, the city preserved sections of the original early 20th-century rails, girders, and bridges and turned the embankment into a walkway, so you are literally tracing century-old train tracks on foot between Sakuragicho Station and the Minato Mirai waterfront.

It is a genuinely practical route as much as a historical one: it cuts out a longer street-level walk and drops you close to Nippon Maru Memorial Park and the Cosmo World area, with old rail fixtures and interpretive signs along the way for anyone curious enough to slow down and read them. Because most people treat it purely as a shortcut, it stays uncrowded even when the waterfront below is packed.

Evening is a good time to cross, when the elevated position gives a clear view over the harbor lights without the crowds gathered at Cosmo World or the Red Brick Warehouse plaza. It is a small detail in a district full of bigger attractions, but it rewards visitors who like their sightseeing layered with a bit of railway history.
