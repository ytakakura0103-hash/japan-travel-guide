Toyosu Market

Tokyo

Toyosu Market opened in 2018 as the replacement for the old Tsukiji wholesale market, consolidating Tokyo's central fish, produce, and flower trading into a large, modern facility on reclaimed land in Koto ward. The most famous activity here is the pre-dawn tuna auction, where whole frozen tuna are inspected and bid on by wholesalers; the market has built an elevated glass-walled public walkway specifically so visitors can watch the auction floor from above without interrupting the trading itself, a more orderly but also more distanced experience than the old Tsukiji setup allowed.

Beyond the auction, the market complex includes several floors with restaurants and shops, many run by vendors who also supply the trade, so it's possible to eat sushi and other seafood dishes made with ingredients sourced just meters away. Public access to the auction viewing area is limited to a set window in the early morning, and building access more broadly follows the market's own schedule rather than typical shopping hours, so checking current visiting hours in advance is worth doing.

Because the market is spread across large, somewhat utilitarian buildings connected by walkways, plan for some walking between sections. An early start, arriving well before the auction viewing window closes, gives the best chance of seeing it; visitors who prefer to sleep in can still enjoy the food floors later in the morning, though some of the best-known sushi counters develop lines as the morning goes on.
