WARP Shinjuku

Tokyo

WARP Shinjuku sits in the basement levels of a Kabukicho building and runs on a distinctive format: the main floor's music genre and overall concept switches at midnight, so a night that starts on one style of event effectively "warps" into a different one partway through, without requiring a new cover charge or a walk to another venue. Multiple floors beyond the main room let different genres run simultaneously for anyone who wants to move between rooms rather than commit to one sound for the night.

Being inside Kabukicho puts it in the middle of Tokyo's most famous (and most touristed) nightlife district, so it draws a mixed crowd of Tokyo clubgoers, students, and visiting tourists rather than skewing toward one scene in particular. That also means the walk there passes through Kabukicho's dense signage and touting activity, worth being aware of using the same general caution as elsewhere in the district: ignore unsolicited offers from people on the street and go straight to the venue.

Hours run late into the morning, typical for Tokyo clubs, and the specific genre lineup for both the pre- and post-midnight slots is posted on the club's own site and social media in advance, worth checking if a particular sound is the reason for the visit.

Dai-ni Toa Kaikan Building B1/B2, 1-21-1 Kabukicho, Shinjuku City, Tokyo 160-0021

Open in Google Maps

About 2 minutes on foot from Seibu-Shinjuku Station's south exit.
