Hiroshima Castle Sannomaru

Hiroshima

Hiroshima Castle Sannomaru opened its first phase in March 2025 on the castle's south side, adding a commercial zone that gives the historic site a genuine dining and shopping dimension it previously lacked. The lineup includes a restaurant specializing in unagi, a curated Hiroshima specialty goods shop called Hiroshima IPPIN, and an archery experience that lets visitors try a version of the traditional skill in a modern setting.

The development connects via pedestrian deck to Hiroshima Gate Park and the city's new stadium, EDION Peace Wing Hiroshima, tying together several of central Hiroshima's newer developments into a single walkable route. That connectivity makes it easy to combine a castle visit with a broader loop through the surrounding park and stadium district rather than treating each as a separate destination.

For visitors who've already seen Hiroshima Castle's main keep and grounds, Sannomiya adds a reason to extend the visit with a meal or some shopping, rounding out what was previously a purely historical sightseeing stop into a fuller half-day outing.
