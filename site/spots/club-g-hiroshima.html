CLUB G

Hiroshima

CLUB G has run in Nagarekawa since December 2006, focused specifically on hip-hop and Black music rather than the broader, more mainstream programming found at some of the district's other clubs. That genre focus has given it a loyal, specific following among Hiroshima's hip-hop scene over nearly two decades of operation, a genuine rarity for a smaller regional club.

Unlike venues that run nightly, CLUB G operates only on Friday and Saturday nights, concentrating its programming into the weekend rather than spreading thin across the week. That schedule is worth knowing before planning a visit, since showing up on a weeknight expecting it to be open will lead to disappointment.

Its Nagarekawa location keeps it within easy reach of the district's other bars and clubs, making it simple to combine with a broader weekend night out. For visitors specifically interested in hip-hop and Black music culture rather than general EDM programming, it's one of the more genre-focused options in Hiroshima's club scene.

Nagarekawa, Naka Ward, Hiroshima
