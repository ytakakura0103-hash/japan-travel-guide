Higashiyama Sky Tower

Nagoya

Built in 1989 to mark the hundredth anniversary of Nagoya's founding, the Higashiyama Sky Tower stands on top of an already elevated hill within the Higashiyama Zoo and Botanical Gardens, putting its observation deck roughly 180 meters above sea level even though the tower structure itself rises about 100 meters. On a clear day the deck offers views across the Nagoya TV Tower and Nagoya Dome, out toward Mount Ontake on the Gifu border, and as far as the Suzuka mountains in Mie, a panorama that has earned the tower recognition as one of Japan's top night-view destinations.

A restaurant on the uppermost floor serves casual Italian and French food for lunch and a more formal menu in the evening, making the tower a workable dinner-with-a-view option rather than just a daytime lookout. Because it sits inside the wider Higashiyama Park complex, which also holds Nagoya's main zoo and botanical garden, a visit here is easy to fold into a longer day in the area rather than a special trip on its own.

The tower gets a fraction of the visitor numbers of Nagoya's more famous downtown observation points, largely because it requires a subway ride out to Higashiyama Koen rather than a stop in the city center.

3-70 Higashiyama-motomachi, Chikusa-ku, Nagoya, Aichi

Open in Google Maps

About a 15-minute walk from Exit 3 of Higashiyama Koen Station on the Higashiyama subway line, roughly 20 minutes from Nagoya Station.
