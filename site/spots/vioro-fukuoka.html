VIORO

Fukuoka

VIORO takes its name from a blend of the Italian words for flower and truth, and positions itself as a fashion and lifestyle building aimed specifically at shoppers in their late twenties and thirties, a slightly older, more established customer than the youth-focused crowd at nearby Fukuoka PARCO. Spread across basement two to the seventh floor, it holds roughly sixty shops and nine restaurants, with anchor tenants like United Arrows and Repetto reflecting its more considered, adult fashion positioning.

Its seventh floor is worth knowing about specifically: a restaurant and bar space there offers views over the Tenjin skyline, giving the building a rooftop-adjacent dining option that most fashion buildings in the district don't offer. It's a reasonable stop for a meal with a view without needing to seek out a dedicated rooftop bar elsewhere in the city.

Its central Tenjin location puts it within easy reach of the district's other major shopping stops, and its more mature fashion positioning makes it a useful alternative for visitors who find PARCO's younger-skewing lineup less relevant to their own style.

Tenjin, Chuo Ward, Fukuoka
