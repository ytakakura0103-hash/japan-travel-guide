Hanazono Rugby Stadium

Osaka

Hanazono holds a specific, well-earned place in Japanese sporting history as the birthplace of rugby in Japan, and the stadium in Higashi-Osaka remains most closely associated today with the national high school rugby tournament held each winter around New Year, a fixture that draws passionate crowds and has launched the careers of many players who went on to the national team. Outside tournament season, the stadium also hosts club and university matches, giving visitors interested in rugby a chance to see a match in a genuinely historic venue rather than a purpose-built modern arena.

For visitors without a specific match to attend, the stadium itself and its surroundings carry a low-key, unpolished charm connected to the sport's deep local roots, though there isn't a great deal to see outside of match days beyond the exterior and general atmosphere of the neighborhood. Checking the current match or tournament schedule before planning a visit is essential, since the experience differs enormously between a quiet non-match day and the packed atmosphere of the New Year tournament.

The stadium sits a little outside central Osaka in Higashi-Osaka, so it's best suited to dedicated rugby fans or those specifically planning a trip around the winter tournament rather than a casual add-on to central Osaka sightseeing. Winter tournament dates draw the heaviest crowds by a wide margin, while off-season matches are generally much easier to get tickets for.
