Tashirojima (Cat Island)

Sendai

Tashirojima earned its 'Cat Island' nickname honestly: fishermen here traditionally kept cats to protect silkworm cocoons and fishing nets from rodents, came to see them as bringers of good luck and weather warnings, and over generations the cat population grew to far outnumber the island's small, aging community of full-time residents. Dogs are not allowed on the island, a rule maintained specifically to keep the cats comfortable and free-roaming.\n\nA small shrine partway across the island, Neko Jinja (Cat Shrine), is dedicated to the animals and marks something like the spiritual center of the island's identity. Beyond the cats themselves, Tashirojima has a handful of cat-shaped guesthouses and a slow, low-key fishing-village pace that makes it feel a world away from Sendai, despite being reachable in a single day.\n\nThe ferry from Ishinomaki takes roughly forty-five minutes to an hour each way and runs only a few times a day, so planning around the return schedule matters; ferries are also sometimes cancelled in rough weather, so building in flexibility helps. There are very few restaurants on the island, so bringing food and water is wise. It's a genuinely uncrowded destination outside of the busiest holiday weekends, and any weekday visit is likely to feel close to having the island to yourself.
