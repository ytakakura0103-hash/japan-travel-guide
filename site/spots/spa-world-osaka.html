Spa World

Osaka

Spa World is less a single bathhouse than a bathing theme park, spread across several floors above Shinsekai. The concept is simple but effective: the men's and women's bathing floors are switched between an "Asian" zone (Bali, Turkey, ancient Rome-style pools) and a "European" zone (Spanish and Greek-inspired baths) on a rotating schedule, so a return visit later can mean an entirely different set of tubs. Beyond the themed floors there are saunas, a relaxation area with reclining chairs, and, upstairs, a water slide and pool section aimed squarely at families with children, which operates separately from the adult bathing floors.

Plan on spending at least half a day here if you want to work through both the baths and the water park; a couple of hours is enough just for a soak. Entry covers the baths, with extra charges for the water park, sauna add-ons, and towel or robe rental, so budget a bit more than the base admission suggests. Weekday afternoons tend to be calmer than weekend evenings, when the facility fills with local families and groups.

As with any Japanese onsen-style facility, tattoos are traditionally frowned upon, and Spa World has specific rules about this that are worth checking before a visit. Lockers are provided for streetwear, and casual swimwear is required in the water park section but not in the bathing floors themselves, where the usual no-clothing bathing etiquette applies.

3-4-24 Ebisuhigashi, Naniwa Ward, Osaka
