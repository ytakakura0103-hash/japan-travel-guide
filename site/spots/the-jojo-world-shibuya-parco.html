THE JOJO WORLD

Tokyo

THE JOJO WORLD opened in July 2025 on the sixth floor of Shibuya PARCO as the first permanent, officially licensed experiential shop dedicated to Hirohiko Araki's long-running manga and anime series JoJo's Bizarre Adventure. Rather than functioning purely as a merchandise store, it's built around small interactive elements, mini-games and photo-friendly set pieces themed around the series' distinctive art style and characters, alongside a food and drink menu that leans into the show's visual language.

Admission is free, and the space is compact at around 264 square meters, so a visit doesn't require a long time commitment even for non-fans passing through PARCO for other reasons. For dedicated fans, the appeal is largely in store-exclusive merchandise not available elsewhere, making it as much a specialty shopping stop as an attraction.

Because it sits inside Shibuya PARCO, a fashion and culture department store that's a worthwhile stop on its own, it combines easily with the rest of the building rather than requiring a dedicated trip. Given its niche appeal, it's a better fit for visitors who already know and follow the series than a general pop-culture attraction for casual tourists.

Shibuya PARCO 6F, 15-1 Udagawacho, Shibuya City, Tokyo

Open in Google Maps

About 5 minutes on foot from Shibuya Station.
