Zao Echo Line

Sendai

The Zao Echo Line is a toll road that winds up the Miyagi flank of Mount Zao, passing through changing bands of vegetation from lowland forest to alpine scrub as it climbs toward the famous emerald-green Okama crater lake near the summit. Driving the full route, rather than just visiting the crater parking area, is where much of the scenery actually lies.

A toll applies for the drive, and the road is only open seasonally, typically closed during the heaviest winter snow months and reopening in spring, sometimes revealing tall snow-wall corridors along the route in the early weeks after opening.

Most visitors treat the drive purely as a means to reach the Okama crater viewpoint, so simply slowing down and stopping at the smaller pull-offs along the way, including Katta Gorge's Takimidai lookout, tends to mean far fewer people. Snow corridors draw attention in late spring, while the drive is at its most colorful during autumn foliage season.
