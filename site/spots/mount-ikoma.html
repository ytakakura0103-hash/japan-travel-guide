Mount Ikoma

Osaka

Straddling the border between Osaka and Nara, Mount Ikoma rises to 642 metres and has drawn day-trippers from Osaka for over a century, historically via a cable car built in the early 20th century that remains one of the oldest in Japan still in operation. Near the summit sits Ikoma Sanjo Amusement Park, a small, old-fashioned theme park with rides geared toward families and a nostalgic, unhurried atmosphere quite different from Universal Studios Japan.

The mountain's other major draw is its night view: from viewpoints near the summit, the lights of Osaka, and on clear nights parts of Kyoto and Nara, spread out across the plain below, a view regularly ranked among the best in the Kansai region and popular for evening dates as much as tourism. The cable car ride itself takes a short while, and a full visit combining the ride, some time at the amusement park and the night view can easily fill three to four hours, more if hiking additional trails near the summit.

Weekday evenings offer a quieter, less crowded version of the night view than weekends, when the viewpoints can get busy with couples and photographers. Cable car and amusement park admission are charged separately, with combination tickets sometimes available. Because the cable car runs on a set schedule, it's worth checking the last departure time before heading up, particularly if planning to stay for the night view. Access begins at Ikoma Station, reachable directly from central Osaka by train.
