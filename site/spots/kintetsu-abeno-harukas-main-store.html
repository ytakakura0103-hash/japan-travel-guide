Kintetsu Department Store Abeno Harukas Main Store

Osaka

Occupying floors B2 through 14 of the Abeno Harukas tower, the tallest building in Japan outside Tokyo, the Kintetsu Department Store's main branch here bills itself as Japan's largest department store by both total floor area and number of items carried, with roughly 100,000 square meters of retail space stacked across the lower half of the tower. That scale is easy to underestimate from outside, since most visitors associate Abeno Harukas primarily with its observation deck higher up in the building, but the department store below is a full destination in its own right.

The restaurant floor is a particular highlight, gathering 44 restaurants and around 2,800 seats in what's marketed as Japan's largest single restaurant floor, spanning everything from casual chains to more considered dining options. Below that, the standard department store mix of fashion, cosmetics, and household goods runs across multiple floors, along with a substantial depachika food hall typical of Japan's major department stores.

Because the tower also houses the Harukas 300 observation deck covered elsewhere in this guide, a single visit can combine both: shopping and a meal in the department store's lower floors, followed by an elevator ride up to the observatory for the view. Given the building's overall scale, allowing a few hours for a combined visit is more realistic than treating either half as a quick stop.

1-1-43 Abenosuji, Abeno Ward, Osaka (Abeno Harukas, B2-14F)
