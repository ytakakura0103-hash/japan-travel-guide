Akiu Great Falls (Akiu Otaki)

Sendai

Akiu Otaki drops in a single, dramatic sheet down a rock face in the hills above Akiu Onsen, and is counted among Japan's celebrated waterfalls for its scale and setting. A short, well-maintained path leads from the parking area down to a viewing platform close enough to feel the spray on a windy day, without requiring any real hiking experience.\n\nThere is a small parking fee at the nearby lot, and the walk down and back takes roughly twenty to thirty minutes at an easy pace. It's about thirty to forty minutes from central Sendai by car or bus.\n\nArriving before mid-morning generally means beating the tour buses that stop here as part of Akiu Onsen day trips. Autumn foliage frames the falls particularly well in the surrounding hills, and the volume of water noticeably increases after rain, making the falls more dramatic but also the platform a bit more slippery underfoot.

Akiu-machi Yumoto area, Taihaku-ku, Sendai, Miyagi

About 40 minutes by bus from Sendai Station West Exit toward Akiu Onsen, then a short walk from the falls parking area.
