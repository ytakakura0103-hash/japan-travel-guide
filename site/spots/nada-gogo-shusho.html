Nada Gogo Shusho

Kobe

Nada Gogo Shusho opened in 2022 as a dedicated tasting hall for the Nada Gogo brewing district, a stretch of coastline east of central Kobe that's long been Japan's single most productive sake-producing region, home to major names covered elsewhere in this guide like Hakutsuru and Kiku-Masamune. Rather than requiring a separate visit to each individual brewery, the hall gathers roughly forty different sake varieties from all twenty-six Nada Gogo breweries under one roof, letting visitors taste widely across the district's output in a single stop.

Food pairings are available alongside the tastings, giving the space more of a proper tasting-room feel than a simple sample counter, and the venue has continued hosting sake-focused events since opening, keeping it active within Kobe's broader sake tourism scene. For visitors who don't have time to walk the full Uozaki Sake Brewery Walk covered elsewhere in this guide, or who want a curated overview before deciding which individual breweries to visit in depth, it's a useful complement to that walking route.

Its focused, single-district scope makes it a good stop specifically for sake-curious visitors, offering breadth across the region's many breweries in a way that visiting one or two individual brewery museums can't replicate.
