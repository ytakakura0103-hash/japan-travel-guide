MIYASHITA PARK

Tokyo

MIYASHITA PARK reimagines a strip of public park that has sat above Shibuya's rail lines for decades, wrapping a multi-level shopping and dining complex underneath a rooftop lawn that keeps the site's original function alive. The rooftop area includes a skate section, climbing wall, and open grass where people picnic, nap, or watch the sun go down over Shibuya's mix of low-rise streets and glass towers — a markedly calmer scene than the crossing just a few minutes away on foot. The lower floors mix international streetwear brands with casual restaurants and a food hall, plus a hotel built into the same structure.

Because the complex sits right beside Shibuya Station, it is an easy stop to fold into a day of shopping in the area, and an hour or so covers a casual look at both the shops and the rooftop lawn. Weekday afternoons are the quietest time to enjoy the grass without stepping around picnic blankets, while weekend evenings bring out skaters, groups with speakers, and a livelier, younger crowd. The rooftop is open-air, so a very hot or rainy day will thin out the lawn considerably and push people toward the covered shopping floors instead.

Families should note that some sections of the rooftop, particularly the skate and climbing areas, are more geared toward teens and adults, though the open lawn itself is comfortable for children of any age.

6-20-10 Jingumae, Shibuya City, Tokyo
