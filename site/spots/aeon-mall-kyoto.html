AEON MALL Kyoto

Kyoto

AEON MALL Kyoto sits a short walk from Kyoto Station's south exit and functions as the largest shopping mall in the central city, a genuinely different shopping experience from the historic department stores and covered arcades around Shijo-Kawaramachi. Spread across four floors, it holds roughly 130 specialty stores alongside more than fifty duty-free shops, a scale and format aimed at practical, one-stop shopping rather than the more curated, browse-focused experience of Takashimaya or Daimaru.

A cinema and more than thirty restaurants round out the complex, making it a reasonable option for a full afternoon or evening that combines shopping, a meal, and a movie without needing to move between different parts of the city. Its south-side location, opposite the main tourist-facing north side of Kyoto Station, means it draws a more mixed crowd of residents and visitors than the malls directly attached to the station's main concourse.

For visitors staying near Kyoto Station or catching a Shinkansen and looking to shop conveniently beforehand, it's one of the most straightforward options in the city, requiring no transfer or long walk from the station itself. The duty-free shopping in particular may be of interest to visitors doing a final round of purchases before departing Kyoto.

Near Kyoto Station (south side), Minami Ward, Kyoto
