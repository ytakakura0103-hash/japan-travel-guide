Yokohama Bay Quarter

Yokohama

Built along the canal on the east side of Yokohama Station, this complex mixes fashion, lifestyle, and homeware shops with restaurants that make the most of their waterside setting, including outdoor seating overlooking the canal in warmer months. Its direct connection to Yokohama Station makes it a practical stop for visitors arriving or departing by train who want to shop without a separate trip into Minato Mirai proper.

Compared to MARK IS or Queen's Square, it draws a more local, commuter-heavy crowd rather than tourists, since Yokohama Station itself is more of a transit hub than a sightseeing destination in its own right. That gives it a calmer, more everyday atmosphere, useful for a straightforward shopping stop without needing to navigate crowds built up around a specific attraction.

Evenings along the canal-facing restaurants are a pleasant, if under-the-radar, spot for a meal with water views that rival the more famous Minato Mirai waterfront, minus the crowds that gather around the Landmark Tower and Cosmo World a short distance away.

1-10 Konko-cho, Kanagawa-ku, Yokohama
