Onomichi Station Cycle Terminal

Hiroshima

Onomichi Station's new rental cycling terminal opened in October 2025, replacing and significantly expanding the previous port-side terminal to become the flagship starting point for trips along the Shimanami Kaido cycling route, already covered elsewhere in this guide. Its location directly at the station makes it the most convenient possible starting point for visitors arriving by train and heading straight onto the route.

A large-scale mural titled "ONOMICHI Creation," painted by local artist Shosin Ueda, covers part of the terminal's exterior wall, giving the building its own visual identity beyond its practical function as a bike rental hub. That mural has quickly become a photo stop in its own right, worth a look even for visitors not planning to cycle the full route.

Given its role as the primary gateway to one of Japan's most celebrated cycling routes, the terminal is worth knowing about specifically for its convenience: renting a bike here means starting the Shimanami Kaido journey immediately upon arrival, without needing to navigate to a separate rental location elsewhere in the city.
