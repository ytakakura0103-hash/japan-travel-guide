Tanukikoji Shopping Street

Hokkaido

Tanukikoji dates back to the late 19th century, making it one of Sapporo's oldest shopping streets, and its glass-roofed arcade means it's a reliable option regardless of weather, which matters a lot given how much of the year Sapporo spends under snow or rain. The mix of shops is genuinely broad: long-running kimono and stationery stores sit next to game arcades, discount drugstores, and small izakaya tucked into side alleys.

It runs roughly parallel to Odori Park a couple of blocks south, which makes it an easy detour from a day centered on the park and Susukino. Unlike the more polished malls near Sapporo Station, Tanukikoji still has some of the slightly worn, lived-in feel of an older Japanese shotengai, and prices tend to run a bit friendlier than department stores.

Evenings bring the heaviest foot traffic as the street connects naturally into the Susukino nightlife district at its eastern end, so a daytime or early-evening visit is calmer if browsing is the goal rather than dinner and drinks. There's no strong seasonal angle here since it's fully covered, which actually makes it a useful fallback plan on a rainy or heavy-snow day when outdoor sightseeing is less appealing.

Minami 2-3 Jo, Chuo-ku, Sapporo, Hokkaido

Runs between Nishi 1-chome and Nishi 8-chome, a short walk south of Odori Station via several subway exits along its length.
