Onagawa Seaside Town

Sendai

Around seventy percent of Onagawa was destroyed by the 2011 tsunami, and rather than accept a standard seawall solution, the small fishing town pushed for a more ambitious rebuild, carving into a nearby hillside to create new, higher ground to build on. The results are visible today in a modern town center connected to the station by a wide brick promenade lined with shops, small restaurants, and a compact seafood market.\n\nWalking the promenade and browsing the shops costs nothing, and a couple of hours is enough to see the main area and grab a meal of fresh local seafood.\n\nBecause Onagawa is a genuinely small town of a few thousand residents rather than a mass-market attraction, it never feels overwhelmed by visitors even during regional tourist season. An autumn saury fish festival draws a bigger local crowd than usual, otherwise it remains a fairly calm, everyday sort of stop.

Onagawa, Oshika-gun, Miyagi
