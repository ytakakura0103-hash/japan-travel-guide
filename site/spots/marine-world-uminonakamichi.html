Marine World Uminonakamichi

Fukuoka

This aquarium centers its exhibits on marine life found in the waters around Kyushu, an approach that gives it a slightly different focus from more generalist aquariums elsewhere in Japan. Tanks cover everything from the open ocean species of the Genkai Sea to smaller coastal ecosystems, and scheduled dolphin and sea lion shows run through the day, popular with families traveling with kids.

Because it's a separate facility from Uminonakamichi Seaside Park next door, despite sharing the same peninsula, it requires its own admission ticket and isn't automatically included in park entry. Combination tickets covering both are usually available, worth looking into if visiting both on the same day.

Show times are fixed, so checking the daily schedule on arrival helps in planning the rest of the visit around them. It tends to be busiest during school holidays and weekends with families; weekday mornings outside those periods are noticeably quieter, particularly for getting a good spot at the show tanks.

18-28 Saitozaki, Higashi Ward, Fukuoka
