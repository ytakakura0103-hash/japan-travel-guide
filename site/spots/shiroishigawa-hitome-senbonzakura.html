Shiroishigawa Hitome Senbonzakura

Sendai

Roughly twelve hundred cherry trees, some more than eighty years old, line both banks of the Shiroishi River for around eight kilometers, forming what's often described as one of the most impressive cherry blossom corridors in the Tohoku region. On a clear day, the trees frame views of the still snow-capped Zao mountain range beyond, and fallen petals drifting on the river create a pink, carpet-like effect.\n\nViewing is free, and given the sheer length of the route, exploring by bicycle or car makes far more sense than trying to walk the whole stretch on foot. The Ogawara Sakura Festival runs alongside the bloom each year.\n\nThe area immediately around Funaoka Station and the festival grounds gets genuinely crowded during peak bloom, but heading toward either end of the eight-kilometer stretch, away from the main festival area, generally means a much calmer, equally scenic experience. Bloom typically falls in early to mid-April, though exact timing shifts year to year with the weather.

Shiroishi River embankment, Shibata/Ogawara, Shibata-gun, Miyagi
