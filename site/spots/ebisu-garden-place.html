Local Pick

Ebisu Garden Place

Tokyo

Ebisu Garden Place occupies the former grounds of the Yebisu Beer brewery, and the plaza today keeps a noticeably calmer, more grown-up pace than the shopping streets around Ebisu Station, with a central open square, a scattering of restaurants, and two focused cultural stops rather than a dense retail crowd. The Tokyo Photographic Art Museum, Japan's major dedicated photography museum, runs rotating exhibitions in the plaza's museum building, while the nearby Yebisu Garden Place Tower includes a free observation lounge on one of its upper floors with skyline views that stretch toward Tokyo Tower and, on clear days, further beyond.

A visit to the plaza itself takes well under an hour if you're only passing through, but plan on an additional hour or more if a photography exhibition catches your interest, since the museum's shows rotate regularly and are worth checking in advance. The observation lounge is a quick, low-key alternative to Tokyo's paid observation decks, and because it draws far fewer visitors, there is rarely a wait even in the evening.

Weekday visits are consistently the quietest, and the plaza has a slightly more residential, local feel than tourist-heavy parts of the city — a reasonable stop for travelers who want a skyline view or a small art museum without the crowds that come with more famous options elsewhere in Tokyo.
