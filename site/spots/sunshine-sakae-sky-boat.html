Sunshine Sakae Sky-Boat Ferris Wheel

Nagoya

Rather than standing on the ground like most Ferris wheels, the Sky-Boat sits directly on top of the Sunshine Sakae shopping and entertainment complex, functioning as the building's unmistakable visual signature from street level. Riders board from an upper floor of the complex and circle above the rooftops of Sakae, Nagoya's densest entertainment district, with sightlines toward the Nagoya TV Tower, the greenery of Hisaya-odori Park, and the wider grid of the city center.

At night the wheel is lit in changing colors, adding to Sakae's already bright evening atmosphere and making it a popular, if slightly kitschy, photo subject from the streets below. The ride itself is short and inexpensive, more of a novelty stop than a major attraction, but the elevated, close-up view of a working entertainment district is different from what taller, more famous observation decks in the city offer.

It pairs naturally with a visit to the SKE48 Theater in the same building, or with an evening out exploring Sakae's restaurants and bars.

3-24-4 Nishiki, Naka-ku, Nagoya, Aichi
