Rinnoji Temple Garden

Sendai

Rinnoji was founded in 1441 by Date Mochimune and moved to its current spot north of central Sendai alongside the rest of the Date family in the early 1600s. Beyond the modest main hall lies the real draw: a strolling garden built around a central koi pond, with a three-story pagoda rising at one end and pine trees carefully shaped into rope-wrapped cones that look almost sculptural.\n\nAdmission is a modest few hundred yen, and most visitors spend thirty to forty-five minutes wandering the garden paths. It's reachable via the JR Senzan Line to Kitayama Station, followed by about a ten-minute walk.\n\nBecause the temple sits away from the castle-and-Zuihoden circuit that most first-time visitors follow, the garden rarely feels crowded even during peak color seasons. The planting is deliberately seasonal: azaleas in spring, water lilies through summer, and some of the city's best autumn foliage reflected in the pond by late October or early November.

Kitayama area, Aoba-ku, Sendai, Miyagi
