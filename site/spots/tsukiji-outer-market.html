Tsukiji Outer Market Food Tour

Tokyo

When Tokyo's central wholesale fish market moved to Toyosu in 2018, the outer market around Tsukiji stayed behind, and it remains one of the city's best places to eat and shop for food. Narrow lanes are packed with seafood stalls, produce sellers, dried goods shops, and long-established knife makers, alongside stand-up counters serving grilled skewers, tamagoyaki (rolled omelet) on a stick, and fresh sashimi bowls. It's less about the wholesale auction, which now happens at Toyosu, and more about grazing your way through a dense, working market district that still supplies many of the city's restaurants.

Morning is the best time to visit, both because the market is livelier then and because a number of stalls sell out of their best items or close by early afternoon. Some shops close on Sundays and irregular holidays, so it's worth checking before making a special trip on those days. Weekday mornings tend to be calmer than weekend mornings, when the lanes can get tightly packed with visitors.

A couple of hours is enough to eat and browse, though food lovers can easily spend longer. Prices vary stall by stall, and cash is still useful even though more shops now accept cards. Combining a visit with nearby Hamarikyu Gardens or a walk along the Sumida River makes for an easy half-day itinerary.
