Hirose River Green Belt

Sendai

The Hirose River curls around central Sendai in a wide arc, and long stretches of its banks are lined with a green belt of trees and paved or dirt paths used daily by local joggers, cyclists, and dog walkers. It's not a single attraction so much as a free, flexible way to see a quieter side of the city, with views back toward the castle hill and downtown skyline from several points.

There's no cost and no fixed route; you can walk for ten minutes or a couple of hours depending on how far you follow the water. Access points are scattered throughout downtown, so it's easy to join or leave the path near almost any other stop on a Sendai itinerary.

Because it's used mainly by residents rather than visitors, it stays pleasantly uncrowded even close to the station. Autumn brings good foliage color along the banks, and the riverside path is a nice way to cool down after a day of sightseeing in warmer months.
