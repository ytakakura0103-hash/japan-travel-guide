Hiroshima Orizuru Tower

Hiroshima

Opened in 2016, Orizuru Tower rises from a renovated 1978 building right across the Motoyasu River from the Atomic Bomb Dome, and it was built partly to give visitors an elevated, contemporary vantage point on a part of the city otherwise defined by its wartime history. The rooftop "Hiroshima Hill" observation deck offers open-air views over Peace Memorial Park, the Dome, and the surrounding city and mountains, and is a common spot for photos that place the Dome within the wider modern skyline.

The descent from the observation floor runs through a spiral ramp called the Orizuru Spiral Slope, where a glass shaft several stories tall is gradually filled with folded paper cranes contributed by visitors, echoing the crane tradition tied to the nearby Children's Peace Monument. Visitors can fold and drop in their own crane as part of the ticketed experience, and the cranes collected are later put to community use rather than simply archived.

Lower floors house a cafe, gift shop with Hiroshima-themed goods, and rotating exhibits, making it a reasonably self-contained stop that pairs naturally with a Peace Memorial Park visit rather than competing with it.

1-2-1 Otemachi, Naka Ward, Hiroshima, Hiroshima Prefecture
