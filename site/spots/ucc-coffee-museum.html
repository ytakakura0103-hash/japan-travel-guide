UCC Coffee Museum

Kobe

Run by UCC, one of Japan's major coffee companies with roots in Kobe's port trading history, this museum traces the journey of coffee from cultivation through roasting and brewing, using historical equipment, sensory displays, and interactive exhibits rather than static panels alone. After a lengthy closure for renovation, it reopened with a substantially updated set of hands-on experience courses, including options to try roasting, blending your own beans, and even attempting 3D latte art under guidance.

Because the paid experience courses now run on a reservation basis and are limited in number each day, visitors who want to do more than just walk through the exhibits should plan and book ahead rather than assume walk-in availability. A basic visit through the exhibition areas alone takes under an hour, while adding one of the hands-on courses extends that considerably.

Given the recent, well-publicized renovation, the museum has been drawing renewed interest, so weekend slots for the popular experience courses may fill faster than weekday ones; booking as early as reservations open is the safest approach for a specific date.

6-6-2 Minatojima Nakamachi, Chuo-ku, Kobe
