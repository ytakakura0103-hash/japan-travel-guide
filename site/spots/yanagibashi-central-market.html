Local Pick

Yanagibashi Central Market

Nagoya

Functioning as something like Nagoya's answer to Tokyo's Tsukiji, Yanagibashi is primarily a working wholesale market for restaurants and retailers, with stalls of fish, shellfish, produce, and dried goods changing hands from the small hours of the morning. A handful of small sushi counters and eateries tucked into and around the market serve extremely fresh seafood to the public alongside the trade buyers, and this is really the reason most visitors come.

There's no entry cost, and meal prices at the small sushi counters are reasonable for the quality given how directly they source from the surrounding stalls; plan on an early start, since the market's energy and stock both thin out by mid-morning.

Because it functions on a professional schedule rather than a tourist one, it stays refreshingly free of crowds even when busy, though the narrow aisles mean courtesy toward working vendors matters more here than at a typical tourist market. Weekday mornings show the market at its most active.

4-15-2 Meieki, Nakamura-ku, Nagoya, Aichi 450-0002

Open in Google Maps

About 7-minute walk from Nagoya Station or Kokusai Center Station (Sakura-dori subway line)
