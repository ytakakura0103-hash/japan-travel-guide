Rengadori Shopping Arcade

Hiroshima

Rengadori (literally 'brick street,' also known as Kure Nakadori) runs through the center of Kure and became something of a small landmark in retail architecture when it installed Japan's first retractable roof arcade in 1990, allowing the covering to open on clear days rather than staying permanently enclosed like a typical shotengai.

The arcade itself is a mix of clothing shops, bakeries, cafes, and casual restaurants, several of which serve the city's certified navy curry, along with older stores that have operated there for decades. It's a working commercial street for Kure residents rather than a tourist attraction, which is exactly why it's a useful stop — it shows a side of the city the naval museums don't.

Because it's close to JR Kure Station and sits on the way toward the Yamato Museum waterfront, it's easy to fold into a museum day without a special detour, and it's a reliable place to grab a casual lunch between exhibits.
