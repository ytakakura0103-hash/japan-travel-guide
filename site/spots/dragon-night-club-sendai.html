Dragon Night Club

Sendai

Dragon Night Club opened in Kokubuncho in August 2025, one of the newer additions to Sendai's small but active club scene, built around a hip-hop-focused booking policy and a two-story open floor plan that lets the crowd move between levels rather than being confined to a single room. That layout gives it more visual and spatial variety than many of Kokubuncho's smaller, single-room clubs and bars.

Its focus on hip-hop sets it apart from the more general, mainstream programming at larger venues like 101 SENDAI nearby, giving Kokubuncho's club scene a bit more genre variety than it had before the venue opened. As with any newer club, its identity and regular event lineup were still taking shape in its first year, so checking current programming is worthwhile.

Its Kokubuncho location puts it within easy reach of the district's yokocho alleys and other nightlife, making it simple to fold into a broader evening exploring the area. Given how recently it opened, confirming current hours and event details directly before visiting is a sensible precaution.

Kokubuncho, Aoba Ward, Sendai
