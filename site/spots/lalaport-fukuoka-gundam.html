LaLaport Fukuoka & Gundam Park

Fukuoka

LaLaport Fukuoka opened in April 2023 as a large-format shopping mall near Hakata Station, but its standout feature isn't a store at all: Gundam Park, built around a nearly 25-meter-tall standing Gundam statue with functioning lights and moving joints, one of only a handful of full-scale Gundam statues anywhere in Japan. The sheer scale of the figure, roughly the height of an eight-story building, makes it visible well beyond the mall itself and has turned it into one of Fukuoka's most photographed pieces of public art.

Beyond the statue, the mall itself offers a standard large-format retail mix, including a substantial Uniqlo and a broad range of fashion, lifestyle, and dining options, making it a practical stop for everyday shopping needs alongside the Gundam spectacle. Periodic light shows and movement sequences run on the statue at scheduled times, worth timing a visit around if seeing it in action rather than static is the priority.

Given its proximity to Hakata Station, it's an easy add-on to a day built around the station's other shopping and dining options, whether before catching a train or as a dedicated stop for Gundam fans specifically. Checking the current light-show schedule on-site is worthwhile if the animated display is the main draw.
