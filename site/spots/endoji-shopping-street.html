Endoji Shopping Street

Nagoya

Just north of the glass-and-steel towers around Nagoya Station, Endoji feels like a step back a few decades: a covered shotengai lined with butchers, tea shops, small eateries, and family-run stores that serve the surrounding neighborhood more than passing tourists. It lacks the polish of Osu but offers a more everyday, unfiltered look at how a Nagoya shopping street actually functions.

There's nothing to pay to walk through, and a visit can be as short as twenty minutes or extended over a meal at one of the small restaurants tucked into the arcade.

The street is normally quiet enough to shop without crowds, which makes its transformation each summer notable: a lantern festival strings the arcade with illuminated decorations and draws a much larger evening crowd than the street sees the rest of the year, worth timing a visit around if festival atmosphere appeals, or avoiding if you prefer the everyday version.

Endoji-dori, Nishi-ku, Nagoya, Aichi
