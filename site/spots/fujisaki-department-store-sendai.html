Fujisaki

Sendai

Fujisaki's history in Sendai stretches back to 1819, when it opened as a textile shop called Ebisuya, giving it more than two centuries of continuous operation in the same city, a genuinely rare lineage among Japanese department stores. Unlike Sendai Mitsukoshi nearby, which belongs to a national chain based in Tokyo, Fujisaki has remained a homegrown, Sendai-headquartered company throughout its history, and that local identity has helped it become the top-selling department store in the entire Tohoku region.

The store spans four connected buildings, the Main Building, Omachi Building, Ichibancho Building, and First Tower Building, giving it a genuinely large footprint across central Ichibancho rather than a single tower. That scale supports a full range of fashion, cosmetics, and gourmet food floors typical of a major department store, alongside the kind of gift and souvenir selection that reflects its role as the region's default upscale shopping destination.

Its central Ichibancho location puts it within easy walking distance of Sendai Mitsukoshi, S-PAL Sendai, and the Kokubuncho nightlife district, making it simple to fold into a broader day of shopping and dining in central Sendai. As the region's most established department store, it's a reasonable first stop for anyone specifically curious about Tohoku's retail history alongside standard shopping needs.

Ichibancho, Aoba Ward, Sendai
