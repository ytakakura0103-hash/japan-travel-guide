GINZA SIX

Tokyo

GINZA SIX opened in 2017 on the former site of several older buildings along Ginza's main avenue, and at roughly 240,000 square meters it's the largest commercial complex in the district, built specifically to compete with Omotesando and international luxury retail hubs. The exterior's latticed aluminum facade, designed to echo traditional Japanese textile weaving patterns at a monumental scale, is one of the more distinctive pieces of contemporary architecture along Chuo-dori.

Inside, the store leans heavily toward flagship-level luxury and designer boutiques rather than mid-range retail, and it supplements shopping with cultural programming: a dedicated art space on the lower level hosts rotating exhibitions and installations, and large-scale art pieces, including a recurring giant sculptural installation in the atrium, are part of the building's identity rather than an afterthought.

The rooftop garden, one of the largest in central Ginza, is open to the public and offers a genuinely quiet break from the shopping floors below, with views across the district's rooftops. Between the architecture, the art programming, and the rooftop, GINZA SIX rewards a walk-through even for visitors who have no intention of buying anything at its boutiques.

6-10-1 Ginza, Chuo City, Tokyo 104-0061
