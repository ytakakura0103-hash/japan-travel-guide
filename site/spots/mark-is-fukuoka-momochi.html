MARK IS Fukuoka Momochi

Fukuoka

MARK IS Fukuoka Momochi opened in 2018 attached directly to MIZUHO PayPay Dome, home stadium of the Fukuoka SoftBank Hawks, giving the mall a natural tie-in with game days and baseball culture that most Fukuoka shopping centers don't share. Its roughly 160 stores cover a broad range of fashion, lifestyle, and dining options, and a cinema and amusement facilities round out the offering for a full day out rather than a quick shopping trip.

The mall's connection to the dome means it can get considerably busier around game days, when Hawks fans pass through on their way in or out of a match, adding a distinctly local, sports-fan energy that shifts the atmosphere from a standard shopping center to something closer to a stadium district on those days. Outside of game days, it functions as a more conventional large-format mall serving the Momochi and Sawara area.

Its location beside the seaside Momochi district, already covered elsewhere in this guide for its parks and Fukuoka Tower, makes it easy to combine a mall visit with a walk along the waterfront or a stop at the tower, rounding out a full day in this part of the city beyond just shopping.

Momochihama, Sawara Ward, Fukuoka
