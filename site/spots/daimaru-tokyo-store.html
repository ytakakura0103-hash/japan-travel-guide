Daimaru Tokyo

Tokyo

Daimaru Tokyo is built directly into the Yaesu side of Tokyo Station, close enough that it functions less like a standalone shopping trip and more like an extension of the station concourse itself. That location makes it genuinely useful in a way that's easy to overlook: it's a practical stop for last-minute souvenirs, boxed gifts (omiyage), or a final proper meal before catching a shinkansen, without adding any real detour to a day already centered on the station.

The basement food hall follows the same depachika format found at Tokyo's other major department stores, extensive prepared food, bakeries, and confectionery counters, and it's particularly well suited to picking up beautifully packaged sweets or bento to take on a train. Upper floors cover the usual range of fashion, cosmetics, and household goods, though the store doesn't have the historic architecture or trend-setting reputation of Nihombashi's older department stores.

Given its function, most visitors treat Daimaru Tokyo as a convenient stop bookending a Tokyo Station visit rather than a planned destination, useful to know about specifically for the window right before departure when a dedicated shopping trip elsewhere isn't practical.

1-9-1 Marunouchi, Chiyoda City, Tokyo 100-6701

Directly connected to Tokyo Station's Yaesu North exit; about 5 minutes on foot from Nihombashi Station.
