Namba Parks

Osaka

Built on the footprint of the old Osaka Stadium, Namba Parks reimagines the standard shopping mall by wrapping it in a terraced rooftop garden that its designers describe as a man-made canyon — a winding series of paths, lawns, and planted terraces that climb up and over the retail floors below. It's an unusual enough piece of architecture that it draws visitors who have no particular interest in shopping, simply to walk the rooftop paths and look back over the surrounding Namba skyline.

Inside, the mall itself carries a broad mix of fashion, lifestyle, and specialty stores across several floors, along with a solid range of restaurants, making it an easy place to combine shopping with a meal. A rooftop-only visit can be done in well under an hour, while working through the retail floors as well easily fills a half day.

The rooftop garden is at its best in spring and autumn, when the planted terraces are in bloom or turning color and the weather makes lingering outdoors more comfortable; it's also a pleasant spot for a breather in the evening, when the terraces are lit and the crowds below in Namba's more famous covered arcades will have thinned only slightly. Because it sits directly connected to Namba Station, it's an easy stop to fold into a day exploring Dotonbori and the surrounding Minami district rather than a separate trip.

2-10-70 Nanba Naka, Naniwa Ward, Osaka
