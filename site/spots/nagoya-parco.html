Nagoya PARCO

Nagoya

Nagoya PARCO opened in 1989 and grew over the following decades into a multi-building complex, adding a South Building in 1998 and a further annex in 2015, spread across West and East Buildings that split roughly between menswear and a women's fashion and beauty focus. Throughout, it's maintained a distinctly youth- and street-culture-oriented identity, standing apart from the more traditional department stores nearby with tenants like Tower Records, video arcades, anime and manga specialty shops, and a rotating lineup of independent designer boutiques.

The East Building also houses a cinema, and the top floors across the complex are given over to restaurants, including options serving Nagoya specialties like miso-katsu, giving visitors a chance to try the city's distinctive food culture without leaving the building. That mix of fashion, pop culture, and food makes it a natural stop for a longer browsing session rather than a quick errand.

The South Building is scheduled to close at the end of February 2027 as part of a joint redevelopment with the adjacent Matsuzakaya South Building, reopening later as a combined department-store and PARCO facility; the Main, West, and East Buildings continue operating normally in the meantime. Its location directly beside Matsuzakaya makes it easy to cover both stores in a single Sakae shopping outing.
