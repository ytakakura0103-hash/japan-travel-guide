Takino Suzuran Hillside National Park

Hokkaido

Takino Suzuran is one of the few national government parks in Japan and covers a large hillside area south of Sapporo, mixing manicured flower gardens with forest trails, cycling paths, and a children's play area built around giant slides that are as popular with adults as kids. Because the terrain is genuinely hilly rather than flat parkland, cycling here has more variety than most city parks, and rental bikes are available on-site.

The flower gardens shift through the seasons: tulips and other spring bulbs, followed by roses and a wide summer bloom, then a large-scale illumination event in some years during autumn. Because the park sits well outside the city and requires a bus transfer, it draws mostly Sapporo-area families rather than international tourists, giving it a noticeably local feel even at its busiest.

Given the size of the grounds, arriving early and picking two or three zones to focus on works better than trying to cover everything in one visit; the walk between the northern forest area and the southern garden area alone takes a while.

In winter the park becomes a large-scale snow play and cross-country ski area, effectively making it a different destination depending on the season, and it's worth checking which sections are open before visiting outside summer.

153-1 Takino, Minami-ku, Sapporo, Hokkaido 005-0862

About 50-60 minutes from Sapporo Station: subway to Makomanai Station, then a Chuo Bus (about 25-30 minutes) to the park entrance.
