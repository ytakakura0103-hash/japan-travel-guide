Zao Okama Crater Lake

Sendai

Okama sits in an active volcanic crater on the ridge of Mount Zao, right along the prefectural border, and its water famously changes color depending on light, mineral content, and season, ranging through shades of emerald, turquoise, and grey-blue, which has earned it the nickname 'goshiki-numa,' the five-colored pond. The barren, volcanic slopes surrounding the crater give the whole scene a stark, almost otherworldly look compared to the forested valleys elsewhere in Miyagi.

Getting there requires either your own car along the seasonal Zao Echo Line toll road, which typically closes in winter due to snow, or a seasonal sightseeing bus; there's no year-round public transit directly to the crater rim, so it takes more planning than most Miyagi day trips. A short walk from the main parking and viewing area gets you to the best overlook, though hikers can continue further along the ridge trails for more solitary views.

There's no fee to view the crater itself, though the toll road and any bus service involved carry their own costs. Because access is weather- and season-dependent, checking current road and bus status before setting out is essential. Midday in clear summer weather draws the most visitors and the clearest water color; the crater is often shrouded in cloud, so building flexibility into travel plans helps.
