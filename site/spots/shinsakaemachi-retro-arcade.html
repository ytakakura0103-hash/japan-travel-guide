Local Pick

Shinsakaemachi Shopping Arcade

Nagoya

Just off the polished main streets of Sakae, this covered arcade and its surrounding underground passages retain a distinctly older, rougher-edged atmosphere, with narrow storefronts given over to idol merchandise, trading cards, secondhand records, retro video games, and various niche hobby goods. It's the kind of place that rewards actually going shop to shop rather than expecting a curated experience, since the appeal is in the density and unpredictability of what's stocked.

Browsing costs nothing, and how much time it takes depends entirely on how deep an interest you have in the specific subcultures represented; an hour covers a reasonable loop for a casual visitor.

It draws a knowledgeable but low-key crowd of hobbyists rather than crowds of tourists, and most shops stay comfortably navigable even on weekends. Because some of the storefronts and passages feel a little worn and dimly lit compared with mainstream Sakae, it's easy to walk past the entrance without realizing what's inside — worth deliberately seeking out rather than stumbling upon.

Shinsakae-machi, Naka-ku, Nagoya, Aichi

Short walk from Sakae Station (Higashiyama and Meijo subway lines)
