Local Pick

Hirose River Riverside Walk

Sendai

The Hirose River loops around the western and southern edge of central Sendai, running below the castle hill and through the Kawauchi and Ohashi areas, and it's a big part of why the city calls itself the 'City of Trees' and prides itself on a green, livable core. Unlike many rivers running through Japanese city centers, the Hirose is unusually clear and shallow in stretches, with visible gravel beds, herons, and the occasional kingfisher.

Paved and dirt paths run along much of its length, used constantly by joggers, cyclists, and dog walkers rather than tour groups, and the river valley offers some of the best casual photo spots in the city, particularly the view of the castle-hill cliffs rising above the water. It costs nothing to walk, and there's no single 'entrance,' making it an easy, flexible add-on between other Sendai sights rather than a planned destination.

The path is busiest with locals exercising in the early morning and early evening; a midday walk on a weekday is quiet and unhurried. Cherry blossoms line parts of the bank in April, and the maples along the Kawauchi stretch turn a deep red in November, making both seasons especially rewarding for a slow riverside walk.

Hirose River, Aoba-ku, Sendai, Miyagi

Accessible on foot from Kawauchi Station on the Tozai subway line, or from the Sendai Castle Ruins area.
