Futsukaichi Onsen

Fukuoka

Futsukaichi is one of the older hot spring areas in the region, with a history said to stretch back over a thousand years and long associations with travelers passing through nearby Dazaifu. It's a compact, low-key onsen town rather than a large resort area, built around a cluster of ryokan and public bathhouses rather than flashy tourist infrastructure.

A number of the ryokan here offer day-use bathing, known as higaeri onsen, letting visitors soak in the mineral waters for a few hours without booking a room, which makes it realistic to fit in as a short detour on the way to or from Dazaifu Tenmangu rather than a separate overnight trip. Facilities and prices vary between establishments, so it's worth checking a couple of options if you have a specific one in mind.

Because it sits just one stop before the Dazaifu transfer on the same rail line, combining a temple visit with a soak afterward is straightforward logistically. Weekday afternoons tend to be quieter than weekends, when day-trippers from Fukuoka city often stop in.

Futsukaichi, Chikushino, Fukuoka Prefecture
