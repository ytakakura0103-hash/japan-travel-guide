Nogeyama Zoo

Yokohama

Unlike most zoos of comparable size in Japan, Nogeyama Zoo charges no admission at all, a policy that has made it a well-used neighborhood institution for local families rather than a headline tourist attraction. Its collection is compact but includes a respectable range of animals, including lions, elephants, and red pandas, arranged along a hillside path that also gives partial views over the surrounding Noge and Sakuragicho rooftops.

Because it is free and modest in scale, it works best as a low-key stop of an hour or so rather than a dedicated half-day outing, and it pairs naturally with a visit to Noge's bars and eateries just down the hill, or with the Sakuragicho waterfront a short walk beyond that. Weekday mornings are consistently quiet, while weekend afternoons bring local families in for a stroll.

The zoo is entirely uncommercialized compared to bigger operations, with no elaborate gift shop or ticketed extras, which is part of its charm for visitors who find larger zoos overwhelming. Cherry blossoms bloom around parts of the grounds in spring, adding a seasonal draw on top of the animals themselves.

Oimatsu-cho, Nishi-ku, Yokohama
