Isezakicho Shopping Street

Yokohama

Isezakicho has functioned as one of Yokohama's principal shopping streets since the late 19th century, and while its status as the city's premier destination has since shifted toward Minato Mirai, it remains a busy, functional shopping district serving residents rather than a curated tourist attraction. A long covered arcade runs much of its length, making it a comfortable option regardless of weather, lined with a broad mix of clothing shops, drugstores, restaurants, and older local businesses.\n\nBecause it caters primarily to Yokohama residents doing everyday shopping, prices and the general atmosphere feel more ordinary and less staged than the waterfront district, which is part of its appeal for visitors who want a look at how locals actually shop. It also has a long-standing connection to Japanese pop music, having been referenced in classic songs about the area, giving it a certain nostalgic status among older visitors.\n\nWeekday afternoons are the easiest time to browse without crowds, since weekends bring a heavier mix of shoppers and diners. It sits a short walk from both Kannai and the Yokohama Stadium area, making it easy to combine with a visit to Yokohama Park or the Three Towers district.

Isezaki-cho, Naka-ku, Yokohama
