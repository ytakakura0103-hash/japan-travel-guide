Rokko Cable Car

Kobe

Operating since the 1930s, the Rokko Cable car climbs a notably steep grade up the mountainside in a ride that takes roughly 10 minutes, gaining a substantial amount of elevation in a short time and offering wide views back over Kobe and the bay as the carriage ascends. The current cars keep a slightly retro look that fans of the line appreciate, with large windows angled to make the most of the view during the climb.

The cable car is the main practical way most visitors reach Rokko Garden Terrace, the Shidare Observatory, and the mountain's other attractions, so for many people it is simply a means to an end rather than a destination itself, but the ride is scenic enough to be worth savoring rather than rushing through. A round trip alone, without stopping at the top, takes under half an hour including the wait.

Weekend and holiday afternoons can mean a short wait for a seat, especially heading up around midday and coming back down around sunset when everyone descends after the evening view; a slightly earlier or later departure than the peak crowd avoids most of that.

Cable-shitacho, Nada-ku, Kobe

Base station (Rokko Cable-shita) is reachable by bus from Rokko Station on the Hankyu Line.
