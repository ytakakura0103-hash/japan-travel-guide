Otokoyama Sake Brewery Museum

Sapporo

Otokoyama traces its brewing lineage back to Edo-period Japan, and its Asahikawa operation was established to take advantage of the exceptionally soft, clean snowmelt water flowing down from the Daisetsuzan mountain range, still the core of its water source today. The on-site museum walks through that history along with the brewing process, and admission and entry are free, a notable contrast to many comparable facility museums elsewhere in Japan.\n\nA tasting counter lets visitors sample a range of the brewery's sake styles, and the shop sells bottles not always easy to find outside Hokkaido, including limited or seasonal releases. The museum building itself has a formal, traditional look that suits the subject better than a purely modern facility would.\n\nBecause it requires a bus ride out from central Asahikawa and doesn't attract the same tour-group traffic as Sapporo's own beer museum, it stays fairly quiet even on weekends, and tastings rarely involve any wait.\n\nWinter, when Asahikawa's cold snaps are most extreme, has a certain logic to it: the same conditions that make the city notoriously cold are part of what makes the local water and brewing climate work, so a winter visit connects the dots a bit more directly than a summer one.

2-7 Nagayama 2-jo, Asahikawa, Hokkaido 079-8412
