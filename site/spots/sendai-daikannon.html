Sendai Daikannon

Sendai

Completed in 1991 on the grounds of Daikanmitsuji temple, this statue was briefly the tallest in the world and remains one of Japan's largest figures of the Buddhist goddess of mercy. It stands on raised ground in a residential part of Izumi Ward, meaning it often appears suddenly in views from the highway or a nearby train line long before you actually reach it.\n\nAn elevator inside the statue's pedestal, reached for a modest fee, carries visitors up to viewing windows partway up the figure, giving a rare vantage point over the Sendai plain and surrounding mountains on clear days. The whole visit, including the elevator ride, generally takes under an hour.\n\nBecause it rarely makes it onto standard tourist itineraries, the site functions more as a local landmark than a destination, which means short or nonexistent queues for the elevator even on weekends. Clear winter days tend to give the best long-distance mountain views from the observation windows.

Daikanmitsuji area, Izumi-ku, Sendai, Miyagi

About 20 minutes by bus from Izumi-Chuo Station on the Sendai Municipal Subway Namboku Line.
