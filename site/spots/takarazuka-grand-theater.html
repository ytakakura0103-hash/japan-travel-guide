Takarazuka Grand Theater

Kobe

Founded in 1914, the Takarazuka Revue casts only women in all roles, including the male leads, known as otokoyaku, who often develop passionate individual fan followings comparable to idol culture. Productions range from adaptations of Western musicals and European history to original fantasy and manga-inspired works, staged with elaborate costumes, sweeping staircases and a scale of production that rivals major Western musical theater.\n\nThe Grand Theater itself, an Art Deco-influenced building, is the troupe's main home stage and the center of a small surrounding district built up around the fandom, gift shops, cafes themed to specific troupes, and photo displays of current performers. Even without seeing a full show, walking through this area gives a sense of just how devoted and organized the fan culture around the Revue is.\n\nTickets for popular productions can sell out well in advance, particularly for shows featuring especially popular performers, so booking ahead is strongly advised if seeing an actual performance is the goal. Even a walk around the theater exterior and adjoining Flower Road gives a worthwhile sense of the spectacle-driven culture that's grown up around it over more than a century.

Takarazuka, Hyogo Prefecture

From Sannomiya, take the Hankyu Kobe and Imazu Lines directly to Takarazuka (roughly 30-40 minutes), or JR via Osaka to Takarazuka; the theater is a short walk from Takarazuka Station.
