Etajima Coastal Cycling Route

Hiroshima

Etajima is best known internationally, if at all, for its former Imperial Naval Academy, but the rest of the island — connected to the mainland by bridge as well as ferry — is a mix of small fishing settlements, hillside citrus orchards, and quiet coastal roads that make it a favorite low-traffic cycling loop for residents of Kure and Hiroshima city.\n\nBicycle rental is available near the main ferry terminals, and a loop of the island's coastal roads takes most riders three to five hours depending on stops, passing beaches, small Shinto shrines tucked into the hillside, and roadside stands selling citrus in season.\n\nCompared to the internationally famous Shimanami Kaido, Etajima's routes are shorter, flatter in stretches, and see a fraction of the cyclist traffic, making it a good option for a more relaxed half-day ride, or for visitors basing themselves in Kure who want an outdoor activity beyond the naval museums.
