Entsuin Temple

Sendai

Entsuin was built to honor Date Mitsumune, a grandson of Date Masamune who died young, and its centerpiece is a mausoleum whose interior is decorated with gold leaf and elaborate carvings that have survived remarkably well since the Edo period. Unlike many temples in the area, it also has a Western-style rose garden added in more recent decades, an unusual pairing with the otherwise traditional temple grounds.\n\nAdmission is a modest separate fee from Zuiganji next door, and a visit takes around twenty to thirty minutes, longer if the roses are in bloom and worth lingering over.\n\nMost visitors to Matsushima funnel straight into Zuiganji, Matsushima's headline temple, and skip this smaller neighbor entirely, which keeps Entsuin noticeably calmer. Roses peak in June, and the temple occasionally holds evening illuminations that show the mausoleum's gold interior in a different light.

91 Matsushima, Matsushima-machi, Miyagi-gun, Miyagi
