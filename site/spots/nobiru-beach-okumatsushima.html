Nobiru Beach & Okumatsushima

Sendai

The Nobiru area was heavily damaged in the 2011 tsunami, and its train station and surrounding neighborhood have since been rebuilt on higher, safer ground as part of the broader Okumatsushima district. Today the area combines a sandy beach facing Matsushima Bay with a scenic lagoon behind it, making it popular for swimming, kayaking, and simply walking along the shore.

Beach access is free, and it's an easy day trip directly by train from Sendai, without needing a car or bus transfer. Basic facilities are available near the station area.

Because it sits a step further from Matsushima's main tourist circuit, Nobiru stays noticeably quieter, even in summer. Swimming season runs through the warmer months, while the shoulder seasons offer a calmer, more contemplative visit if the beach crowds aren't the point.
