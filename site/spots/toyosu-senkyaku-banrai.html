Toyosu Senkyaku Banrai

Tokyo

Toyosu Senkyaku Banrai is a purpose-built market street next to Toyosu Market, designed with an old-fashioned, Edo-period-style facade to evoke a traditional shopping street even though the complex itself only opened in the early 2020s. Inside, around seventy restaurants and food stalls serve seafood sourced from the adjacent wholesale market, alongside souvenir shops selling regional snacks and goods, giving visitors a more atmospheric, tourist-friendly alternative to browsing the working market itself.

The complex's other notable feature is a hot-spring bathhouse on the upper floor, using genuine onsen water and including both indoor and outdoor bathing areas along with a view out over Tokyo Bay, a fairly unusual combination to find directly next to a fish market. Admission to the bathhouse is separate from the food and shopping areas, and it operates on its own hours, generally into the evening, well past when the food stalls wind down.

Because it was built specifically to complement Toyosu Market, the busiest period here tends to mirror the market's own morning rush, though the food stalls stay busy into the afternoon as well, unlike parts of the main market that close earlier. It's an easy stop to combine with a Toyosu Market visit, with the bathhouse offering a way to extend the visit into an evening activity rather than a quick morning stop.
