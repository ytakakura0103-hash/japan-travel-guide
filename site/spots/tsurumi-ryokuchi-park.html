Tsurumi Ryokuchi Park

Osaka

Built as the main venue for the 1990 International Garden and Greenery Exposition, this sprawling park in eastern Osaka still carries the legacy of that event in its layout, with themed gardens representing different countries and regions scattered across its roughly 120 hectares, alongside a large greenhouse, sports facilities and open lawns that see everyday use by local residents rather than only visitors. The park's spring display is its best-known feature, when a large tulip planting blooms alongside the season's cherry blossoms, creating a combination of colour that draws crowds specifically for the display in addition to the park's usual visitors.

Because of its size, a full exploration of the international gardens and other sections can easily take half a day, though a shorter visit focused on the central lawn and nearby gardens can be done in an hour or two. The park is free to enter, with the greenhouse and select facilities charging separate admission.

Spring weekends during the tulip and cherry blossom season bring the heaviest crowds, so a weekday visit or an early arrival is worth considering during that window; outside spring, the park is considerably quieter and works well simply as an open-air walk or picnic spot. It sits a short walk from Tsurumi-ryokuchi Station on the Osaka Metro, making it accessible without a car despite being further from the city centre than most of Osaka's headline attractions.
