Yokohama Vivre

Yokohama

Yokohama Vivre has operated near Yokohama Station's west exit since 1978, originally opening as a Nichii general store before rebranding as Vivre in 1985, giving it a longer continuous history at this location than most of the shopping complexes that have sprung up around Yokohama Station in the decades since. Its roughly 122 tenants span a fairly standard fashion and lifestyle mix, but a renovation push since 2022, branded internally as "LIVE YOKOHAMA," has shifted its identity in a more specific direction.

That renovation has significantly expanded the building's anime, game, and fan-goods retail, chasing the growing "oshi-katsu" fan-culture spending trend among Gen Z shoppers, a category that's become an increasingly significant part of Japanese retail more broadly. That pivot gives Vivre a genuinely different character from the department stores and general fashion buildings nearby, positioning it as a pop-culture-forward alternative within Yokohama Station's dense cluster of shopping options.

For visitors specifically interested in anime and game merchandise, Vivre is worth checking alongside more dedicated pop-culture destinations elsewhere in the city, and its ongoing renovation means the specific tenant lineup has been evolving fairly quickly, worth confirming current offerings before a visit built around a particular fandom.
