Sotetsu JOINUS

Yokohama

Sotetsu JOINUS combines the Sotetsu railway's station building with an extensive underground shopping arcade at Yokohama Station's west exit, anchored by Takashimaya Yokohama and organized in a way that makes it manageable even for first-time visitors. Rather than a maze of undifferentiated shops, the underground arcade is zoned by category, fashion in one section, prepared foods in another, a bookstore area elsewhere, cutting down on the disorientation that can come with navigating a large underground mall.

JOINUS DINING, the basement restaurant street spanning two levels, gathers a wide mix of Yokohama specialty restaurants and international cuisine, giving visitors a substantial range of dining options without needing to surface at street level. The complex previously operated partly under the name Diamond Chikagai before its current JOINUS branding, part of a shopping district history that stretches back decades at this station.

Being entirely underground and directly connected to the station, it's one of the most weather-proof shopping options in Yokohama, useful on a rainy day or simply as a convenient stop while passing through one of the city's busiest transit hubs. Its zoned layout makes it easier to find a specific type of shop quickly compared to more sprawling underground arcades elsewhere in Japan.
