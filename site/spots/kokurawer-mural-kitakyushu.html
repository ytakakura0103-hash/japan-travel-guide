Kokurawer

Fukuoka

Kokurawer is a giant mural, roughly seven meters tall and twenty meters wide, unveiled in June 2025 inside Kitakyushu's Uomachi Ginten-gai shopping arcade, one of the city's oldest covered shopping streets. It was created specifically as a symbol of recovery after a fire tore through part of the arcade the previous year, destroying a section of a shopping street with deep roots in the local community, and the mural's scale and placement were deliberately chosen to give the rebuilding arcade a new visual landmark.

That backstory gives the mural a weight beyond its size alone: it functions as a community rallying point as much as a piece of public art, and visiting it doubles as a way to see how the arcade has continued operating and rebuilding in the aftermath of the fire. The mural has quickly become a meeting point and photo spot within the arcade, drawing both locals checking on the area's recovery and visitors specifically seeking out the artwork.

Because it sits within a working shopping arcade rather than a standalone gallery or plaza, visiting is naturally combined with browsing the surrounding shops and getting a sense of Kokura's everyday shopping street life, distinct from the more polished retail experiences at Kitakyushu's larger malls.

Uomachi Ginten-gai, Kokurakita Ward, Kitakyushu
