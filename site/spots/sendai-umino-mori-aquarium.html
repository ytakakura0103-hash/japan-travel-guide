Sendai Umino-Mori Aquarium

Sendai

Opened in 2015, this aquarium organizes its tanks around the seas of Tohoku itself rather than a generic global tour, tracing a route from the shallow, island-dotted waters of Matsushima Bay out to the deeper Pacific waters off the Sanriku coast. It's a useful way for visitors to understand the marine environment behind the region's famous seafood before or after visiting the actual coast.

The big-ticket features are a large open-water tank with sharks and rays viewed from a walkway, and scheduled dolphin and sea lion shows that are popular with families. Interactive touch pools and smaller exhibits focused on Tohoku river and coastal species round out the collection, making it a reliably engaging half-day stop for kids of a wide age range.

Admission runs on the higher side for a family day out, as is typical for large aquariums, and a full visit with show times factored in takes two to three hours. It gets busiest on weekends and during school holidays, particularly around show times; arriving near opening on a weekday gives noticeably more breathing room at the main tanks.

The aquarium marked its tenth anniversary in 2025 with a run of animal-related milestones, including a dolphin birth in May 2025 and new penguin chicks, that have kept it in steady rotation on social media alongside its regular seasonal exhibits and collaborations.

4-6 Nakano, Miyagino-ku, Sendai, Miyagi

Open in Google Maps

About 20 minutes by JR Senseki Line from Sendai Station to Nakano-Sakae Station, then about 5 minutes by local bus (or a 15-minute walk) to the aquarium; a direct bus from Sendai Station's east exit is also available but takes about 54 minutes.
