The Mall Sendai Nagamachi

Sendai

The Mall Sendai Nagamachi opened in 1997 as one of Tohoku's largest shopping centers, spread across roughly 85,000 square meters in the Nagamachi district south of central Sendai, offering a genuinely different shopping experience from the department stores and covered arcades downtown. A major 2017 renovation expanded the complex to more than 150 specialty stores and a larger food court, adding more family and children's-focused tenants alongside its existing anchor stores.

Its scale and suburban, car- and family-friendly layout set it apart from the denser, more walkable shopping streets of Ichibancho and Kokubuncho, closer in spirit to a large American-style mall than a traditional Japanese covered arcade. A cinema complex within the Part 2 building rounds out the offering, making it possible to combine shopping, a meal, and a movie in a single visit.

For visitors staying in southern Sendai or looking for a more spread-out, one-stop shopping experience with young children in tow, it's a practical option that Sendai's downtown shopping districts don't quite replicate. Its direct connection to Nagamachi Station keeps it accessible by train despite its more suburban setting.

Nagamachi, Taihaku Ward, Sendai
