ATOM Shinjuku

Tokyo

ATOM Shinjuku opened in October 2024, extending a club name already established in Shibuya's nightlife scene into a new Shinjuku location near the edge of Ni-chome. It's built primarily around EDM, with hip-hop and R&B nights mixed into the weekly schedule, aiming at a mainstream dance-and-drink crowd rather than the more niche electronic music programming found at older venues like WOMB.

Being one of the newer additions to Shinjuku's club scene, it's picked up momentum quickly among the current generation of Tokyo clubgoers, and its location gives it easy walking access to both Shinjuku Sanchome's izakaya strip and Ni-chome's bar district, useful if a night out involves more than one stop.

As with any newer venue, checking current event listings and hours directly (the club posts updates via its official Instagram account) is worth doing before a visit, since program details can shift more than at a longer-established club.

5-17-1, 3F, Shinjuku, Shinjuku City, Tokyo

Open in Google Maps

About 3 minutes on foot from Shinjuku-sanchome Station (Tokyo Metro Marunouchi and Fukutoshin Lines); about 9 minutes from JR Shinjuku Station.
