Naruko Gorge

Sendai

Carved by the Oitagawa River, Naruko Gorge cuts a deep, steep-walled ravine through the hills near Naruko Onsen, best appreciated from the Ofukazawa observation bridge, which spans the gorge and offers a clean view straight down into the ravine and along its length. A walking trail also descends partway into the gorge itself for those wanting a closer look at the rock walls and river below.\n\nViewing from the bridge is free, with roadside parking nearby, and the walk to the viewpoint takes only a few minutes, though the descending trail requires more time and sturdier footwear.\n\nAutumn is unquestionably the peak season here, when the gorge's mixed forest turns a striking mix of red, orange, and yellow, drawing steady but rarely overwhelming crowds compared to more famous foliage spots. Visiting on a weekday during the October peak, rather than a weekend, meaningfully reduces both crowding and the traffic that can build up on the access road.
