Mihara Octopus Cuisine

Hiroshima

Mihara's stretch of the Inland Sea produces octopus known locally for its firm, dense texture, a result of the strong tidal currents that pass through the strait, and the city has made the animal into something close to a civic mascot: octopus statues and decorative flags appear around JR Mihara Station, and restaurants across town build menus specifically around it.\n\nPreparations range from simple boiled octopus served with vinegar-miso sauce to grilled skewers, octopus tempura, and rice dishes mixed with diced octopus, giving visitors a chance to compare textures and flavors across several dishes in one meal rather than trying just one style.\n\nBecause Mihara is a common transfer point for travelers moving between Hiroshima city, Onomichi, and Shikoku, it's an easy place to build in a meal stop, and the octopus theme gives it a distinct identity that's easy to overlook if you only think of the city as a train junction.
