Funaoka Castle Ruins Park

Sendai

Built on the ruins of a former castle overlooking the town of Shibata, this hilltop park is planted with around thirteen hundred cherry trees and is recognized as one of Japan's notable cherry blossom viewing spots. A short slope car, a small cable-hauled car, traditionally carries visitors up through a tunnel of blossoms to the summit, though it's worth checking locally whether it's currently operating, since it has faced periodic mechanical closures.

Even without the slope car, the walk up takes only about fifteen to twenty minutes, ending at a white Kannon statue and an observation deck looking out over the Hitome Senbonzakura cherry corridor below and the Zao mountains beyond.

While the Shibata Sakura Festival period draws real crowds, arriving early in the morning or visiting just before or after the festival's peak weekend tends to make a meaningful difference. Bloom generally runs from early to late April, tracking closely with the nearby riverside cherry trees.
