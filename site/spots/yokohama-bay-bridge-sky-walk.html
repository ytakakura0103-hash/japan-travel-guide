Local Pick

Yokohama Bay Bridge Sky Walk

Yokohama

Opened as an observation facility not long after the bridge itself was completed in the late 1980s, the Sky Walk fell out of regular use and closed for years before a partial reopening allowed visitors back onto part of the walkway and an enclosed panoramic lounge near the base of one of the bridge's main towers. The appeal here is unlike any other viewpoint in Yokohama: standing essentially inside the bridge structure itself as large ships pass beneath, close enough to feel the scale in a way harbor-level views cannot match.

Access is genuinely limited and worth confirming carefully before making the trip, since the facility opens only on a set schedule, generally Saturdays, Sundays, and holidays (11:00-18:00), rather than daily. Public transit access is also less direct than to the central waterfront sights, so a car or a longer bus ride is typically involved.

Because of that limited access, it is very much an off-the-beaten-path option rather than a default itinerary stop, best suited to visitors specifically interested in bridge and port engineering, or those chasing an uncommon photo of a ship passing directly below their feet.

1 Daikoku Futo, Tsurumi-ku, Yokohama
