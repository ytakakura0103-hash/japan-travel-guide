Mitsukoshi Nagoya Sakae

Nagoya

Mitsukoshi Nagoya Sakae rises eleven stories above one of the city's busiest shopping intersections, carrying the full range of brand-name fashion, cosmetics, and gift-quality food typical of a major Mitsukoshi flagship, with a basement food hall and restaurant floors near the top rounding out the offering. Its central Sakae location puts it within easy reach of the district's other major retail anchors, and it's directly connected by an internal passageway to Lachic, a younger, more fashion-forward building operated by the same corporate group next door.

That connection to Lachic gives the two buildings a complementary relationship rather than a competitive one: Mitsukoshi covers the more traditional, established department store experience, while Lachic next door leans into younger, trend-driven fashion, letting visitors move between both without stepping back outside. Above the club scene of Sakae 4-chome and the upscale bars of Nishiki, Mitsukoshi anchors the district's daytime retail identity.

As a straightforward, centrally located department store, it works well as a reliable stop for standard shopping needs without requiring the same amount of time as browsing Matsuzakaya's larger three-building complex nearby. Its underground arcade connection also makes it an easy stop on a rainy day without needing to surface at street level.

Sakae 3-1-15, Naka Ward, Nagoya
