Daikoku Parking Area

Yokohama

Daikoku Parking Area functions, officially, as a standard highway rest stop on the Shuto Expressway's Bay Shore Route, built on reclaimed land jutting into Yokohama Bay. Unofficially, it's become one of the best-known informal gathering spots in Japan for custom car culture, where owners of modified sports cars, JDM classics, and show cars park up to display their vehicles and meet other enthusiasts, a tradition that's built up organically over years rather than through any official event schedule.

Friday and Saturday nights around 7pm tend to bring the heaviest concentration of cars and photographers, when the parking area fills with everything from meticulously restored vintage models to heavily modified current builds, all set against the backdrop of the bay and passing container ships. It's become a recurring subject for car culture content on social media specifically because of that unplanned, organic gathering quality, distinct from a formal car show.

Because it's a functioning parking area rather than a dedicated attraction, there's no admission fee and no set hours, but a car is required to visit given its highway location, and the crowd and atmosphere vary considerably by night and season. Visitors without their own car should look into car-focused tour or rideshare options if the custom car scene specifically is the draw.
