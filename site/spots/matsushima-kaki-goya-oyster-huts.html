Matsushima Kaki-goya (Oyster Huts)

Sendai

Miyagi's calm, nutrient-rich bays, and Matsushima's in particular, make it one of Japan's most productive oyster-farming regions, and every winter a scatter of kaki-goya, simple oyster huts, pop up around the bay offering all-you-can-eat grilled oyster sessions within a fixed time limit. Diners grill their own oysters at a shared table, shucking and eating at their own pace, a hands-on, informal style of eating that's as much about the process as the food itself.

The oysters here tend to run smaller and more mineral-forward than the famously huge Miyagi oysters some visitors may have heard of from other prefectures, and the freshness, straight from bay to grill, is the main selling point rather than sheer size. Some huts also offer other bay seafood grilled alongside the oysters for those in a group who want variety.

Pricing is set per session with a time limit, generally moderate for the amount of food included, and it's worth booking ahead during the season's peak, since seating is limited and popular huts fill up on weekends. The season generally runs from around November through March, tapering off as the water warms; a weekday lunch session is the easiest way to get a table without a wait.
