Tanukikoji Shopping Arcade

Sapporo

Tanukikoji is a roofed shopping street that predates much of downtown Sapporo, running seven blocks parallel to the busier Odori and Susukino areas. Alongside the souvenir and drugstore chains that have moved in over the decades, the arcade still holds long-running local businesses: small tea shops, a game arcade, tailors, and family-run restaurants that have operated here for generations.

Because it's covered, it's a reliable option regardless of Sapporo's weather, useful given how often rain or heavy snow can disrupt outdoor plans. The mix of old and new is part of the appeal: a shop selling handmade wagashi might sit next to a modern souvenir stand, and locals still use the arcade for everyday errands rather than treating it purely as a tourist strip.

Compared to the parallel streets around Susukino Station, Tanukikoji draws a more mixed local-and-visitor crowd, and ducking a block or two off the main covered strip into the side entrances reveals smaller shops that most tour groups pass by entirely.

It's a year-round destination by design, since the roof removes the seasonal factor, though it gets a notable bump in foot traffic during the Sapporo Snow Festival when a satellite sculpture site sometimes appears nearby.

Minami 2-3-jo, Nishi 1-7-chome, Chuo-ku, Sapporo, Hokkaido
