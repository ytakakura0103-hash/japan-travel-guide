Local Pick

Ikuta Road

Kobe

Named for the shrine it runs behind, Ikuta Road and the tangle of small lanes around it form Kobe's densest cluster of bars, izakaya, and small restaurants, tucked just out of sight of the main Sannomiya shopping streets. Signage stacks several stories high on some buildings, each floor a different tiny bar or restaurant, which is part of the appeal for anyone who likes exploring rather than following a set itinerary.

Most places are small, sometimes only seating a handful of guests, so it rewards wandering and picking somewhere that looks appealing rather than searching for one specific address. Kobe beef restaurants of varying price points are scattered through the area alongside far cheaper standing bars and ramen counters, so it suits both a splurge dinner and a casual drink.

The area is naturally at its liveliest from early evening onward, and while it never gets as overwhelming as the biggest entertainment districts in Osaka or Tokyo, weekend nights bring a noticeably younger, louder crowd than weekday evenings. Visitors who prefer a calmer, more conversational atmosphere generally find weeknights a better fit.
