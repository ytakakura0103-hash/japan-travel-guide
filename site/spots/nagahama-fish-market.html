Nagahama Fish Market

Fukuoka

Nagahama has functioned as Fukuoka's main wholesale fish market for decades, with fishermen and vendors trading from the very early morning hours, sometimes starting as early as 3am, well before most visitors would reasonably be awake. On ordinary days the actual auction and wholesale floor is closed to the general public, but on the second Saturday of each month it opens up for anyone to walk through and buy directly.

On every other day, the ground floor restaurants attached to the market building remain open to the public and serve genuinely fresh seafood, including popular breakfast sets built around sashimi and grilled fish, a good option for travelers who want the market's freshness without needing to time a visit around the monthly public day.

If you do plan around the second Saturday opening, arriving by around 7:30am or earlier is a common recommendation, since the best selection tends to disappear quickly and the market can sell out and close as early as mid-morning. It's a working market rather than a tourist facility, so come prepared for a functional, unpolished atmosphere rather than a curated food hall.

3-11-3 Nagahama, Chuo Ward, Fukuoka
