Yokohama Anpanman Children's Museum

Yokohama

Anpanman is one of the most enduringly popular children's characters in Japan, and this museum, part of a small national chain, gives young fans a chance to meet costumed characters, play on themed structures, and shop for merchandise not easily found outside Japan. It is split into a paid museum and play area and a free ground-floor mall of Anpanman-themed shops and a bakery, so even families not wanting to pay full admission can browse the shops and grab a snack.

The museum area is designed almost entirely for small children, roughly toddler to early elementary age, so it is less rewarding for visitors without kids in that range, and ticket prices are the same for adults and children over a certain age, which is worth budgeting for. Character greeting events happen on a set schedule through the day, and arriving in time for a favorite character's appearance takes some planning.

Weekends and Japanese school holidays bring the heaviest crowds by a wide margin, so a weekday visit, ideally right at opening, gives noticeably shorter lines for the popular play areas and photo opportunities with costumed characters.

6-2-9 Minato Mirai, Nishi-ku, Yokohama
