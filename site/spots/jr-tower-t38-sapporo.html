JR Tower & T38 Observation Deck

Hokkaido

JR Tower rises directly above Sapporo Station, combining the Stellar Place shopping mall, a hotel, and Daimaru Sapporo into a single multi-use complex that most visitors pass through simply by using the station. Stellar Place alone offers a broad fashion and lifestyle retail lineup spread across the tower's lower and mid floors, making it one of the most convenient shopping stops in the city given its direct station integration.

The tower's standout feature sits near the top: the T38 Observation Deck, reached by a dedicated elevator from the sixth floor, rises to 160 meters, making it the tallest observation deck in Hokkaido. From up there, views stretch to Ishikari Bay, Mount Teine, Hokkaido University, and Sapporo Dome, and the night view has been ranked among Japan's best. The deck's interior doubles as a small gallery space featuring work from local Hokkaido designers, alongside a cafe and gift shop.

Combining a browse through Stellar Place's shops with the trip up to T38 makes for a fuller visit than treating either half separately, and the deck's central, easily walkable location makes it one of the simplest big-view stops to fit into a Sapporo travel day. Checking current hours before visiting is worthwhile, since they shift seasonally.
