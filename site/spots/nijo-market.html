Nijo Market

Hokkaido

Nijo Market has operated near Sapporo's waterfront-facing streets since the late 1800s and remains smaller and more compact than the better-known Sapporo Central Wholesale Market's outer market, which puts it within easy walking distance of downtown rather than requiring a separate trip. Stalls sell fresh and processed seafood, Hokkaido melon in season, and other local produce, with several small sushi counters tucked into the covered lanes for a sit-down meal.

Because it's older and physically smaller than the wholesale market's outer stalls, some vendors here still sell primarily to Sapporo households doing everyday shopping, which gives it a different rhythm than a purely tourist-facing market. Prices and quality vary stall to stall, so it's worth a quick look around before committing to a sushi counter.

It sees a steady flow of visitors given its central location, but rarely the dense crowds of the outer wholesale market, especially earlier in the morning before tour groups arrive. Weekday mornings are the calmest window.

Crab and seasonal seafood are best in the colder months, while Yubari melon and other Hokkaido produce peak in summer, making the market worth two very different visits depending on when you're in the city.

Minami 3-jo, Higashi 1-2-chome, Chuo-ku, Sapporo, Hokkaido 060-0053
