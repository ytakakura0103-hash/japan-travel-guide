Suma Rikyu Park

Kobe

Built on land that once served as a detached imperial villa, Suma Rikyu Park combines a formal Western-style rose garden with wooded walking paths climbing the hillside above Suma Beach. Several hundred varieties of roses bloom through the garden in two main seasons, roughly late spring and again in autumn, drawing local garden enthusiasts more than international visitors, and the forested upper sections offer quiet trails and occasional glimpses of the coastline below.

A thorough visit covering both the rose garden and a walk into the wooded park takes a couple of hours, though a shorter loop through just the formal garden is enough for most casual visitors. The park also has a small waterfall and pond area that many people miss if they stick only to the rose beds near the entrance.

Because it sits up the hill from Suma's main beach and aquarium attractions, this park sees only a fraction of the foot traffic, even during peak rose bloom, making it one of the more reliably peaceful green spaces in the city. It rewards a slower pace and is best treated as a dedicated outing rather than a quick add-on.

Sumaurakoen, Suma-ku, Kobe
