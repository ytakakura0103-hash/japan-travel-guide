Local Pick

Yokohama English Garden

Yokohama

This garden sits well outside the central waterfront district, in a quieter residential part of the city, and it takes its design cues from English country gardens rather than the Japanese landscape style found at Sankeien, with informal borders, climbing roses, and a cottage-like layout that feels distinctly different from most gardens in the region. Its rose collection is the main draw, with a wide range of varieties in bloom through the late spring and again into autumn.

Because it charges admission and sits off the main tourist path, it draws a smaller, more dedicated crowd of garden enthusiasts rather than general sightseers, which keeps it comfortably uncrowded even during peak bloom. A cafe on site allows for a slow visit rather than a quick walk-through, and the seasonal plantings change enough through the year that a repeat visit in a different season can feel like a different garden.

Given the distance from central Yokohama, roughly a twenty to thirty minute train journey from Sakuragicho or Yokohama Station toward Kikuna, it suits visitors with a specific interest in gardens or roses rather than those trying to cover the city's headline sights in a single day.

6-1 Nishihiranuma-cho, Nishi-ku, Yokohama (inside tvk ecom park)
