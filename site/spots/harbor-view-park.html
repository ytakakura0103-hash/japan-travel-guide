Harbor View Park (Minato no Mieru Oka Koen)

Yokohama

Perched on the Yamate bluff above the old foreign settlement, this park is split into a few distinct zones, a French-influenced hillside garden, an English-style rose garden, and an open observation terrace that lines up almost perfectly with Yokohama Bay Bridge in the distance. It has been a favored viewpoint since the area was home to foreign consulates in the late nineteenth century, and the elevation still gives it one of the better free harbor views in the city.

The rose garden peaks twice a year, mid-May to mid-June and again in October to November, and photographers show up early on clear mornings to catch the blooms with the bay in the background. Outside those windows the park is simply a pleasant green space, quieter and less photographed but still worth the walk up from Motomachi or Ishikawacho Station.

Because several of Yamate's Western-style heritage houses sit within a short walk, this park works best as an anchor point for a longer stroll through the old foreign quarter rather than a standalone stop. Evenings bring soft lighting over the bay that is worth timing a visit around, and the park stays open well after dark, unlike most of the heritage buildings nearby.

114 Yamate-cho, Naka-ku, Yokohama

Open in Google Maps

About 5 minutes on foot from Minatomirai Line Motomachi-Chukagai Station (exit 6), or 20 minutes on foot from JR Ishikawacho Station.
