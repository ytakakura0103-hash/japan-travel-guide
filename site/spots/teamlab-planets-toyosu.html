teamLab Planets

Tokyo

teamLab Planets is a barefoot, walk-through digital art museum where the boundary between visitor and artwork is intentionally blurred: guests wade through a room of ankle-to-knee-deep water lit from below with drifting koi projections, cross a mirrored room where the floor seems to fall away, and walk through a greenhouse of hanging orchids that changes color and mood by the hour. Because of the water rooms, visitors are asked to roll up trousers or wear shorts, and towels are generally provided at the entrance; a locker area lets you store shoes and bags.

The museum is intentionally dark and disorienting in places, which is part of the appeal but worth knowing in advance, particularly for visitors with small children or mobility concerns. A full visit, moving through all the rooms at a comfortable pace and stopping for photos, usually takes around an hour to ninety minutes.

Because teamLab Planets sells timed tickets and each session has a capacity limit, advance booking is strongly recommended, especially for weekends, and same-day walk-up availability can be very limited. Earlier time slots tend to be less crowded than midday and evening ones. The museum periodically closes for short periods between exhibition changeovers, so it's worth checking that it's open before planning a visit. Toyosu itself is on the waterfront and pairs easily with a stop at nearby Toyosu Market for food afterward.
