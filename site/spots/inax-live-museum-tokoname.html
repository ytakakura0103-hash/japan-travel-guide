Local Pick

INAX Live Museum

Nagoya

Run by the building-materials company LIXIL under its INAX tile brand, the INAX Live Museum is really a small campus of separate exhibition spaces built around Tokoname's identity as a ceramics town. A preserved noborigama, or climbing kiln, along with a tall brick chimney once used for firing terracotta pipes, anchors the outdoor section, giving visitors a close look at the industrial-scale kiln technology that powered the region's pottery trade for over a century.

Indoors, separate galleries cover the history of roof tiles across Japan, decorative architectural ceramics from around the world, and the broader story of how clay has been used in construction from ancient times to the present. Hands-on workshops let visitors try their hand at basic clay work, making the museum a reasonable stop for families as well as design and architecture enthusiasts.

The museum sits close enough to the Yakimono Sanpomichi pottery footpath that the two make an obvious pairing for a half-day in Tokoname focused on ceramics.

1-130 Okuei-cho, Tokoname, Aichi

Open in Google Maps

About a 25 to 30-minute walk from Tokoname Station on the Meitetsu line, or a roughly 6-minute bus ride (Chita Bus) followed by a short walk, or a short taxi ride.
