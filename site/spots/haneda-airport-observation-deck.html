Haneda Airport Observation Deck

Tokyo

Haneda Airport, Tokyo's main domestic and a major international gateway, has free public observation decks on the roofs of its terminal buildings, giving unusually close views of aircraft taxiing, taking off, and landing without needing a ticket or boarding pass. Each terminal's deck has a slightly different vantage point on the runways, so the best views can depend on wind direction and which runways are active on a given day; aviation enthusiasts often check flight and wind information in advance to pick the most active terminal.

Terminal 3, which handles international flights, is also home to Edo Koji, a themed food and shopping floor built to resemble an Edo-period townscape, with reproduction wooden shopfronts and lantern-lit streets housing restaurants and souvenir shops; the theming is elaborate enough that it's become a minor attraction in its own right, separate from the observation decks or actual air travel. It's a popular stop for visitors with a layover or an early flight who want a meal with more atmosphere than a typical airport food court.

The observation decks are outdoors and exposed to the weather, so a visit is more pleasant on a clear day. Because Haneda is a working airport with security procedures for the boarding areas, the observation decks and Edo Koji are located in publicly accessible parts of the terminals and don't require a boarding pass to visit.
