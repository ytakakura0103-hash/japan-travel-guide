Ichiran Ramen Original Shop

Fukuoka

Ichiran's private-booth ramen format, where diners order through a form and eat facing a wooden partition rather than a shared counter, started here in Fukuoka and this Nakasu location functions as both a working shop and the company's headquarters. It's larger and busier than a typical branch, with multiple floors of the individual booths the chain is famous for.

The draw for ramen fans specifically is that this is Ichiran's original shop, serving the brand's classic Natural Tonkotsu Ramen recipe that has remained unchanged since the company's founding here. (Note: the limited-edition Gokaku Ramen, with its extra-long noodles, is exclusive to Ichiran's Dazaifu Sando branch, not this Nakasu headquarters.) Beyond that, the ordering process is the same as any Ichiran: a paper form lets you customize noodle firmness, broth richness, and spice level before your bowl arrives.

Because this is a flagship location rather than a hidden gem, expect a queue, especially in the evening; a vending-machine ticket system out front manages the line. Late morning or mid-afternoon, outside the standard lunch and dinner rushes, tends to move faster. It sits close enough to the Nakasu yatai stalls that the two make an easy combined food outing.

5-3-2 Nakasu, Hakata Ward, Fukuoka
