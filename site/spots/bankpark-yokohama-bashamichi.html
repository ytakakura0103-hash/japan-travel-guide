BankPark YOKOHAMA

Yokohama

BankPark YOKOHAMA occupies the former Yokohama branch of Japan's first modern bank, a building recognized as a Yokohama City Designated Historic Structure, which reopened in October 2025 after a careful renovation that preserved its original atrium while converting the interior into a genuinely mixed cultural space. Rather than a single tenant, the building now houses a cafe, a crafts and design gallery, a flower shop, and a library, all arranged around the atrium's open, light-filled center.

That combination gives the building a community and cultural-exchange character distinct from a typical commercial redevelopment, positioning it as somewhere to linger and browse rather than a quick shopping stop. The contrast between the building's early 20th-century bank architecture, all columns and formal proportions, and the contemporary, casual use of the space underneath makes for a striking visual pairing that's driven much of its social media attention since reopening.

Its location within Bashamichi, already covered elsewhere in this guide for its historic streetscape and shopping, makes it an easy stop on a broader walk through the district. The atrium space alone is worth a look even for visitors not planning to buy anything from the shops inside.

Bashamichi, Naka Ward, Yokohama
