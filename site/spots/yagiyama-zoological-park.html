Yagiyama Zoological Park

Sendai

Perched on a wooded hill on Sendai's western edge, Yagiyama Zoo houses a broad, if not exotic, range of animals including big cats, primates, elephants, and a variety of birds, laid out along shaded walking paths that make for a pleasant slow loop even without children in tow. A small, slightly retro amusement park sits adjacent, with modest rides aimed at younger kids, giving families an easy way to stretch a visit into most of a day.

What sets it apart from bigger city zoos is its low-key, neighborhood feel: it's clearly built for local families on a weekend outing rather than international tourism, and the hillside setting gives it some genuinely nice views over the surrounding forest, especially from the higher enclosures.

Admission is inexpensive, particularly for the zoo portion, and a relaxed visit with the amusement area takes three to four hours. Weekday mornings are quiet; weekends, especially with good weather, bring out plenty of local families, so early arrival helps if you want the paths to yourself. The wooded setting turns a good gold and red in November.

1 Yagiyama Honcho, Taihaku-ku, Sendai, Miyagi

Open in Google Maps

About 12 minutes by the Tozai subway line from Sendai Station to Yagiyama Zoological Park Station, right by the entrance; a bus from Sendai Station takes about 25 minutes.
