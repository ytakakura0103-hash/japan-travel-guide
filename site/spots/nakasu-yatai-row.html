Nakasu Yatai Row

Fukuoka

Along the west bank of the Naka River, dozens of small mobile food carts set up each evening, each seating maybe eight to a dozen people shoulder to shoulder along a narrow counter. Nakasu has the highest concentration of yatai in the city, known especially for ramen, yakitori skewers, and oden hot pot, and the neon-lit riverside setting makes it feel distinct from the more neighborhood-oriented Tenjin stalls.

Most stalls set up around 5:30pm and start serving by 6pm, running until around 1am, later on weekends. Cash is essentially mandatory; very few stalls take cards or mobile payment, so carrying enough yen matters more here than almost anywhere else in the city. A realistic budget is a few thousand yen per stall visited, and stall-hopping between two or three is a normal way to spend an evening.

Arriving right at opening, around 6 to 7pm, gets the shortest waits and a seat at the counter rather than standing behind someone eating. Etiquette matters at these tight counters: keep bags on your lap or under the stool rather than on an empty seat, which is reserved for the next customer, and don't bring in outside food or drink.

Nakasu, Hakata Ward, Fukuoka
