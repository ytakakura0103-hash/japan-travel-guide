Shiroishi Uji Somen Noodles

Sendai

Shiroishi has long been associated with a particular style of thin, hand-stretched somen noodles known locally as Uji somen, made using a traditional oil-free stretching technique passed down through small family-run producers around the town. It's a regional specialty distinct from somen made elsewhere in Japan, prized for its smooth, delicate texture.\n\nA noodle set at one of the town's small restaurants costs a modest amount, and some producers offer brief noodle-making demonstrations or tastings for visitors interested in the process itself.\n\nBecause it's a food specialty rather than a single ticketed site, it tends to get overlooked by visitors focused on the castle or the nearby fox village, even though sampling it takes little extra effort while already in town. It's traditionally served chilled with a dipping sauce in summer and in a warm broth, called nyumen, during colder months.
