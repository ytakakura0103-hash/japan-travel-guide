Nagoya Noh Theater

Nagoya

Noh is a slow, stylized form of masked theater dating back over six centuries, and unlike watching a shortened tourist-oriented demonstration, this municipal theater stages full performances on a proper cypress-wood stage built to traditional specifications, complete with the pine-tree backdrop and covered walkway prescribed by the art form's conventions. Performances aren't continuous, so a visit needs to be planned around the published schedule rather than dropped into casually.

Ticket prices vary depending on the program, and a full performance typically runs a couple of hours; some programs include a brief introduction aimed at newcomers unfamiliar with Noh's conventions, worth seeking out if this is your first exposure to the art form. The building itself, with its own garden and lobby, is worth a look even outside performance times if it happens to be open.

Because Noh has a genuinely devoted but relatively small local following rather than a mass tourist audience, performances rarely sell out to the point of difficulty, though popular programs featuring well-known performers are worth booking a little ahead of time.

1-1-1 Sannomaru, Naka-ku, Nagoya, Aichi 460-0001
