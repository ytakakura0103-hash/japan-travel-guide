LAGOON HIROSHIMA

Hiroshima

LAGOON HIROSHIMA opened in July 2024 in a basement space in Nagarekawa previously occupied by HERBIE, a club that had run for years before closing after well-documented financial difficulties, including a crowdfunding campaign that ultimately wasn't enough to keep it open. LAGOON's arrival in the same space reflects the ongoing churn typical of Japan's smaller regional club scenes, where a venue's closure often makes way for a new operator rather than the space sitting empty.

As one of the newer additions to Nagarekawa's club scene, its booking policy leans toward mainstream EDM and hip-hop aimed at a general night-out crowd, giving it a broadly accessible identity within Hiroshima's compact club landscape. Its central Nagarekawa location puts it within easy reach of the district's dense concentration of bars and izakaya.

Given how relatively recently it opened in its current form, checking the current event calendar before visiting is a reasonable precaution, as with most of Hiroshima's smaller, independent clubs where programming and even ownership can shift within a few years.

Nagarekawa 8-20, Naka Ward, Hiroshima
