Nissan Global Headquarters Gallery

Yokohama

Occupying the ground floor of Nissan's headquarters building near Yokohama Station, this gallery functions as both a corporate showcase and a genuinely popular free attraction, letting visitors get close to current production models, motorsport vehicles, and occasionally concept cars not yet on public sale. Car enthusiasts in particular treat it as a worthwhile stop, since the lineup includes performance models that are otherwise hard to see up close outside a dealership or motor show.\n\nThe specific vehicles on display rotate periodically, so what is shown on any given visit can vary, and it is worth treating the exact lineup as unpredictable rather than expecting a fixed set of cars. The building itself, with its tall glass atrium, is a piece of contemporary architecture in its own right, distinct from the historic Meiji and Taisho-era buildings found around Kannai.\n\nBecause admission is free and the space is compact, it works well as a short, low-commitment stop for visitors near Yokohama Station, particularly families with kids drawn to the cars, and it rarely feels crowded outside of any special events the company occasionally hosts there.

Takashima, Nishi-ku, Yokohama
