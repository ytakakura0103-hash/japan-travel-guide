Kyoto BAL

Kyoto

Kyoto BAL opened in 1970 and underwent a substantial renovation and reopening in 2015, emerging as a boutique-style shopping building rather than a conventional department store, spreading thirty-three curated shops across eight floors with high ceilings and wide aisles that give it more of an art-gallery feel than a typical retail interior. That design sensibility carries through the tenant mix, which leans toward considered, design-forward fashion and lifestyle brands rather than the broad, everything-under-one-roof approach of Kyoto's larger department stores.

Its two-basement-level Maruzen bookstore is a particular draw for English-speaking visitors and residents, carrying one of the strongest English-language book sections in the city, useful for anyone running low on reading material partway through a Japan trip. A MUJI store on the fifth floor and a Mariage Frères tea shop on the first round out a tenant list aimed at a design-literate, moderately upscale customer.

Its central Kawaramachi location makes it easy to combine with a visit to nearby Takashimaya, Nishiki Market, or the Kawaramachi nightlife district, and its smaller scale compared to a full department store makes it a manageable stop that doesn't require setting aside hours to see properly.

Kawaramachi, Nakagyo Ward, Kyoto
