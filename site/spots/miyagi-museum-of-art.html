Miyagi Museum of Art

Sendai

Sitting in the hills of Aoba-ku not far from Sendai Castle, this museum focuses its permanent collection on modern and contemporary Japanese art, with particular depth in artists who have some connection to the Tohoku region, alongside a smaller selection of international works. It's a useful counterpoint to the history-heavy sightseeing that dominates most Sendai itineraries, giving visitors a look at the region's creative output beyond samurai-era artifacts.

The building itself, with garden courtyards and quiet exhibition wings, is designed to be walked slowly rather than rushed through, and rotating special exhibitions supplement the permanent collection with themed or touring shows throughout the year. A children's gallery and workshop space also runs periodic hands-on programs, making it more family-friendly than a typical fine art museum.

Admission for the permanent collection is modest; special exhibitions usually carry a separate, moderate fee. The museum reopened in June 2026 after a multi-year renovation that added a visible art-storage viewing area and a kids studio; as with any municipal cultural institution, checking the current exhibition schedule before a visit is still worthwhile. It draws a fairly local crowd rather than tour groups, so even on weekends it rarely feels crowded, and a quiet weekday morning visit is easy to arrange.

34-1 Kawauchi Motohasekura, Aoba-ku, Sendai, Miyagi 980-0861
