Arima Fuji Park

Kobe

The park takes its name from Mt. Arima Fuji, a modest peak whose symmetrical cone shape earned it the nickname despite standing at only a fraction of the real Mt. Fuji's height, an easy climb of under an hour that rewards with wide views over the surrounding reservoir and countryside. The park itself wraps around Fukushima-ike reservoir, with lakeside paths, playgrounds and open lawns that make it a popular weekend spot for Sanda-area families rather than a sightseeing destination.

Birdwatchers also frequent the reservoir, which attracts a range of waterfowl depending on the season, and a small nature center near the park entrance provides information on the local ecology for anyone interested in going beyond a casual walk. The short summit hike is manageable in ordinary walking shoes, though the final stretch has some genuine incline.

Because Sanda sits inland from Kobe's usual tourist corridor and this park serves a mostly residential catchment, it's a useful example of an everyday recreational space rather than a curated attraction, worth visiting if you want a sense of ordinary suburban life around Kobe alongside the exercise and views.

Sanda, Hyogo Prefecture

From Sannomiya, take the JR Kobe Line to Osaka then the JR Fukuchiyama Line to Shin-Sanda, or the Kobe Municipal Subway/Shintetsu lines toward Sanda; a bus or short taxi connects to the park.
