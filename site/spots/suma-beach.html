Suma Beach

Kobe

Suma Beach has functioned as the Kobe area's principal swimming beach since the late 1800s, and its combination of soft sand, a pine-lined promenade, and views toward Awaji Island across the strait have kept it popular through generations of change in the surrounding neighborhood. During the official summer swimming season the beach fills with sunbathers, beach volleyball games, and seasonal food stalls, while outside that window it becomes a much quieter place for a walk or a sunset view.

The beach sits directly next to Kobe Suma Sea World, so the two make an easy pairing for a beach-and-aquarium day, and there are enough cafes and casual restaurants along the waterfront to cover a full day out without needing to leave the area. Facilities like showers and rentals are generally more available during the official swimming season than the rest of the year.

Summer weekends bring dense crowds, particularly from families and groups coming out from central Kobe and Osaka, so a weekday visit or an outside-summer walk gives a very different, much calmer experience of the same stretch of coast.
