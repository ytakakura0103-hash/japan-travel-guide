Nagarekawa District

Hiroshima

Nagarekawa is generally considered the largest entertainment district in the Chugoku region of western Japan, a compact but dense network of streets and multi-floor buildings just east of the Hondori shopping arcade. On any given evening it holds an enormous number of independent bars, izakaya, karaoke spots, and restaurants, ranging from tiny counters seating four or five people to larger multi-story izakaya aimed at group parties.

Because the area developed organically over decades rather than as a planned entertainment zone, the atmosphere varies block by block, with some streets leaning toward casual, tourist-friendly izakaya and others toward smaller, more local hostess bars and snack bars that are less approachable without a Japanese-speaking guide or regular introduction. For visitors, the more open izakaya and yakitori counters along the main streets are the easiest entry point, generally busiest from around 7pm on weeknights and later on weekends.

Nagarekawa sits close enough to Hondori and the Peace Memorial Park area that it works naturally as an evening stop after a day of sightseeing, though as with any dense nightlife district, it is worth being cautious of touts on the street offering unusually cheap all-you-can-drink deals.

Nagarekawa-cho, Naka Ward, Hiroshima, Hiroshima Prefecture
