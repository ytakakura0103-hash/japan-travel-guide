Oyunuma River Natural Footbath

Sapporo

The Oyunuma River carries hot spring water directly downstream from Noboribetsu's volcanic Jigokudani (Hell Valley), and a section of the riverbank has been set up with simple wooden benches so visitors can soak their feet in the naturally heated flowing water, no plumbing or construction involved beyond the seating. It's a genuinely different experience from a built footbath facility, since the water temperature and clarity shift slightly with the river's natural flow.

It's reachable either by a short bus-and-walk combination or via a roughly 30-minute forest walking trail from the main Noboribetsu Park Service Center near Hell Valley, which turns the footbath into a small hike rather than a quick stop if you choose that route. Admission is free, and the surrounding forest walk is a reasonable way to see more of the Noboribetsu volcanic landscape beyond the main Hell Valley boardwalks that most visitors stick to.

Because reaching it takes more effort than the main Hell Valley viewpoints, which are close to the bus terminal and hotel district, it draws a smaller crowd of visitors willing to walk further, and it's rarely as packed as the central geothermal sights.

Parking near the footbath closes in winter and trail conditions can be icy, so warmer months are the more reliable time to visit, though the water itself stays hot year-round.

About 1.5-2 hours from Sapporo: JR Limited Express to Noboribetsu Station (about 1 hour), then a bus toward Noboribetsu Onsen and the Ashiyu Iriguchi stop (about 15 minutes), followed by a short walk, or a roughly 30-minute forest trail walk from the Noboribetsu Park Service Center near Hell Valley.
