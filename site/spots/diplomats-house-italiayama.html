Diplomat's House, Italia Yama Garden

Yokohama

Originally built in Tokyo's Shibuya for a Japanese diplomat and his American wife, this wood-frame residence was moved to Yokohama's Yamate bluff in the 1990s and now anchors the small Italia Yama Garden, named for the site's earlier history as home to the Italian consulate. The clapboard exterior and wraparound veranda give it a distinctly American shingle-style look that stands apart from the Spanish-influenced houses elsewhere in Yamate.

The garden around it is compact but carefully planted, with a bulb-flower bed that rotates through tulips and other spring blooms, making it one of the better small-scale flower photo spots in the district when in season. Admission to the house itself is free.

Because Italia Yama Garden sits slightly downhill and off the main Harbor View Park route, it draws noticeably fewer visitors than the park's rose garden, even during flower season. It makes a pleasant, quick final stop on a Yamate walking loop before heading back down toward Ishikawacho Station, and the garden's benches are a good spot to rest before the walk back.

16 Yamate-cho, Naka-ku, Yokohama

About 5 minutes on foot from JR Negishi Line Ishikawacho Station.
