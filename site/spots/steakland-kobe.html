Steakland Kobe-kan

Kobe

Steakland has built a reputation as one of the more accessible places in central Kobe to try genuine, registered Kobe beef without needing a reservation weeks in advance or facing the highest-end pricing of the city's most exclusive steak houses. Seating is arranged around a teppanyaki grill counter, so diners watch the chef sear the steak, saute vegetables, and fry garlic chips a few feet away, which makes even a fairly quick lunch feel like a bit of a show.

Set menus typically bundle a portion of steak with rice, miso soup, pickles, salad, and grilled vegetables, and lunch sets are generally more affordable than the dinner menu for the same quality of beef, making a midday visit a reasonable way to try Kobe beef without a large budget. Because the kitchen moves quickly, turnover at the counter is fast even when there is a short line outside.

The restaurant is well known enough, including among Japanese diners, that queues are common around typical lunch and dinner hours; going right when it opens or slightly outside the standard meal windows generally means walking straight in rather than waiting.

Sannomiya Kogyo Building 6F, Chuo-ku, Kobe
