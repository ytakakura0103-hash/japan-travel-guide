Hiroshima Motomachi Aquarium

Hiroshima

Hiroshima Motomachi Aquarium opened in October 2025 inside the Pacela mall near Hiroshima Castle, bringing a genuinely theatrical approach to the aquarium format with a "Stage of Life" theme that runs through its roughly 66 tanks and 200 species. Rather than a straightforward educational layout, the exhibits are built around dramatic, artistic lighting design, giving the space a more atmospheric, almost gallery-like feel than a typical municipal aquarium.

That visual emphasis has made it an immediate draw for photography since opening, with the lighting design specifically calibrated to make each tank read well both in person and through a camera lens. Being located centrally within Pacela rather than on the waterfront gives it an unusually convenient downtown location compared to most Japanese aquariums, which tend to sit in dedicated waterfront facilities.

Its proximity to Hiroshima Castle makes it easy to combine with a visit to the castle grounds, letting a single outing cover both historical sightseeing and a distinctly different, more contemporary attraction just a short walk away.
