Noboribetsu Jigokudani (Hell Valley)

Hokkaido

Jigokudani, literally Hell Valley, is a volcanic crater a short walk above Noboribetsu Onsen, its surface pocked with steaming vents, bubbling pools and sulfur-stained rock that together supply the mineral-rich water feeding the town's many hot spring baths below. Wooden walkways loop through part of the crater, close enough to feel the heat and smell the sulfur without any real safety concern, making it accessible as a short, dramatic walk rather than a hike.

Noboribetsu Onsen itself is considered one of Hokkaido's premier hot spring towns partly because of the sheer variety of spring types feeding its baths, a rarity even by Japanese onsen standards, so combining the crater walk with an actual soak, either a day-use bath or an overnight ryokan stay, is the fuller version of the experience rather than the crater alone.

Entry to the crater viewing area is free and it's open year-round; visiting in early morning or later afternoon avoids the middle-of-the-day tour bus crowds that tend to converge with the timing of day-trip buses from Sapporo. Winter brings a striking contrast between the steaming vents and surrounding snow, along with a torch-lit evening walk event in the town during colder months, while summer offers easier, less icy walking conditions on the crater's wooden paths.
