Toei Kyoto Studio Park

Kyoto

Toei Kyoto Studio Park operates as both a working film and television backlot and a theme park open to the public, built around sets of recreated Edo-period streets, a merchant town, and a red-light district facade, all originally constructed for use in jidaigeki, Japan's genre of samurai and period dramas. Because filming still occasionally takes place on-site, visitors have a real, if not guaranteed, chance of walking past an actual shoot in progress, adding a layer of novelty on top of the sets themselves.

Beyond wandering the streets, the park runs live stunt and ninja shows at set times through the day, along with a few themed indoor attractions and a costume rental service that lets visitors dress as samurai, ninja, or geisha for photos around the sets. A full visit, taking in a show or two along with the sets, generally runs two to three hours; admission is charged, with some attractions and costume experiences priced separately on top of the base entry fee.

The park skews toward families and visitors interested in Japanese film and pop culture rather than a typical temple-focused itinerary, and it's usually busiest around midday when stunt shows draw crowds; arriving near opening allows more time to explore the sets before they fill in. It's a short walk from Uzumasa Station, somewhat removed from central Kyoto's main sightseeing loop.
