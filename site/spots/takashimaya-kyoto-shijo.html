Takashimaya Kyoto

Kyoto

Takashimaya's presence at the Shijo-Kawaramachi intersection traces back to 1829, when the company operated a textile and kimono business at this same site, and it grew into a full department store here as the Takashimaya brand expanded through the 20th century. The main building spans eight floors covering fashion, interiors, jewelry, and cosmetics, along with an art gallery and a crafts salon, and its seventh-floor dining garden, Kyo Kairo, is built in a traditional Kyoto-inflected modern design worth a look even without eating there.

A newer annex, T8, added seven floors above and one below to the complex, repositioning the whole site as "Kyoto Takashimaya S.C." The addition brought in a Nintendo Kyoto store and a large Tsutaya Bookstore, giving the department store a pop-culture and lifestyle dimension that sets it apart from Kyoto's more traditionally focused shopping options and appeals to a younger demographic than the main building's core customer base.

Sitting directly at Shijo-Kawaramachi, it's about as central as Kyoto shopping gets, within easy walking distance of Pontocho, Nishiki Market, and the Kawaramachi nightlife area. The basement depachika food hall is worth a stop even for visitors not planning to shop the fashion floors, offering a dense concentration of prepared foods and Kyoto-style sweets.

Shijo-Kawaramachi, Shimogyo Ward, Kyoto
