Local Pick

Cosmo Tower Sakishima

Osaka

Standing 252 metres tall in Osaka's waterfront Nanko district, Cosmo Tower's observatory offers a genuinely unobstructed 360-degree view, since its location away from the dense city centre means there are no comparably tall buildings nearby to block sightlines toward the bay, the mountains, or on clear days, back across the water toward central Osaka's skyline. Because the tower sits somewhat outside the usual tourist circuit, it tends to draw a fraction of the visitors that crowd the more central Umeda Sky Building or Harukas 300, making it a notably calmer option for visitors who want a comparable panoramic view without competing for space at the railings.

A visit to the observation floor takes around forty-five minutes to an hour. Admission is charged and generally priced below the city's more famous observation decks. Sunset remains a popular time here too, though even then the tower rarely reaches the crowd levels seen at more central options, so timing a visit around avoiding crowds matters less here than elsewhere in the city.

The tower is reached via the New Tram Nanko Port Town Line, an automated elevated train that is itself a mildly novel way to travel, adding a bit of interest to the journey out. Because of its location, it works best as a deliberate outing focused on the view itself rather than a stop combined with other central Osaka sightseeing.

1-14-16 Nankokita, Suminoe Ward, Osaka
