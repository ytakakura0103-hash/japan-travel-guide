Omotesando Oyster Stalls

Hiroshima

Hiroshima Bay produces roughly half of Japan's farmed oysters, and Miyajima's 350-meter Omotesando arcade, running from the ferry pier toward Itsukushima Shrine, is where much of that harvest gets grilled on the spot. Several small shops and street stalls along the street shuck and grill oysters over open charcoal right in front of customers, served simply with a squeeze of citrus or in variations like garlic butter and cheese.\n\nBeyond the classic grilled oyster, the arcade has also produced odder hybrids that locals treat with a mix of pride and amusement, including deep-fried oyster curry bread sold at a bakery a few doors down — a genuinely Miyajima-specific snack rather than a generic tourist treat.\n\nEating your way down Omotesando works well as a pre- or post-shrine activity, since the street sits directly between the ferry pier and Itsukushima Shrine's entrance; just budget extra time, since the smell of grilling oysters makes it hard to walk past without stopping at least once.

Miyajimacho, Hatsukaichi, Hiroshima
