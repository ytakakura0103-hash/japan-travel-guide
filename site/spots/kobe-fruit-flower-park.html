Kobe Fruit Flower Park

Kobe

Set in the more rural, hilly northern part of Kobe city rather than the harbor side most visitors picture, this park spreads flower fields, orchards, and a German-style windmill tower across open countryside that feels far removed from the dense city center. Depending on the season, visitors can pick strawberries, blueberries, or other fruit in dedicated orchard sections, while broad flower beds rotate through tulips, sunflowers, and other seasonal blooms through the year.

Because it is built around agricultural and seasonal activities rather than a single fixed attraction, what there is to do changes noticeably by the month, so timing a visit around a specific fruit season or flower bloom is worth checking ahead. A relaxed visit covering the grounds, a fruit-picking session, and a stop at the tower observation deck can fill much of an afternoon.

As a park oriented toward local families rather than international sightseers, it draws a distinctly different, more relaxed crowd than Kobe's harbor attractions, and even its busier fruit-picking weekends rarely feel as packed as the city's central sights.

2150 Kamiosawa, Osawacho, Kita-ku, Kobe

Open in Google Maps

About a 15-minute taxi ride from JR Sanda Station or Kobe Electric Railway Okaba Station; on weekends and holidays also reachable by a roughly 35-minute Shinki Bus (Route 38) ride from Sannomiya. A car or taxi is more convenient given its rural location.
