Local Pick

Hakata Ichibangai

Fukuoka

Tucked into the basement level beneath Hakata Station's modern concourses, this narrow underground arcade has a noticeably older, more worn-in atmosphere than the polished shopping floors above, with small counter-style shops crammed close together and low ceilings that add to the retro feel. Ramen dominates the food options, though a few other quick-eat counters are mixed in.

Because it's underground and easy to walk past without noticing the entrance, it tends to draw more local office workers and commuters grabbing a fast meal than tourists, which keeps the atmosphere unpretentious and the prices reasonable compared with dining upstairs in the main station building. Most counters seat only a handful of people, so a short wait at peak lunch or dinner hours is common.

It's an easy stop if you're already moving through Hakata Station, whether killing time before a Shinkansen departure or just looking for a quick, no-frills bowl of ramen away from the busier food floors above ground. Late morning, just before the lunch rush, tends to have the shortest waits.

Hakata Ekichuogai, Hakata Ward, Fukuoka
