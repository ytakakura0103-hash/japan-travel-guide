Hisaya-odori Park

Nagoya

This long park strip splits the wide Hisaya-odori boulevard down the middle of Sakae, Nagoya's central business and nightlife district. A renovation completed in recent years replaced older, more closed-off sections with open lawns, terraces, and low-key commercial pavilions housing cafes and shops, making it a far more inviting place to sit down than it used to be. The TV Tower rises at its northern end, giving the whole stretch a recognizable landmark to walk toward.

There's no cost to wander through, and it works well as a connector between Sakae's shopping streets rather than a single-stop destination — plan on twenty minutes to an hour depending on how much you linger. Illuminated fountains and seasonal light displays run in the evenings during certain months.

Lunchtime on weekdays brings office workers eating on the lawns, while weekends see families and events fill the space; an early morning walk offers the most peaceful view of the tower reflected in the fountains. Cherry blossoms along parts of the park bloom in early spring, drawing a modest crowd compared with the city's larger parks.
