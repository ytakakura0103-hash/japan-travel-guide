Nijigen no Mori

Kobe

Nijigen no Mori occupies a section of Awaji Island Park and takes a genre-mixing approach to the anime theme park format, dividing its grounds into distinct areas themed around different well-known franchises rather than committing to a single intellectual property. A Dragon Quest-themed island area lets visitors explore a physical recreation of the beloved game series' world, while a separate Godzilla zone includes a zipline course built around a giant Godzilla installation, letting visitors literally fly past the monster's head.

Naruto and Crayon Shin-chan-themed areas round out the park's offering, giving it a broader appeal across different anime and manga fan bases than a park built around a single franchise. That variety has helped it remain one of Awaji Island's biggest draws well after its initial opening, continuing to expand and add attractions rather than settling into a fixed lineup.

Getting there requires a bus connection from Kobe or Sannomiya to Awaji Island, making it a deliberate day-trip destination rather than a quick stop, but it pairs naturally with the island's other attractions covered elsewhere in this guide, including Awaji Yumebutai and the Hyakudanen flower terraces, for a fuller day exploring Awaji.
