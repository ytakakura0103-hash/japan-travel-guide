Showa Kinen Park

Tokyo

Showa Kinen Park occupies the former grounds of a U.S. air base in Tachikawa, and its scale reflects that history — this is one of the largest parks in the greater Tokyo area, with wide cycling paths, open lawns, a children's play area with large slides, and enough space that crowds rarely feel dense even on a busy weekend. The park's seasonal flower displays have become its main draw for visitors from central Tokyo: a hillside blanketed in vivid blue nemophila blooms each spring, followed later in the year by cosmos fields, and an avenue of ginkgo trees that turns a deep gold in autumn, considered one of the best in the region for that particular display.

Given the park's size, a visit can range from an hour for a quick walk near one of the flower fields to a full day if you plan to explore multiple areas, rent a bicycle, or bring a picnic to one of the large lawns. Weekday mornings are the easiest time to enjoy the flower fields without crowds; the nemophila and ginkgo seasons in particular draw large weekend crowds, with photography enthusiasts arriving early for the best light.

Admission is charged at the gate and generally considered reasonable for a park of this size. Bicycle rental is available inside if you'd rather cover more ground than a walk allows.

3173 Midoricho, Tachikawa, Tokyo

Open in Google Maps

10-minute walk from Nishi-Tachikawa Station (JR Ome Line)
