Mount Moiwa Ropeway

Hokkaido

Mount Moiwa rises directly at the southwestern edge of central Sapporo, close enough that the ropeway ride up takes only a few minutes yet delivers a full view over the city grid, Ishikari Bay, and the surrounding mountains. A short cable car covers the lower slope, followed by a smaller mini cable car for the final stretch to the summit observation deck, which is a slightly more involved journey than a single ropeway but adds some novelty.

The night view here is frequently mentioned alongside Hakodate's as one of Hokkaido's best, and pairs of padlocks left by couples at the summit ("lovers' sanctuary") point to its popularity as a date spot. During the Sapporo Snow Festival and other winter events, the city lights below take on an extra sparkle against the snow.

Sunset and the twenty to thirty minutes after it tend to be the most crowded window, since that's when the transition from daylight to city lights is most photogenic; going up earlier in the afternoon for daylight views or well after dark for a settled night view are both quieter alternatives. Bring warm layers even in summer, since the summit is noticeably cooler and windier than street level, and expect the mountain to be closed to hiking trails, though not the ropeway, during heavy snow.

5 Chome Fushimi, Chuo-ku, Sapporo, Hokkaido
