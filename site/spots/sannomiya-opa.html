Sannomiya OPA & OPA 2

Kobe

Sannomiya OPA and its larger neighbor, Sannomiya OPA 2, sit immediately by Sannomiya Station's east exit and together represent two of only three OPA-brand stores still operating anywhere in the Kansai region, alongside Kawaramachi OPA in Kyoto. The original Sannomiya OPA traces back to 1969, though it operated under the name Sannomiya VIVRE for years before being renamed in 2019; its five floors skew toward women's fashion across roughly twenty-two shops.

Sannomiya OPA 2, which opened in 2017, is the larger of the two, with floors two through seven given over to fashion and lifestyle specialty shops, nearly fifty in total, several of which opened their first-ever Japan, Kansai, or Hyogo Prefecture locations here. Its eighth and ninth floors are dedicated entirely to dining, giving the building a genuine food destination alongside its retail floors.

Being one minute's walk from Sannomiya Station's east ticket gate, the pair function as one of the most convenient shopping stops in the district for anyone arriving by train, and their status as OPA holdouts in a shrinking brand footprint gives them a bit of retail-history interest beyond the shopping itself.
