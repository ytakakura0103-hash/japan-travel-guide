Fukuoka Art Museum

Fukuoka

Fukuoka Art Museum occupies a lakeside site at the edge of Ohori Park and holds a collection spanning historic Buddhist art and craft objects alongside a modern and contemporary wing that includes well-known 20th-century Western artists as well as Japanese and Asian contemporary work. The split between old and new across the two collection areas gives visitors a fairly broad overview in a single visit.

The building underwent a significant renovation in recent years, and the surrounding grounds connect directly into Ohori Park's walking paths, making a stop here an easy extension of a park visit rather than a separate trip. A cafe and museum shop are on site for a break between galleries.

Weekday visits tend to be noticeably quieter than weekends, particularly during special touring exhibitions, which can draw larger crowds than the permanent collection alone. Parking near the museum fills up quickly on weekends and holidays, so arriving by subway or bus is generally the smoother option during peak times.

1-6 Ohorikoen, Chuo Ward, Fukuoka

Open in Google Maps

10-minute walk from Ohori-Koen Station, or about 5 minutes from Tenjin by bus
