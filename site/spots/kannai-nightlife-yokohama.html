Local Pick

Kannai (evening)

Yokohama

Kannai's daytime identity, built around office towers and government buildings on the site of Yokohama's former foreign settlement, gives little indication of what the district looks like after dark. Tucked into the narrow alleys behind the main streets is a surprisingly dense concentration of retro snack bars, karaoke lounges, and small izakaya, informal enough in layout that the area functions much like a yokocho even without carrying that name officially.

Compared to the more curated, English-friendly nightlife of central Minatomirai, Kannai's evening scene is distinctly local, built around office workers finishing a shift rather than visitors, and menus and signage are more likely to be Japanese-only. That makes it a rewarding area for visitors specifically looking for a less filtered slice of Yokohama's after-work drinking culture, best approached with a willingness to point at a menu or follow the crowd into whichever alley looks busiest.

Its central location, walkable from Bashamichi, Nihon-Odori, and Yokohama Stadium, makes it easy to combine with sightseeing earlier in the day or a game at the stadium, sliding naturally into an evening exploring the alleys once the sun goes down.
