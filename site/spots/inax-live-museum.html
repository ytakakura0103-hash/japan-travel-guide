Local Pick

INAX Live Museum

Nagoya

Run by the housing and tile manufacturer LIXIL, this museum complex centers on a genuine registered heritage kiln, a brick chimney structure that once fired ceramic pipes, preserved in place rather than reconstructed. Surrounding buildings house a tile museum with an eclectic global collection, a clay-working studio, and hands-on workshops where visitors can decorate or shape their own small ceramic piece to take home.

Admission covers the main exhibition halls, with workshops priced separately and sometimes requiring a same-day sign-up depending on availability. A visit covering the museum buildings takes about an hour and a half; adding a workshop extends that further.

It draws a small, steady stream of visitors rather than crowds, even on weekends, making it a relaxed stop to pair with a walk along the nearby pottery footpath. The workshops are popular with families, so booking or arriving early on weekends is worth doing if a hands-on activity is the goal.

50 Okuei-cho, Tokoname, Aichi
