Fukuoka Tower

Fukuoka

Standing roughly 234 meters tall on the Momochi waterfront, Fukuoka Tower is often described as Japan's tallest tower built directly on a coastline, and its exterior of half-mirrored glass panels gives it a distinct, faceted look that changes with the light throughout the day. The observation deck offers views back across the city toward Tenjin and Hakata as well as out over Hakata Bay.

Late afternoon into early evening is generally considered the best window to visit, since it allows for daylight views followed by sunset and then the city lights coming on, essentially two experiences in one ticket. The tower stays open into the evening, though last entry is typically earlier than closing time, so check current hours before planning a sunset visit specifically.

It sits within walking distance of Momochi Seaside Park and PayPay Dome, making it easy to combine into a half-day covering the whole Momochi waterfront area. Weekends and holiday evenings draw the largest crowds to the observation deck; a weekday visit offers more room to actually enjoy the view.

2-3-26 Momochihama, Sawara Ward, Fukuoka

About a 20-minute walk from Nishijin or Fujisaki subway stations, or a direct bus from Tenjin or Hakata Station
