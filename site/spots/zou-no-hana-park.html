Zou no Hana Park

Yokohama

Long before Osanbashi or the Red Brick Warehouse existed, this curved breakwater was the original point where Yokohama's port opened to foreign trade in 1859, and its distinctive bowed shape earned it the nickname elephant's trunk, which the park's name literally means. The current park recreates the mid-Meiji-period shape of the pier, reopened in 2009 to mark the port's 150th anniversary, and walking its curve gives a tangible sense of the port's original scale before all the later landfill and expansion.\n\nAt the park's center, a small rest house doubles as a café and occasional art space, serving simple food and drinks with a view back toward the harbor, a low-key spot to sit rather than just pass through. The open lawns nearby are popular for casual picnics among nearby office workers on lunch breaks.\n\nBecause it sits between the more heavily marketed Osanbashi Pier and Yamashita Park, many visitors walk right past without realizing its historical significance as the actual birthplace of the port. It is a worthwhile pause on the walk between Nihon Odori and Yamashita Park, with good sightlines for photos of the bay and passing ships.

Kaigan-dori 1-chome, Naka-ku, Yokohama

Open in Google Maps

About 5 minutes on foot from Minatomirai Line Nihon-odori Station.
