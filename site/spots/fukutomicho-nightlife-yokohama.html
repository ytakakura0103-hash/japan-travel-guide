Fukutomicho

Yokohama

Fukutomicho sits just south of Kannai Station and carries a noticeably edgier character than the more polished streets of central Yokohama nearby, packed with snack bars, karaoke lounges, and a handful of nightclubs that together make up much of the city's modest dance-club scene. Unlike Tokyo or Osaka, Yokohama was never known for a large nightclub district, and Fukutomicho functions as roughly the closest thing the city has to one, on a much smaller scale.

The area's club scene turns over quickly, with venues opening, closing, and rebranding more often than the bars and snack lounges around them, so specific club names and current programming are worth confirming close to a visit rather than assuming a fixed lineup. That said, the neighborhood's overall character as Yokohama's small nightclub pocket has held steady even as individual venues have come and gone.

Its proximity to Kannai and Bashamichi makes it easy to fold into a broader evening across central Yokohama's nightlife areas, moving from a quieter drink in Bashamichi to Fukutomicho's livelier, later-running scene as the night goes on.
