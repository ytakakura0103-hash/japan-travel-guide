Kakuozan Shopping Street

Nagoya

Kakuozan retains a shitamachi, old-town feel that's increasingly rare closer to central Nagoya — narrow storefronts selling traditional wagashi sweets, secondhand kimono, and household goods have coexisted here for decades, and in recent years a wave of small independent cafes and bakeries has moved in without erasing that older character. The street runs conveniently between the subway station and Nittaiji Temple, so the two make a natural pairing.

There's nothing to pay just to walk through, and browsing plus a cafe stop typically runs an hour or two. Prices at the cafes and sweet shops are generally reasonable, closer to neighborhood spots than tourist-priced venues.

The street is calm even on weekends compared with Osu or Sakae, making it a good antidote if you want a slower-paced shopping experience. Autumn is a particularly good season to combine a visit here with the nearby temple grounds, where foliage color adds to the old-town atmosphere.

Kakuozan-dori, Chikusa-ku, Nagoya, Aichi
