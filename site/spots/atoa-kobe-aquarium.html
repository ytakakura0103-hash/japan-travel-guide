atoa

Kobe

atoa takes a deliberately theatrical approach to the aquarium format, pairing marine life exhibits inside the Kobe Port Museum with immersive digital art installations that blur the line between a conventional aquarium and a contemporary art space. Since opening in 2021, it's built a strong following, passing two million visitors and continuing to add new programming rather than settling into a static exhibit lineup.

A keeper-led backstage tour, launched in April 2025 for visitors aged seven and up, gives a behind-the-scenes look at the facility's daily animal care routines, adding a hands-on educational dimension beyond simply viewing tanks. A rooftop deck with harbor views rounds out the visit, connecting the aquarium's indoor exhibits back to Kobe's waterfront setting outside.

Its location within the Kobe Port Museum near Meriken Park makes it easy to combine with a broader waterfront day covering Kobe Port Tower and Meriken Park itself, all within walking distance of each other. Given its focus on atmosphere and art as much as marine biology, it suits visitors looking for something more immersive than a standard aquarium visit.
