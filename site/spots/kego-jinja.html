Local Pick

Kego Shrine

Fukuoka

Kego Shrine has stood in what is now Fukuoka's busiest commercial district for over a thousand years, predating the department stores and cafes that now surround it on nearly every side. Its unassuming stone torii gate opens directly onto a Tenjin side street, and stepping through it drops the ambient noise of the shopping district almost immediately, thanks to a small stand of trees around the modest main hall.

Because it has no famous festival or headline artifact, it rarely appears on standard sightseeing lists, despite sitting closer to central Tenjin than almost any other shrine in the city. Locals stop by for quick prayers before work or between errands, and the shrine's understated presence makes it feel more like a neighborhood fixture than an attraction.

A visit takes only ten or fifteen minutes, which makes it an easy way to break up a day of shopping between Tenjin's malls and Daimyo's boutiques. It is at its best during the New Year period, when queues form for hatsumode despite the shrine's small footprint, and during any of the shopping district's evening hours, when its quiet grounds contrast sharply with the neon outside.

2-2-20 Tenjin, Chuo-ku, Fukuoka

About 2 minutes on foot from Tenjin Station, just off the main shopping streets.
