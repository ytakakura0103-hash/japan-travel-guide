Tochoji Temple

Fukuoka

Tochoji is said to have been founded by the monk Kukai on his return from China, and today its main draw is the Great Buddha Hall, where a roughly 11-meter seated wooden Buddha looms over a backdrop of thousands of miniature Buddha statues. The scale is genuinely startling for a temple tucked into an ordinary Hakata side street.

Beneath the statue runs the so-called Hell and Paradise tunnel, a completely dark passage you feel your way through by touch; reaching a carved wheel partway along is said to bring good fortune. Entry to the main grounds and Buddha hall is free, with only a small coin fee for the tunnel walk, so bring some loose change since no change is given inside.

The temple opens early, and arriving close to opening time means having the courtyard and its five-story pagoda largely to yourself before coach groups start arriving mid-morning. Two to three hours is enough to see the Buddha hall, walk the tunnel, and sit with the pagoda without feeling rushed.

2-4 Gokushomachi, Hakata Ward, Fukuoka

Open in Google Maps

1-minute walk from Gion Station (Kuko Line), or about a 10-minute walk from Hakata Station
