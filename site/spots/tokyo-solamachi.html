Tokyo Solamachi

Tokyo

Tokyo Solamachi is the shopping and dining complex built around the base of Tokyo Skytree, spread across 13 floors, from three basement levels up to the 30th and 31st floors, and holding more than 300 stores. It's organized into loosely themed zones rather than one undifferentiated mall floor: a shotengai-style "Solamachi Shopping Street" section for a more traditional market feel, dedicated souvenir and character-goods floors, and a food marché alongside standard fashion and lifestyle retail.

The standout dining option is Solamachi Dining Sky Tree View, seated around 150 meters up, which pairs restaurant meals with close-up views of the tower itself and the surrounding cityscape, different from the observation deck experience higher up in the tower proper. Because the complex sits directly beneath Skytree and shares its station access, it works naturally as a combined visit: tower observation deck for the view, Solamachi afterward (or before) for food and shopping without needing to relocate.

Given its size, treat a visit as requiring genuine time rather than a quick pass-through if the goal is to see much of it, and expect it to be busy on weekends and around Skytree's own peak visiting hours. It's also one of the more family-friendly large malls in Tokyo, with enough variety in food and shopping to occupy a full afternoon on its own even without going up the tower.

1-1-2 Oshiage, Sumida City, Tokyo 131-0045
