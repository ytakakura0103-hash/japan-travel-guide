teamLab Botanical Garden Osaka

Osaka

Rather than building a dedicated venue, teamLab worked with the existing plant collection inside Nagai Park's botanical garden, layering light projections, interactive digital installations, and ambient sound directly onto the trees, paths, and pond that were already there. The result reads less like a gallery show and more like the garden itself has been reprogrammed for the evening, with different themed zones scattered along the walking route rather than a single central installation.

The exhibition only runs after dark and only during specific seasonal windows rather than year-round, so checking the current published dates before planning a visit is essential; the garden has previously closed for maintenance stretches between seasons and reopened on a new schedule. A full visit typically takes an hour to ninety minutes at a comfortable pace, longer for anyone stopping frequently for photos, and comfortable walking shoes are worth it given the garden's uneven natural paths.

Because the installations rely on darkness for effect, arriving close to opening time gives the most comfortable experience before crowds build, particularly on weekends. It pairs naturally with a daytime visit to Nagai Park itself or the Osaka Museum of Natural History, both in the same park complex, letting a single trip cover the park's daytime and nighttime sides.

Nagai Park, Higashisumiyoshi Ward, Osaka

Open in Google Maps

About 5 minutes on foot from Nagai Station (Osaka Metro Midosuji Line) or Nagai Park itself.
