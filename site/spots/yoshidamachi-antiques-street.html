Yoshidamachi Antique Street

Yokohama

Sitting just off the busier Isezakicho shopping mall, Yoshidamachi has a long-standing identity as an artists' and collectors' street, home to secondhand bookshops, print and art-supply dealers, and a cluster of antique and curio shops that draw a very different crowd than the mainstream retail nearby. Many of the shops have operated for decades under the same families, and browsing here feels more like flipping through someone's specialized collection than shopping a curated tourist market.\n\nBecause the merchandise skews toward used goods, antique ceramics, old prints, secondhand cameras, vintage kimono fabric, prices and quality vary shop to shop, and there is real reward in taking time to actually look rather than rushing through. Cash is the safer bet at smaller, older stores that may not take cards.\n\nAlmost no English signage exists along this street, which keeps it firmly a locals' shopping district rather than a tourist stop, and that is precisely the appeal for visitors who enjoy browsing over being sold to. It pairs naturally with a walk through Isezakicho, letting you contrast Yoshidamachi's specialist, slow-browse character with the busier main shopping mall a block or two away.

Yoshidamachi, Naka-ku, Yokohama

About 7 minutes on foot from Keikyu Hinodecho Station.
