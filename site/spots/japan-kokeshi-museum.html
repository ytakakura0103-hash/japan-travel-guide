Japan Kokeshi Museum

Sendai

Kokeshi dolls originated in the onsen towns of the Tohoku region, including Naruko, where craftsmen originally turned them as simple wooden toys for bathers passing through the hot spring resorts. This museum traces that history through an extensive collection of dolls made in different regional styles, along with tools and explanations of the traditional lathe-turning technique.

Admission is modest, and a full visit including the exhibits takes around an hour. A hands-on painting experience is usually available for an additional fee, letting visitors decorate their own blank kokeshi to take home.

Compared to Naruko's hot spring baths, which draw the bulk of the town's visitors, the museum is a relatively quiet stop, making it easy to take your time with the painting experience without a queue. It works well as an indoor activity on a rainy day or a cold winter afternoon.
