Spa LaQua

Tokyo

Spa LaQua is a hot-spring bathing facility inside the Tokyo Dome City entertainment complex, drawing genuine onsen water pumped from roughly 1,700 meters underground rather than using heated tap water, which is unusual for a facility this central in Tokyo. The bathing areas include a mix of indoor pools, an open-air rotenburo bath, and various sauna rooms, along with a separate relaxation lounge with reclining chairs where visitors can rest, nap, or read between soaks, since a day pass typically allows extended stays rather than a quick in-and-out visit.

Admission is modest but not cheap by typical bathhouse standards, reflecting its central Tokyo location, and towels, robes, and basic toiletries are usually included or available to rent, so visitors don't need to bring much beyond a swimsuit for the small number of mixed-gender areas (the main baths themselves are gender-separated). As at most Japanese bathing facilities, visitors with tattoos should check current policy in advance, since some areas may restrict or require covering them.

Because it sits inside the wider Tokyo Dome City complex, which includes an amusement park, shops, and restaurants, it's easy to combine a soak with a broader day out. Weekday daytime visits tend to be quieter than evenings and weekends, when the lounge in particular can fill up. Plan for at least two to three hours to make the visit worthwhile.
