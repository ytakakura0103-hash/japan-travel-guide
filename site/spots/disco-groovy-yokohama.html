Local Pick

DISCO Groovy

Yokohama

DISCO Groovy occupies a modest basement space in a Bashamichi office building and commits fully to a specific concept: an authentic 1980s-style disco, complete with the era's music, lighting, and dance-floor culture, rather than the DJ-led electronic club format that dominates most of Japan's nightlife today. At roughly thirty tsubo with seating for around forty-two, it's an intimate room, closer in scale to a neighborhood bar than a large club, which suits its focused, adult-oriented atmosphere.

That commitment to a specific era and format has made it something of a rarity, drawing both nostalgic regulars who remember the original disco boom and younger visitors curious about a scene they've only seen in period films or photos. The crowd skews older than a typical club night, and the atmosphere leans toward genuine dancing rather than a standing-and-drinking format.

Its central location, equidistant from Bashamichi, Nihon-Odori, and Kannai Stations, makes it an easy stop regardless of which part of central Yokohama a visitor is exploring. Given its specific, era-defined concept, it suits visitors curious about that particular slice of nightlife history more than anyone looking for current chart hits or mainstream club programming.

Honmachi Central Building B1F, Naka Ward, Yokohama
