JR Hakata City & Hakata Hankyu

Fukuoka

Hakata Hankyu occupies roughly 42,000 square meters across nine floors of the JR Hakata City complex, which rebuilt and reopened around Hakata Station in March 2011. As the department store anchor of the station complex, it puts a full Hankyu-branded shopping experience directly at one of Kyushu's busiest transit hubs, convenient for anyone passing through the station regardless of their final destination.

Its ground floor covers cosmetics and accessories, typical of a department store's most heavily trafficked level, while the basement food hall leans specifically into Kyushu regional specialty goods, a useful one-stop option for gift shopping before catching a train out of the city. That regional focus distinguishes it somewhat from the more broadly national offerings at Tenjin's department stores across town.

Its direct connection to AMU Plaza Hakata, already covered elsewhere in this guide, means the two complexes function almost as one continuous shopping experience, letting visitors move between department store shopping and AMU Plaza's own retail and dining without leaving the station building. Given its station location, expect heavier foot traffic during commuter rush hours than at Tenjin's standalone department stores.
