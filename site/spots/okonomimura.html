Okonomimura

Hiroshima

Hiroshima-style okonomiyaki differs from the more familiar Osaka version by layering its ingredients, usually cabbage, pork, and a generous portion of fried noodles, rather than mixing the batter together, and it is cooked in stages directly on a large iron griddle in front of the customer. Okonomimura brings together roughly two dozen independent okonomiyaki stalls across three floors of a single building in the Shintenchi entertainment district, each run by its own small team with slightly different techniques and toppings.

Because each stall seats only a handful of people around its griddle counter, the atmosphere is informal and conversational, with cooks often chatting with customers while they work. There is no single "best" stall in any official sense, and regulars tend to have their own favorites based on things like noodle texture or how much sauce is used, so first-time visitors are usually better off picking whichever stall has an open seat rather than searching for one specific shop.

The building can get busy during lunch and dinner hours, particularly with tourists, and most stalls are cash-friendly with simple picture menus, though English support varies stall to stall.

5-13 Shintenchi, Naka Ward, Hiroshima, Hiroshima Prefecture

5-minute walk from Ebisucho or Hatchobori tram stops, in the Shintenchi district.
