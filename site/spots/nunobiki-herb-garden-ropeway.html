Nunobiki Herb Garden & Ropeway

Kobe

The Shin-Kobe Ropeway carries cabins up the slope of Mount Rokko in just a few minutes, and the ride itself offers sweeping views back over the city and harbor that are worth the trip on their own. At the top, Nunobiki Herb Garden spreads across a series of terraced slopes planted with seasonal flowers and herbs, along with greenhouses, a small garden restaurant, and a viewing platform that is popular for sunset.

Most visitors treat this as a half-day outing: the ropeway ride up, an hour or more wandering the garden terraces, and either a walk back down through the Nunobiki Falls trail or the ropeway back to the base station. Flower displays change with the seasons, with roses generally at their best in late spring and early autumn, and some sections lit up in the evening during limited seasonal illumination events.

Weekday mornings are the calmest time to ride up, since weekend afternoons can mean a wait for ropeway cabins, especially during peak flower or illumination periods. Combining the descent on foot past Nunobiki Falls is a good way to see two attractions in one trip without paying for the ropeway twice.

Kitanochodori area (mountain side of Shin-Kobe Station), Chuo-ku, Kobe
