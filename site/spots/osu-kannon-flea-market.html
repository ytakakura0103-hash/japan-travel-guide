Local Pick

Osu Kannon Flea Market

Nagoya

On certain fixed days each month, the plaza around Osu Kannon fills with dealers laying out secondhand kimono and obi, old coins and banknotes, ceramics, vintage toys, and general household curios on tarps and folding tables. It's a working antiques market for local collectors more than a staged tourist event, and prices are often genuinely negotiable if you're willing to try a bit of polite haggling.

Browsing is free, and how much you spend is entirely up to what catches your eye; an hour is enough to circle the stalls, though collectors can easily spend longer digging through boxes. Cash is the safest bet, since many individual dealers won't take cards.

The market naturally overlaps with the temple's own foot traffic on those market days, so mornings tend to have the best selection before dealers start packing smaller or rarer items away. Checking the specific market dates before visiting is worth doing, since it doesn't run every day and a mistimed visit just finds an empty plaza.

2-21-47 Osu, Naka-ku, Nagoya, Aichi
