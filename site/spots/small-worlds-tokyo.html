Small Worlds Tokyo

Tokyo

Small Worlds Tokyo is a miniature-model theme park where entire neighborhoods and fictional worlds are recreated at a consistent 1:80 scale, detailed enough to include tiny moving trains, cars, and even flickering lights inside individual buildings. The exhibits are organized into distinct zones, including a miniature version of Kansai International Airport with model aircraft taxiing and taking off, a recreation of a retro Tokyo neighborhood, and licensed sections built around popular anime series, which rotate or expand periodically as new collaborations are added.

The level of detail rewards slow looking rather than a quick walkthrough; small figures are posed in scenes throughout each diorama, and the moving elements, particularly the trains and aircraft, run on set schedules that are worth timing a visit around if you want to see a particular sequence in motion. Photography is generally allowed and encouraged, and the low-angle model views make for distinctive photos that read almost like real aerial photography of a miniature city.

A visit typically takes an hour to ninety minutes, longer for visitors who want to watch each moving sequence play out. Because the space is indoor and moderate in size, it doesn't experience the same kind of crowding as Tokyo's largest attractions, though weekends and school holidays bring more families with children. It's located in the Ariake area, within reach of other Odaiba-area attractions for a combined day out.

1-3-33 Ariake, Koto City, Tokyo
