Local Pick

Motomachi Park

Yokohama

Tucked into the slope where the Motomachi shopping street climbs toward the Yamate hilltop, this park mixes ordinary neighborhood facilities, tennis courts, a pool, playgrounds, with quieter traces of the area's history as part of Yokohama's 19th-century foreign settlement, including old stone foundations that once supported residences in the district. It is a genuinely local park, used as much by nearby residents for daily exercise as by visitors passing through.

Because it sits just off the main Motomachi shopping street but requires a short climb, most shoppers below never make the detour up, which keeps it noticeably quieter than the crowded Motomachi and Chinatown streets at the bottom of the hill. It offers a shaded, green rest stop partway through the walk up to Yamate's Western-style houses, useful on a hot afternoon.

Spring brings cherry blossoms along parts of the park, drawing a modest local crowd rather than the huge gatherings seen at bigger citywide blossom spots. For visitors walking the Yamate bluff historic district, treating this park as a stopping point on the way up, rather than skipping straight to Harbor View Park, adds a quieter, more local layer to the walk.
