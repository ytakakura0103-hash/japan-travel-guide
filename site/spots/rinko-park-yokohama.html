Local Pick

Rinko Park

Yokohama

Sandwiched between two much more famous attractions, this compact park rarely appears on first-time visitor itineraries, which is exactly what makes it worth a short detour. Its sloped lawn faces the harbor entrance directly, giving an open view across the water toward the Marine Tower and passing ships without the benches and paths of Yamashita Park being nearly as full.

It works well as a quiet place to sit with a coffee or a snack picked up from the Red Brick Warehouse, especially at lunchtime when nearby attractions are at their busiest. The park has no facilities of its own beyond basic seating and paths, so it functions purely as a rest stop rather than a destination with things to do.

Because it sits directly on the walking route between the Red Brick Warehouse and Osanbashi Pier, most visitors pass along its edge without ever stepping onto the grass, so simply taking the extra minute to walk into the park itself, rather than along the paved path skirting it, is usually enough to find a quieter spot with a good harbor view, particularly around sunset.

1-1-1 Minatomirai, Nishi-ku, Yokohama

About a 5-minute walk from Minatomirai Station (Minatomirai Line), or 15 minutes from Sakuragicho Station (JR / Yokohama Municipal Subway Blue Line).
