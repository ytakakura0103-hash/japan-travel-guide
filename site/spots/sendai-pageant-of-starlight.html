SENDAI Pageant of Starlight

Sendai

For a few weeks each December, the same zelkova trees that shade Jozenji-dori in summer are wrapped in warm-white LED lights, transforming the boulevard into one long illuminated tunnel after dark. It's Sendai's signature winter event and has been running in some form since the 1980s, drawing both couples on dates and families out for an evening walk.

The display runs along Jozenji-dori and the neighboring Chuo-dori, so a full walk of the illuminated stretch takes around thirty minutes, though most people linger far longer taking photos or stopping at the food stalls that pop up alongside it. Nearby cafes get cozy and busy during the run, making it an easy pairing with a hot drink.

Viewing is free and the lights are on every evening through the festival period, typically switching on around dusk. The opening weekend and the two weekends closest to Christmas are the most crowded; a weeknight visit, or arriving right at lighting time before the after-dinner crowd builds, is noticeably calmer. Dress warmly, since Sendai winter evenings are cold and the appeal is entirely about walking outside.

Jozenji-dori and Chuo-dori, Aoba-ku, Sendai, Miyagi

Open in Google Maps

A 15-minute walk from Sendai Station, or accessible via Kotodai-Koen Station on the Namboku subway line.
