Yokohama Nigiwaiza

Yokohama

Opened in the early 2000s specifically to give Yokohama a home-grown venue for rakugo, the seated storytelling art where a single performer conjures an entire cast of characters through voice and gesture alone, Nigiwaiza fills a niche that most first-time Japan visitors do not know exists in Yokohama at all. The main hall follows a traditional layout, and the building also houses smaller rooms used for other stage arts like manzai comic duos and taiko performances.

Performances are almost entirely in Japanese, so the experience rewards visitors who either have some language ability or are happy to enjoy the rhythm, comic timing, and physical performance even without following every word, much like watching a skilled mime. Programs rotate regularly, and tickets for popular performers can sell out, so checking the schedule ahead of a visit is worthwhile.

Sitting right in the Noge neighborhood, it is an easy stop to combine with the Miyakobashi bar alley or Nogeyama Zoo for an afternoon-into-evening loop through one of Yokohama's most lived-in districts, well away from the souvenir shops of Chinatown or the Red Brick Warehouse. Matinee shows tend to be less crowded and often cheaper than evening slots.

2-40 Miyagawa-cho, Naka-ku, Yokohama

Open in Google Maps

About 5 minutes on foot from JR/Yokohama Municipal Subway Sakuragicho Station.
