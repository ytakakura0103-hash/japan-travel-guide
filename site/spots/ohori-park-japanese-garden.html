Ohori Park Japanese Garden

Fukuoka

Created in the 1980s to mark an anniversary of Ohori Park's founding, this enclosed Japanese garden sits within the larger park but is a separately ticketed, gated space, so it stays noticeably calmer than the open lakeside paths outside its whitewashed earthen walls. A traditional strolling-garden layout leads visitors around a central pond, past artificial hills, a tea house, and carefully placed stone lanterns.

Admission is inexpensive, and English-language pamphlets are usually available for overseas visitors, which helps make sense of the garden's design principles for anyone unfamiliar with Japanese garden conventions. The garden is closed on Mondays, so it's worth checking the schedule before building it into an itinerary.

Seasonal color changes are part of the appeal, from spring blossoms to vivid maple foliage in autumn, and the garden tends to be quietest on weekday mornings right after opening. It pairs naturally with a walk around the rest of Ohori Park and a stop at Fukuoka Castle ruins nearby.

1-7 Ohorikoen, Chuo Ward, Fukuoka
