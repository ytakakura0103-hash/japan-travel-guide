Hozugawa River Boat Ride

Kyoto

The Hozugawa river boat ride carries passengers down a roughly 16-kilometer stretch of gorge from Kameoka to Arashiyama, using traditional wooden boats poled and paddled by a small crew of boatmen employing techniques that have been passed down since the Edo period, when the river was a working transport route for timber rather than a sightseeing attraction. The ride alternates between calmer stretches, where the surrounding cliffs and forest are the main draw, and short rapids sections that give the trip its "adventure" reputation, though it's a fairly mild ride overall rather than true whitewater rafting.

A full run takes roughly two hours depending on water levels and season, and boats run on a schedule with a fixed number of departures per day, so booking ahead is worth doing, particularly during the autumn foliage season when the gorge's maples draw the largest crowds and demand for seats peaks. Tickets are priced per adult, and warmer or covered clothing is worth bringing since river spray and shade can make the ride noticeably cooler than the surrounding city.

The boat ends near Togetsukyo Bridge in Arashiyama, making it easy to combine with the Sagano Scenic Railway, which runs above the same gorge, for a there-and-back loop covering both the train and boat halves of the journey.
