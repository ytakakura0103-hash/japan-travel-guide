minamoa

Hiroshima

minamoa opened on March 24, 2025, at Hiroshima Station's South Exit, replacing the former ASSE building, which had closed in 2020 for a full reconstruction. The new complex spans roughly twenty floors and gathers around two hundred tenants, alongside Hotel Granvia Hiroshima South Gate and a Shochiku cinema, giving the South Exit a scale and variety of amenities the old building never had.

Several notable photo-friendly spots have emerged within the complex since opening: a seventh-floor Wood Deck Plaza offering city views, a ninth-floor Sora-moa Plaza, and a third-floor atrium looking directly down over the tram platforms below, giving visitors multiple vantage points over the station and surrounding city without needing to visit a dedicated observation deck. Many of its shops mark first-ever Hiroshima locations for their respective brands, adding a genuine sense of novelty for local shoppers as well as visitors.

Its direct integration with Hiroshima Station's South Exit makes it an easy complement to ekie on the Shinkansen side, effectively giving the station two substantial shopping and dining complexes on opposite sides, worth checking both if time allows during a stop at the station.
