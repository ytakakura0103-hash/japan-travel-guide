SanKita-dori & EKIZO Kobe Sannomiya

Kobe

SanKita-dori, completed in its current form in 2021 under the EKIZO Kobe Sannomiya redevelopment, transforms the underside of JR Sannomiya's elevated railway tracks into a stylish stretch of bars and restaurants, a genuinely striking use of infrastructure space that most cities leave as a purely functional underpass. The project's design earned a civil engineering award in 2023, recognition for turning an overlooked stretch of urban infrastructure into a destination in its own right.

The result blends the raw, industrial character of the railway structure overhead with contemporary bar and restaurant design underneath, creating an atmosphere distinct from both Sannomiya's polished shopping streets and the more traditional yokocho-style alleys found elsewhere in Japan. Trains rumbling past overhead are a constant, atmospheric reminder of the space's origins rather than something the design tries to hide.

Its location directly by Hankyu Kobe-Sannomiya Station makes it one of the most convenient nightlife stops in the district, whether as a quick after-work drink or a longer evening exploring the various bars and restaurants tucked beneath the tracks.
