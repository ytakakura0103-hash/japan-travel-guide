Naruko Gorge (Ofukazawa Bridge)

Sendai

Naruko Gorge cuts a deep, steep-walled canyon through the hills near Naruko Onsen, and the Ofukazawa Bridge, a viewing platform built across the gorge itself, gives an almost vertigo-inducing look straight down into the ravine and along its length. Walking trails also drop down into the gorge floor for those who want a closer, from-below perspective rather than the aerial view from the bridge.

The gorge is widely regarded as one of the best places in the Tohoku region to see autumn color, with maples and other deciduous trees turning deep red and orange against the pale canyon rock, typically peaking in mid-to-late October. That reputation means the area gets genuinely crowded during peak leaf season, with parking and access roads under real pressure on weekends.

Viewing from the bridge is free. Reaching it from Naruko Onsen Station takes a short taxi ride or, during autumn, a seasonal shuttle bus; walking is possible but takes considerably longer. Because rail service on the western portion of the Rikuu-to Line has been disrupted in recent years, it's worth double-checking current train and shuttle schedules before the trip. Visiting on a weekday during foliage season, or arriving right at sunrise, is the most reliable way to avoid the worst of the crowds.
