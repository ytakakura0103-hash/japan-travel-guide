Tatara Bridge Sunset Viewpoint

Hiroshima

Tatara Bridge was, at the time of its 1999 completion, the longest cable-stayed bridge in the world, and it carries the Shimanami Kaido cycling path across the strait separating Ikuchijima (Hiroshima) from Omishima (Ehime). Riders cross it as a matter of course, but the more rewarding view is from Tatara Shimanami Park at its base on the Ikuchijima side, where the bridge's full cable fan is visible against the sky.

Late afternoon is the standout time to visit: the cables catch the low sun and the bridge deck silhouettes against color spreading across the Inland Sea, a combination that's made this one of the more photographed structures on the entire route.

Even non-cyclists can reach the viewpoint by car, making it one of the few Shimanami Kaido highlights that doesn't require getting on a bike, though riding across the bridge itself — a gentle, well-protected climb — is a highlight for anyone doing the route.

Setodacho Tarumi, Onomichi, Hiroshima
