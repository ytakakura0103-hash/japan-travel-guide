Sapporo PARCO

Hokkaido

Sapporo PARCO spans eight floors and a basement level with roughly 140 shops, built around a fashion-forward tenant mix that includes both Japanese and international brands aimed at a younger, trend-conscious shopper. Lifestyle anchors like Francfranc and MUJI round out the retail offering, giving it a broader appeal than a purely fashion-focused building.

Its seventh floor is a dedicated pop-culture destination worth a stop regardless of shopping plans, home to a JUMP SHOP carrying merchandise from Shonen Jump's roster of manga series and a Donguri Republic outlet specializing in Studio Ghibli goods. That combination makes it a reliable stop for anime and manga fans visiting Sapporo, on par with dedicated pop-culture districts in larger cities.

Its direct connection to Odori Station makes it one of the most convenient shopping stops in central Sapporo, particularly useful on days with poor weather given how much of the surrounding area connects via underground passages. It's an easy pairing with Marui Imai and Sapporo Mitsukoshi, both a short walk away, for a fuller shopping outing around Odori.

Odori, Chuo Ward, Sapporo
