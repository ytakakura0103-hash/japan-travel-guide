Cat's FUKUOKA

Fukuoka

Cat's FUKUOKA occupies several floors of a building on Oyafuko-dori, Tenjin's main nightlife strip, and markets itself as the city's top nightclub, a claim it backs up with a 2018 renovation and a booking policy that spans multiple genres rather than committing to one narrow lane. That range makes it a reasonably safe bet for a group with mixed music tastes, since different floors or nights can lean toward EDM, hip-hop, or other current sounds.

Its Oyafuko-dori location puts it directly in the middle of Tenjin's most concentrated nightlife stretch, already covered elsewhere in this guide for its density of bars and clubs, making it an easy anchor point for a night that also takes in the surrounding backstreets. The multi-floor layout gives it more capacity than most of Fukuoka's smaller club spaces, useful on weekend nights when the area gets busy.

As Fukuoka's self-described leading club, it draws a broad mix of locals, students, and visitors rather than a niche scene, making it one of the more approachable options for anyone new to the city's nightlife. Checking the current event calendar before visiting is worthwhile, since specific nights can carry different genre focuses and cover charges.

Oyafuko-dori, Chuo Ward, Fukuoka
