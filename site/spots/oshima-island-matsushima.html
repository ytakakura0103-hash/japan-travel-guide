Local Pick

Oshima Island

Sendai

For centuries, ascetic Buddhist monks used this small island as a retreat for meditation and religious training, and its cliffs are still pocked with hand-carved caves and dotted with stone monuments and small Buddhist statues left behind by generations of practitioners. It sits just across a short bridge from the Matsushima waterfront, close enough to see from the main temple complex but far enough to feel separate from it.

Unlike Fukuurajima, the bridge here is free to cross, and the island itself is small enough to explore fully in fifteen to twenty minutes.

Because it's overshadowed by the much larger and more famous Zuiganji temple complex just across the water, Oshima tends to get overlooked entirely, even by visitors standing right next to it. It's a quiet, slightly eerie spot at any time of day, but especially atmospheric near sunset when the bay's light turns golden.

Matsushima, Matsushima-machi, Miyagi-gun, Miyagi
