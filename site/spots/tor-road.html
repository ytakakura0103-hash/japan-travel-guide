Tor Road

Kobe

Tor Road runs in a fairly straight line from near the harbor up toward the Kitano hillside, and it has long been associated with Kobe's foreign trading and fashion history; several old trading company buildings and a Meiji-era Catholic church still stand along or just off the road. Today the street is lined with independent boutiques, hairdressers, antique and import shops, and a scattering of cafes, giving it a different character from the department-store shopping of central Sannomiya.

A leisurely walk from the bottom to the top of the road, browsing shopfronts along the way, takes roughly 30 to 40 minutes without stopping, longer if you duck into individual stores. It works well as a walking route to link the harbor area with the Kitano Ijinkan district rather than a single-purpose destination.

Because it is more of a through-street than a tourist attraction in itself, Tor Road rarely feels crowded even when nearby Kitano and Sannomiya are busy, making it a pleasant lower-key alternative for anyone wanting to browse shops without fighting foot traffic.
