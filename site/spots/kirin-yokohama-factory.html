Kirin Beer Yokohama Factory Tour

Yokohama

This is a genuinely operating Kirin brewery, not a museum built to look like one, and the guided tour walks you through the malting, brewing, and packaging process before finishing with a tasting session comparing the flavor of freshly brewed unfiltered beer against the bottled version most people know. A small admission fee applies for anyone over the drinking age, and the tour runs a bit over an hour, with samples of malt and hops along the way to smell and taste before the beer itself.

The factory sits in Namamugi, a district whose name is tied to a famous 1862 incident involving a British merchant and a samurai procession, one of the sparks that eventually pushed Japan toward the end of its feudal era. It is a slightly out-of-the-way corner of Tsurumi Ward that most sightseeing itineraries skip entirely.

Because the tour requires advance reservation and runs on a set schedule, it rewards a bit of planning rather than a spontaneous drop-in, and non-drinkers or minors are accommodated with soft-drink tastings instead. Weekday tours tend to have more availability than weekend slots, which fill up with local groups.
