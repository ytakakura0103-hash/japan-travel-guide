Hyakumanben Student Nightlife District

Kyoto

Hyakumanben, the intersection right outside Kyoto University's main gate, functions as the university's real student town, distinct from the more general riverside hangout scene at nearby Demachiyanagi. Locals sometimes call it a "sleepless town," a nod to how late many of its izakaya stay open compared to the rest of Kyoto, where most restaurants and bars wind down well before midnight. Spacious izakaya with private tatami rooms cater specifically to large student groups and the Japanese custom of nijikai, moving to a second bar after the first round wraps up.

The atmosphere here is markedly more relaxed and unpolished than Kyoto's tourist-facing nightlife districts, shaped by decades of student culture rather than the geisha-district heritage of Gion or the bar-hopping density of Kiyamachi. It's increasingly finding an audience among visiting travelers looking for an authentic, low-cost night out alongside Kyoto's student population, though it remains far less internationally known than the city's headline nightlife spots.

Term-time evenings, particularly after exams or at the start of a new semester, bring the liveliest atmosphere, while the area quiets down considerably during university breaks. It pairs naturally with a daytime visit to the Kyoto University campus or the Yoshida Shrine hillside nearby.
