Chuden MIRAI TOWER (Nagoya TV Tower)

Nagoya

Completed in the mid-1950s, this steel lattice tower was Japan's first purpose-built TV tower and long served as the transmission point for Nagoya's broadcasters before digital switchover made the role obsolete. Rather than being torn down, it was renovated into an observation deck and small hotel, and its slender silhouette lit up at night remains one of the most recognizable images of downtown Nagoya. It carries naming-rights branding as "Chuden MIRAI TOWER" since 2021, though most locals still just call it Nagoya TV Tower.

An observation ticket takes you up for city views that stretch toward the castle and, on clear days, distant mountains; the visit itself takes well under an hour. The tower sits inside Hisaya-odori Park, so it pairs naturally with a walk through the surrounding green space and fountains.

Evening is the best time to visit, when the tower's illumination changes color and the park below fills with a relaxed after-work crowd rather than daytime tour groups. Weekday evenings are noticeably quieter than weekends, when the plaza around the tower's base can get busy with events and food trucks.

3-6-15 Nishiki, Naka-ku, Nagoya, Aichi
