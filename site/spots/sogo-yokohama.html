Sogo Yokohama

Yokohama

Sogo Yokohama opened on September 30, 1985, directly connected to the east side of Yokohama Station, and made an immediate mark on Japanese retail history: at the time, it boasted the largest sales floor of any department store in Asia, and it went on to post the highest first-year sales of any department store in Japan, a record that reportedly still stands decades later. Spread across twelve floors, from basement two to the tenth, it covers food, beauty, fashion, luxury goods, and lifestyle floors, with a dedicated dining floor rounding out the offering.

Its basement food floor, accessible from the main entrance, is the store's most heavily trafficked level, packed with roughly two hundred gourmet shops covering everything from prepared foods to regional specialties. Less expected for a department store, the sixth floor houses the Sogo Museum of Art, the only art museum inside a Japanese department store certified under the country's Museum Law, running around ten exhibitions a year, giving visitors a genuine reason to visit beyond shopping.

As the top-selling store in the Sogo chain nationwide, it remains open all 365 days of the year, including New Year's Day, a rarity among Japanese department stores that typically close for at least the first few days of January. Its direct station connection makes it one of the most convenient shopping stops in Yokohama for anyone passing through the station regardless of destination.
