Local Pick

Akiu Craft Park (Akiu Kogei no Sato)

Sendai

Rather than a single museum, Akiu Craft Park is a small campus of independent artisan workshops set among the hills near Akiu Onsen, each specializing in a different traditional Tohoku craft, from tsutsumi-yaki pottery and indigo dyeing to woodworking and kokeshi doll production. Visitors can walk between studios watching artisans at work, and several offer hands-on experiences, painting your own kokeshi or trying a pottery wheel, for those who want a souvenir with a personal stake in making it.

The atmosphere is unhurried and workshop-like rather than a polished retail experience, which is part of the appeal for travelers who've already seen more commercial craft centers elsewhere in Japan and want something closer to the source. It fits naturally into a broader Akiu day trip alongside the winery and hot spring, forming a loose circuit that locals sometimes do as a single relaxed outing.

Admission to walk through and browse is typically free or very low cost, with hands-on workshops charging a separate modest fee per activity, generally taking thirty minutes to an hour depending on the craft. It sees a fraction of the foot traffic that Akiu Onsen itself gets, so it rarely feels crowded at any time, though calling ahead for a specific hands-on workshop is worth doing if you have your heart set on one.

Akiu-machi Yumoto, Taihaku-ku, Sendai, Miyagi

About 45 minutes by bus from Sendai Station on the Akiu Line, near Akiu Onsen.
