Shimanami Kaido Cycling Route

Hiroshima

The Shimanami Kaido opened as a full expressway and cycling route in 1999, connecting Honshu to Shikoku by threading a chain of bridges across six small islands in the Seto Inland Sea: Mukaishima, Innoshima, Ikuchijima, Omishima, Hakatajima, and Oshima. Unlike most of Japan's other Honshu-Shikoku crossings, this route was built with a dedicated, largely separated cycling and walking path running alongside the motor traffic lanes across every bridge, which is a major reason it developed a strong international cycling reputation.

The full one-way ride from Onomichi to Imabari covers roughly 70 kilometers and can typically be completed in a single day by a reasonably fit rider, though many visitors break it into a more relaxed two-day trip, stopping at island towns, temples such as Kosanji, and rental cycle terminals along the way. Bicycle rental is widely available at terminals on both ends and at several points in between, including standard cross-frame bikes, road bikes, and e-bikes, with one-way rental allowing pickup on one end and drop-off on the other.

Route markings, rest stops, and toilets are placed at regular intervals, and the blue line painted along much of the path makes navigation straightforward even for first-time visitors unfamiliar with the islands.

Onomichi, Hiroshima Prefecture (northern terminus)

Starts near JR Onomichi Station in Hiroshima Prefecture and ends near Imabari Station in Ehime Prefecture; Onomichi is about 90 minutes to 2 hours from central Hiroshima by JR train.
