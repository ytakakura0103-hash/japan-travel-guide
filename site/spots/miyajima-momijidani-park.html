Momijidani Park

Hiroshima

Momijidani ('maple valley') is a small park following a mountain stream at the base of Mount Misen, planted heavily with Japanese maples that turn deep red and orange through most of November. A traditional arched bridge crosses the stream partway through, framed by the trees, and it's one of the most reliably photographed single spots on Miyajima during foliage season.\n\nOutside of autumn, the park is a quieter, shadier alternative to the crowded shrine area — a place to sit by the stream, watch the resident deer wander through (they're generally unbothered by people here, unlike the more aggressive ones near the ferry pier), and cool off before or after the Misen hike.\n\nThe park also marks the base station for the Miyajima Ropeway, so most visitors pass through it whether they intend to stop or not; the difference between rushing through and actually sitting down for twenty minutes is significant, especially in foliage season when the crowds thin out toward late afternoon.

Miyajimacho, Hatsukaichi, Hiroshima
