Meinohama Beach

Fukuoka

Meinohama's shoreline sits just past the western edge of Fukuoka's built-up center, close enough to reach by subway yet open enough to feel like a genuine escape from the city once the sand and seawall come into view. It functions primarily as a neighborhood beach and ferry departure point for Nokonoshima Island rather than a resort destination, which keeps facilities simple: a walking promenade, some casual seafood restaurants nearby, and open views west across Hakata Bay toward Itoshima.

Sunset is the main draw, with the flat western horizon over the bay giving an unobstructed view as the sky changes color, a scene that draws a small but steady crowd of local residents on clear evenings rather than tour groups. Because the ferry terminal for Nokonoshima sits right here, it's easy to combine a morning or afternoon island trip with an evening beach walk on the way back into the city.

The beach rarely appears on standard sightseeing itineraries, which keeps it comfortably uncrowded even at the golden hour that draws the most people. It is a reasonable stand-in for visitors who want a coastal sunset without committing to the longer drive out to Itoshima's more famous Sunset Road.
