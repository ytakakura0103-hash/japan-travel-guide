LUCUA & LUCUA 1100

Osaka

LUCUA is built from two connected buildings directly above JR Osaka Station, together forming one of the largest fashion retail complexes in Japan with roughly 500 stores. The original east building, LUCUA, and the newer west building, LUCUA 1100, split the shopping experience by tone rather than by category: LUCUA 1100 skews toward a more mature, considered customer, anchored by an Isetan shop and @cosme Osaka, a beauty floor gathering roughly 500 cosmetics brands under one roof, plus a full floor dedicated to outdoor and technical apparel brands.

Dining is concentrated in the basement, where BARCHICA and the LUCUA FOOD HALL offer a range of casual bar and restaurant options for a quick meal or a drink before catching a train. Because the complex sits directly above Osaka Station, it functions as a natural last stop for visitors heading out of the city, letting shopping run right up until departure without a separate trip.

A third building, LUCUA SOUTH, has been under development to extend the complex further; check current signage or the official site to see what's open, since large station-area developments like this are sometimes completed and opened in phases. Between LUCUA's two established buildings, expect to need at least a couple of hours to browse meaningfully given the sheer number of stores.

3-1-3 Umeda, Kita Ward, Osaka
