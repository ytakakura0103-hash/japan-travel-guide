Yokohama Red Brick Warehouse

Yokohama

Built in the early 20th century as Yokohama's state-of-the-art customs warehouses, these two brick buildings survived the 1923 earthquake and wartime bombing largely intact, which is part of why they now feel more solid and less manufactured than a typical shopping complex. Inside, the exposed brick, iron beams, and old cargo elevators have been kept visible around the boutiques, cafes, and craft shops that now occupy the space.

The plaza in front of the warehouses hosts markets, beer gardens, and seasonal events throughout the year, including a well-known winter illumination and ice rink. Weekend afternoons draw heavy crowds, particularly when an event is running in the plaza, so a weekday morning visit is the easier way to actually browse the shops without jostling for space.

The warehouses sit on the waterfront between Yamashita Park and Minato Mirai, making them a natural stop on a walk connecting Chinatown, Yamashita Park, and the Landmark Tower area. In summer, the surrounding lawn fills with people picnicking with a view of passing cruise ships at the nearby Osanbashi pier, and the building's warm brick facade is especially photogenic at dusk when the interior lights come on.

1-1 Shinko, Naka-ku, Yokohama
