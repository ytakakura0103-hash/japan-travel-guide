Local Pick

Kitano Tenman Shrine

Kobe

Perched at the top of a long flight of stone steps above the main cluster of Kitano's foreign residences, this shrine is dedicated to Sugawara no Michizane, the deified scholar associated with learning who is honored at Tenman and Tenjin shrines across Japan, making it a popular stop for students praying for exam success. Its real draw for most visitors, though, is the view from the top of its steps, which looks out over the rooftops of the Kitano Ijinkan district toward the harbor and city beyond.

Climbing the steps and taking in the shrine grounds and view takes about 15 to 20 minutes, and there is no admission fee. The framing from partway up the steps, with the shrine's torii gate and the ijinkan rooftops both visible, is a favorite composition for photographers exploring the district.

Despite sitting right above one of Kobe's most visited neighborhoods, the shrine itself sees only a fraction of the foot traffic passing through the ijinkan houses below, since many visitors do not realize the steps lead anywhere in particular, making it a reliably quiet spot even during Kitano's busiest hours.

3-12 Kitanocho, Chuo-ku, Kobe

About a 15-minute walk uphill from Sannomiya Station, above the main Kitano Ijinkan cluster.
