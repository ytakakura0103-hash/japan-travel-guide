Akiu Winery

Sendai

Akiu Winery was established in 2015 in the hills above Akiu Onsen, part of a wave of post-disaster projects aimed at building new, sustainable industries in Miyagi after the 2011 earthquake and tsunami. The vineyard now grows roughly a dozen grape varieties, including merlot and pinotage for reds and gewürztraminer, chardonnay, and pinot gris for whites, planted in soil containing local Akiu stone that's said to give the wine a distinct mineral quality.

The winery produces reds, whites, rosés, sparkling wine, and even an apple cider, and a glass-walled café and shop on site let visitors taste and buy directly, often paired with food made from local Akiu ingredients. Guided tours of the vineyard and production area go deeper into the growing process, though these generally need to be booked a few days in advance rather than joined on a walk-in basis.

A casual tasting stop is inexpensive; the guided tour and pairing experience costs more but runs close to an hour and includes a proper walk through the vines. Because it sits slightly off the main Akiu Onsen strip, it draws far fewer visitors than the hot spring town itself, making it a genuinely uncrowded stop even on weekends. The vineyard is at its most photogenic with fruit on the vines from late summer into the September harvest.

Akiu-machi Yumoto, Taihaku-ku, Sendai, Miyagi

About 30 minutes by car or taxi from central Sendai, in the hills near Akiu Onsen.
