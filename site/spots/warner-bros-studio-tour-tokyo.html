Warner Bros. Studio Tour Tokyo

Tokyo

Warner Bros. Studio Tour Tokyo — The Making of Harry Potter occupies part of the site of the former Toshimaen amusement park in Nerima, converted into a walk-through experience built around real sets, costumes, and props used in the film series rather than a rides-based theme park. Visitors move through recreations of the Great Hall, Dumbledore's office, Diagon Alley, and other familiar locations, with much of the actual filming material on display alongside behind-the-scenes explanations of how effects and sets were built.

Because it's a self-paced walking tour rather than a ride-based attraction, visits run long: most guests spend three to four hours inside, and fans who stop to read every placard and photograph every set can easily stretch that further. Tickets are timed-entry and need to be booked in advance, as walk-up admission is not generally available and popular dates can sell out weeks ahead, especially around school holidays. Weekday visits and the earliest entry slots of the day tend to be least crowded, giving more room to take photos in the more popular sets like Diagon Alley without waiting behind other groups.

The studio includes a cafe serving butterbeer and a large gift shop with props and merchandise not available elsewhere in Japan. Because it sits further from central Tokyo than most attractions on this list, it's worth planning a half or full day around the visit rather than combining it with much else.

1-1-7 Kasugacho, Nerima City, Tokyo
