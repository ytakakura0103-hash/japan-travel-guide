Akarenga Chosha (Former Hokkaido Government Office)

Hokkaido

Known locally as Akarenga Chosha, or simply the red-brick office, the Former Hokkaido Government Office has been a fixture of central Sapporo since the Meiji era, its neo-baroque red-brick facade standing out sharply against the surrounding modern city. After more than five years of restoration work, it reopened to the public in July 2025 with a refreshed set of exhibits and a renewed emphasis on free public access.

The reopened building added a showcase corner highlighting all 179 of Hokkaido's municipalities, useful for visitors curious about the prefecture beyond Sapporo, and a dedicated room exploring Ainu culture and history, giving the building a more substantial cultural dimension than its architecture alone. A new shop and cafe round out the visitor experience, making it easier to spend a full hour or more here rather than just viewing the exterior.

Its location a short walk from Odori Park makes it easy to combine with a broader walk through central Sapporo, and the building's photogenic red-brick symmetry has made it a reliable stop for photography regardless of season. Checking current exhibit hours before visiting is worth doing, since some sections may have different opening times than the building's general access hours.

Kita 3-jo Nishi 6-chome, Chuo Ward, Sapporo

A short walk from Odori Station (Sapporo Municipal Subway) or Sapporo Station.
