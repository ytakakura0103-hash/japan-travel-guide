Okurayama Ski Jump Stadium Observation Deck

Sapporo

Okurayama was built for the 1972 Sapporo Winter Olympics and is still an active competition ski jump, which makes the view from the top genuinely vertigo-inducing: the observation platform sits at the jumpers' start gate, looking straight down the in-run toward the city below. A small lift, the same style used by athletes, carries visitors up in about five minutes.\n\nAt the base, the Sapporo Olympic Museum covers the city's Olympic history with some hands-on simulators, including a ski-jump simulator, which makes it more interactive than most sports museums. The stadium also hosts summer ski-jump training and occasional competitions, so there is sometimes a chance to watch jumpers in action even outside winter.\n\nCompared to Mt. Moiwa's more famous night-view ropeway, Okurayama draws a noticeably smaller crowd, largely because it requires a bus transfer from Maruyama-koen Station rather than a direct ropeway from downtown. That extra step keeps it relatively peaceful even in peak season.\n\nSummer (late April through October) offers extended hours and sometimes evening viewing, while winter access depends on weather and jump competition schedules, so it's worth confirming the lift is running before making the trip out.

1274 Miyanomori, Chuo-ku, Sapporo, Hokkaido 064-0959

Open in Google Maps

About 30-40 minutes from Sapporo Station: Tozai Subway Line to Maruyama-koen Station (about 15 minutes), then a short walk to Maruyama Bus Terminal and the Kuramaru-go bus to Okurayama (about 15 minutes).
