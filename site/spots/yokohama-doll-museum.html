Yokohama Doll Museum

Yokohama

The collection here runs well beyond the classic Japanese hina and gogatsu festival dolls most visitors expect, extending into folk dolls, fashion dolls, and traditional dress dolls contributed from countries around the world, giving the museum a genuinely international scope across its several floors. Traditional Japanese doll-making techniques are also explained in enough detail to interest adults, not just the families with young children who make up a large share of visitors.

Because dolls carry different cultural weight in Japan than in much of the West, tied to specific festivals, family rites, and craft traditions, the museum works well as an entry point into aspects of Japanese culture that the bigger art museums downtown do not really cover. Rotating special exhibitions occasionally bring in contemporary doll artists alongside the permanent historical collection.

Sitting on the street just behind Yamashita Park, it is an easy add-on to a harbor walk, and it tends to be treated as optional by visitors focused on bigger attractions, which keeps the galleries comfortably uncrowded. A small shop sells doll-related crafts and traditional items that make distinctive gifts.

18 Yamashita-cho, Naka-ku, Yokohama

Open in Google Maps

About 3 minutes on foot from Minatomirai Line Motomachi-Chukagai Station, facing Yamashita Park.
