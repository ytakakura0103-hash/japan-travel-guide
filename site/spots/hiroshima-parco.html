Hiroshima PARCO

Hiroshima

Hiroshima PARCO spans two connected buildings, a Main Building running basement two to the tenth floor and a New Building from basement two to the ninth, together gathering roughly one hundred and seventy shops that make it one of the leading fashion destinations in the entire Chugoku-Shikoku region. Its position alongside Hondori, Namiki-dori, and Kinza-gai puts it directly within central Hiroshima's main downtown shopping cluster, a short walk from the Peace Memorial Park area.

The complex's tenant mix follows the PARCO brand's typical fashion-forward, trend-conscious approach, spanning both established and select boutique brands, interior and lifestyle goods, and a live-music venue that adds a cultural dimension beyond pure retail. That live venue gives Hiroshima PARCO a role in the city's music scene that most shopping complexes don't share.

Its central location makes it an easy stop to combine with Hondori Shopping Street and the broader downtown shopping district, and its scale means it's worth setting aside real time for browsing rather than treating it as a quick stop between other sights.
