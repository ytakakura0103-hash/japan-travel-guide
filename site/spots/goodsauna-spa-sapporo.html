goodsauna & spa SAPPORO

Hokkaido

goodsauna & spa SAPPORO opened in November 2025 directly connected to Susukino Station, riding Japan's broader sauna boom into one of Hokkaido's most talked-about new wellness facilities. It was produced with involvement from well-known figures in Japan's contemporary sauna scene, giving it credibility with the enthusiast crowd that's driven much of the recent surge in dedicated sauna facilities across the country.

Rock-bath rooms set it apart from more conventional sauna setups, offering a different heat and humidity experience than the standard dry or steam sauna format found at most Japanese bathing facilities. Its around-the-clock operation, unusual for a facility of this scale, makes it a genuinely flexible option for visitors on an irregular schedule, whether looking to unwind after a long flight or cap off a late night in Susukino with something other than another drink.

Its direct connection to Susukino Station makes it one of the most convenient wellness stops in central Sapporo, requiring no separate trip beyond the subway ride most visitors are already making to reach the nightlife district. As with any newer facility, checking current hours and any membership or reservation requirements on-site is worthwhile.

Susukino, Chuo Ward, Sapporo
