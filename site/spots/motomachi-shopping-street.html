Motomachi Shopping Street

Yokohama

When Yokohama's foreign settlement split into a hillside residential district at Yamate and a business district down at Kannai, this street became the route foreign residents walked between the two each day, and local shopkeepers gradually shifted their businesses to cater to that foreign clientele, bringing in bakeries, tailors, and imported goods well before such things were common elsewhere in Japan. That history still shows in the street's slightly more upscale, boutique-heavy character compared to Isezakicho or other Yokohama shopping streets.\n\nToday it is a comfortable, pedestrian-friendly stretch of fashion boutiques, cafes, and long-established local shops, some of which have operated for generations. It sits at the base of the climb up to Yamate, making it a natural starting or ending point for a bluff walking route, and its proximity to Chinatown means the two districts are often combined in a single outing.\n\nWeekend afternoons bring the heaviest foot traffic, particularly when combined with Chinatown crowds spilling over, so a weekday visit or an early Saturday morning gives a noticeably calmer look at the street's shopfronts and architecture.
