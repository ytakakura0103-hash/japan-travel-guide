Saikokuji Temple's Niomon Gate and Stone Steps

Hiroshima

Saikokuji is a working Shingon-sect temple a short walk east of the main Cat Alley cluster, and it draws far fewer visitors than the Senkoji ropeway route despite having one of the more dramatic approaches in town: a long, steep flight of stone steps rising to a vermillion Niomon gate guarded by two muscular Nio statues.

The temple grounds themselves are modest and quiet, with a small main hall and bell tower, but the staircase and gate combination is what locals point tourists toward when they want a photo without a crowd in it. Come in late afternoon when the light angles up the steps rather than flattening them.

Because Saikokuji sits slightly off the main pilgrimage route most guidebooks describe, it tends to get skipped by people on a tight schedule — which is exactly why it's worth the extra ten minutes if you've already done the busier temples nearer the station.

29-27 Nishikubocho, Onomichi, Hiroshima
