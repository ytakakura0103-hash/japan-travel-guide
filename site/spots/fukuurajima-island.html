Fukuurajima Island

Sendai

Fukuurajima is connected to the Matsushima shoreline by a long red-painted footbridge, itself a popular photo spot, leading to a small island maintained largely as a natural botanical garden. Walking paths loop through the trees, past a modest shrine and a rotating cast of native plants and flowers that bloom at different points across spring and summer.\n\nCrossing the bridge requires a small toll, and exploring the island's paths generally takes about an hour at an unhurried pace. It sits within easy walking distance of Matsushima's main harbor area.\n\nBecause most day-trippers stick to the boat cruise around the bay and the cluster of temples near the pier, the island itself stays relatively uncrowded even when Matsushima's waterfront is busy. Spring through early summer brings the best flowering displays, though the shaded paths are pleasant even outside bloom season.
