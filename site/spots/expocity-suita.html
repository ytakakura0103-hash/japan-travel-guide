EXPOCITY

Osaka

Built next to Expo '70 Commemorative Park on the site of the 1970 World's Fair grounds, EXPOCITY is one of the larger shopping and entertainment complexes in the Osaka area, bringing together retail floors, restaurants, a large observation wheel, the NIFREL interactive aquarium-and-art hybrid, and an indoor snow park where visitors can experience real snow regardless of the season outside. The scale here is considerable, and a visit that includes even a couple of the entertainment attractions alongside some shopping can easily fill most of a day.

NIFREL in particular blends aquarium displays with art installations in a way that distinguishes it from a standard aquarium, and it's worth budgeting an hour or two if it's on your list. The observation wheel offers wide views back over the Expo park's iconic Tower of the Sun and the surrounding Suita suburbs, and rides typically run in a continuous loop rather than requiring a long wait outside peak periods.

Weekday visits are noticeably calmer than weekends, when EXPOCITY draws heavy crowds of local families, particularly around the entertainment attractions. Because it sits directly next to Expo '70 Park, with its own cherry blossom season in spring and the landmark Tower of the Sun, it's easy to combine a EXPOCITY visit with time in the park for a fuller day trip north of central Osaka.
