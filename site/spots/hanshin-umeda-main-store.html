Hanshin Umeda Main Store

Osaka

Hanshin Umeda Main Store markets itself explicitly as the "Hanshin of Food," and its two-story basement food hall backs that up as the clear centerpiece of the store rather than an afterthought beneath the fashion floors. The signature item is ika-yaki, a pressed grilled squid pancake that's been a Hanshin depachika fixture for decades, sold from a stand that regularly draws a line. Alongside it, the "Snack Park" gathers a cluster of casual eat-in food counters under one roof, good for a quick, varied meal without committing to a sit-down restaurant.

The food focus continues upstairs and adjacent to the depachika with Hanshin Bar Alley, a stretch of small bars that open from midday, letting visitors day-drink alongside Osaka's own regulars rather than waiting for evening. A rotating "Shokusai Terrace" hosts themed food events throughout the year, and a dedicated Liquor World section rounds out the store's drink-focused identity. Being owned by the same company as the Hanshin Tigers baseball team, the store also carries an official Hanshin Tigers merchandise shop, a notable stop for baseball fans regardless of whether a game is on the schedule.

Its location directly beside Hanshin Umeda Station makes it an easy add-on to a visit to Hankyu Umeda Main Store or Daimaru Umeda, both close by, for a single afternoon covering Umeda's cluster of major department stores.

1-13-13 Umeda, Kita Ward, Osaka
