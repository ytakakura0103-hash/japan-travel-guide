NYK Maritime Museum

Yokohama

Nippon Yusen Kaisha, or NYK, grew into one of Japan's dominant shipping companies from the Meiji era onward, and this museum occupied its former Yokohama branch building, a solid stone-faced structure from the 1930s that survives as a good example of the port district's prewar commercial architecture in its own right. Inside, detailed ship models traced the evolution from early steamships to postwar ocean liners, alongside route maps, uniforms, and printed ephemera like menus and passenger tickets from the golden age of transpacific travel.

Important for planning a visit: the museum has been closed since April 2023 for redevelopment of the surrounding block and is not currently open to the public. It is set to reopen under the new name NYK Museum in spring 2027, in the nearby Yokohama Building rather than its old branch-office home. Check the official museum website for the latest status before including it in an itinerary.

When open, it appealed most to visitors with some interest in maritime or transport history, and the scale was modest enough to see thoroughly in under an hour, making it an easy secondary stop rather than a full outing on its own. Signage included English in most sections, which is not guaranteed at every smaller Yokohama museum.

Sitting just a short walk from the Red Brick Warehouse and Osanbashi Pier, it was bypassed by most visitors heading straight for those bigger attractions, keeping the galleries pleasantly uncrowded. Once it reopens, it should still reward anyone curious about how central shipping was to Yokohama's identity as Japan's premier international port.

3-9 Kaigan-dori, Naka-ku, Yokohama

Open in Google Maps

Currently closed for redevelopment of the surrounding block (since April 2023). The former building is about 2 minutes on foot from Minatomirai Line Bashamichi Station, or about 8 minutes from JR Kannai Station. The reopened NYK Museum (planned for spring 2027) will be in the nearby Yokohama Building, about 4 minutes on foot from Bashamichi Station or 6 minutes from Nihon-odori Station.
