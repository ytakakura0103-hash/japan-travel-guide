Local Pick

Mount Izumigatake

Sendai

Rising northwest of central Sendai and counted among Japan's 300 Famous Mountains, Izumigatake is a genuinely beloved local hiking spot rather than a headline attraction, with gentle, well-marked trails that suit beginner and intermediate hikers alike. From the summit, views stretch across the Sendai plain toward the Funagata mountain range on clear days.

The standard route from the trailhead nature center to the summit and back takes around four hours at a relaxed pace, and the trailhead facility has toilets, a small shop, and a hiking log book. There's no entry fee for the trail itself.

It's popular with local hikers on weekends, especially in good weather, but remains almost entirely off the radar for overseas visitors, so it offers a genuine slice of everyday Sendai outdoor life. Wildflowers bloom along the trail from spring through autumn, with different species appearing as the season progresses.
