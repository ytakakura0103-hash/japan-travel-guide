Sound Lab mole

Hokkaido

Sound Lab mole has operated near the Tanukikoji shopping arcade since 2006, occupying a space that functions as both a live house and a club, hosting touring bands one night and DJ-led club events the next. That dual identity gives it more programming variety than a venue committed purely to one format, and it's built a following among Sapporo's independent music scene over nearly two decades of continuous operation.

Its location near Tanukikoji rather than deep within Susukino gives it a slightly different character than the club-dense heart of Sapporo's nightlife district, drawing a crowd more specifically interested in live music and independent culture than a general night-out audience. That positioning has helped it carve out a durable niche in a nightlife scene where turnover is otherwise common.

Checking the current event calendar before visiting is essential here more than at most clubs, since the venue alternates between full live shows with cover charges tied to specific acts and more standard club nights. Its proximity to Tanukikoji makes it easy to combine with a walk through the covered arcade before or after a show.
