Hishio Beach

Hiroshima

Hishio Beach and its neighbor Tachibana Coast are where Onomichi and Mukaishima families actually go swimming in summer, rather than a curated tourist beach — expect basic changing facilities, calm shallow water suited to children, and a noticeably local crowd compared to the temple hill on the other side of the channel.

The setting is unglamorous but pleasant: modest hills behind the beach, small fishing boats moored nearby, and views back across the water toward the shipyards and Onomichi's hillside. It's not a destination in itself worth crossing the country for, but for visitors staying in Onomichi for more than a day, it's an easy way to cool off away from the crowds around the ropeway and Cat Alley.

Combine it with a walk through Mukaishima's shipyard backstreets or a simple bicycle loop of the island, since the beach alone is a fairly quick stop.
