Hakata Machiya Furusatokan

Fukuoka

This small folk museum recreates a Meiji and Taisho-era merchant house from Hakata's textile trade, with a main building, workshop, and exhibition hall arranged around a narrow courtyard typical of the neighborhood's old row houses. Rather than static displays behind glass, much of the appeal comes from craftspeople actually weaving Hakata-ori obi sashes or hand-painting Hakata dolls at work tables visitors can walk right up to.

A short video with English subtitles covers the district's merchant history and the Yamakasa festival, useful context before wandering the surrounding lanes. The admission fee is modest, and because the museum sits directly across from Kushida Shrine, it pairs naturally with a shrine visit without adding much extra walking.

It tends to be quiet even when Kushida Shrine and Kawabata Arcade nearby are busy, since it isn't on every tour itinerary, making late morning on a weekday about as calm as Hakata gets. Hours run into early evening, with the museum opening slightly earlier during peak summer months around the Yamakasa festival.

6-10 Reisenmachi, Hakata Ward, Fukuoka

Open in Google Maps

5-minute walk from Gion Station, directly across from Kushida Shrine
