Shiribetsu River

Hokkaido

Niseko's international reputation runs almost entirely on winter skiing, which makes the Shiribetsu River something of an off-season secret even among people who already know the area name. Rated among the cleaner rivers in Japan, it runs through the Niseko valley with enough gradient and volume, especially during spring snowmelt, to support genuine whitewater rafting rather than a gentle float.

Several outfitters based around Kutchan and Niseko town run guided rafting trips, generally split into a faster spring snowmelt season (April-May) and a calmer summer season (roughly June-October) better suited to families and first-timers. No experience is required for the standard tours, and guides handle route-finding through the rapids.

Because almost all of Niseko's marketing and infrastructure caters to winter visitors, the river draws a comparatively small, mostly domestic and regional summer crowd, which keeps trips from feeling like an assembly line even at outfitters used to handling groups.

Mt. Yotei is visible from parts of the river for much of the trip, giving a very different, greener view of the mountain than the snow-covered version most visitors associate with the area, and it's a solid way to justify a Niseko visit outside ski season.
