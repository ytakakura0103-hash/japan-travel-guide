Furano Winery

Sapporo

Furano's cool climate turns out to be reasonably well suited to certain grape varieties, and this winery, run by the town itself rather than a private company, has been producing wine from locally grown grapes since the 1970s. A small free tasting counter lets visitors sample several wines before deciding what to buy, and large windows in parts of the facility let visitors watch barrel storage and, depending on the season, some of the pressing process.

Compared to Farm Tomita's lavender fields a short distance away, the winery draws a noticeably smaller, quieter crowd, since it isn't typically the headline stop on a Furano day trip, making it a pleasant lower-key add-on rather than a destination in its own right. The surrounding hillside grounds, planted with grapevines and other greenery, offer a decent view over the Furano basin even for visitors who skip the tasting.

Autumn during the grape harvest, roughly September to October, is arguably the most interesting time to visit given the chance to see some of the pressing process directly, though the tasting room and shop operate year-round regardless of season. It's an easy stop to combine with Farm Tomita or central Furano town, being only a short distance from JR Furano Station.
