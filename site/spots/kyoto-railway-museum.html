Kyoto Railway Museum

Kyoto

Kyoto Railway Museum, opened in 2016 on the site of a former railway yard near Kyoto Station, brings together a large collection of historic locomotives and rolling stock, including a preserved roundhouse of steam engines originally built in the early 20th century that still houses trains today. A working steam locomotive offers a short return ride on a section of track within the museum grounds on a set schedule, one of the more popular single attractions for younger visitors.

The museum's exhibits go beyond static displays, with driving simulators for both conventional and shinkansen bullet trains, detailed scale models, and hands-on stations explaining how different rail systems function, making it a place where visitors can easily spend a few hours moving between exhibits rather than passing through quickly. A rooftop observation area also overlooks the adjacent JR tracks, giving a view of real trains passing on the mainline throughout the day. A thorough visit, including the steam locomotive ride and a simulator turn, typically takes around three hours; admission is charged per adult and child, with the steam ride sometimes priced separately.

The museum sits about twenty minutes on foot from Kyoto Station, or a short bus ride, and is easily paired with the nearby Kyoto Aquarium for a family day trip. Weekends and school holidays bring the largest crowds, especially around the steam locomotive departure times; weekday mornings are the quietest option.
