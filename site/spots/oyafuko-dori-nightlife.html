Oyafuko-dori

Fukuoka

The name Oyafuko-dori roughly translates to something like "unfilial street," a nod to its reputation as the part of town where young Fukuoka residents historically stayed out later than their parents would have liked. Today it's the city's main cluster of nightclubs and late-night bars, with basement-level venues hosting everything from techno and house to hip-hop and reggae, mostly concentrated in a compact area easy to cover on foot.

Most of the real energy here doesn't start until well after 11pm, and many venues run until 4 or 5am on Friday and Saturday nights, so this is very much a late-night rather than early-evening destination. Cover charges and drink minimums are common at the club venues, worth checking before heading in.

During the day the street looks fairly unremarkable, and most of what makes it worth visiting only becomes apparent after dark, so plan accordingly. It sits close enough to the Daimyo backstreets that the two areas naturally blend into a single night out.

Between Tenjin 3-chome and Maizuru 1-chome, Chuo Ward, Fukuoka
