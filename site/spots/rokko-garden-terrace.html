Rokko Garden Terrace

Kobe

At the top of Mt. Rokko, a series of terraces, cafes and a striking spiral observation tower called Rokko Shidare give visitors several different vantage points over the same sweeping view, so it's worth walking the full loop rather than stopping at the first lookout. On clear evenings the lights of Kobe, Osaka and the bay in between form one continuous carpet that's genuinely striking, and it's easy to see why it's long been marketed as one of Japan's great night views.

Beyond the view itself, the summit area has enough small attractions, a music box museum, a garden, seasonal illuminations, to make a half-day out of the trip rather than a quick photo stop. It gets busiest around sunset, when both photographers and couples arrive for the transition from daylight to city lights.

Reaching the top involves a cable car ride from the Rokko Cable Shita base station, which is itself a scenic, old-fashioned funicular ride worth savoring rather than rushing through. Because the summit sits noticeably higher and cooler than the city, pack a layer even in warmer months, and check cable car operating hours in advance since the last car down can be earlier than expected.

From Sannomiya, take a bus or train to Rokko Cable Shita, then ride the Rokko Cable Car to the summit, followed by a short shuttle bus to Garden Terrace; allow about an hour from Sannomiya.
