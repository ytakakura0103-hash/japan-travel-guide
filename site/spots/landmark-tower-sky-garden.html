Yokohama Landmark Tower Sky Garden

Yokohama

For decades this was Japan's tallest building, and the view from its Sky Garden observation floor still makes the case: the whole Minato Mirai waterfront unfolds below, from the Cosmo Clock 21 ferris wheel to the curve of the bay, and on a clear winter morning Mount Fuji is visible in the distance. The ride up is part of the appeal, since the elevators were engineered for speed and cover the climb in well under a minute.

Practically speaking, ticket counters are on the 69th floor itself and lines build up quickly around sunset, when locals come specifically to watch the lights switch on across the bay. Arriving an hour or so before dusk lets you see the skyline in both daylight and lit-up form without fighting the golden-hour crowd. The observation floor has closed for renovation stretches in the past, so it is worth a quick check that it is open before making the trip up.

Below the observation floor, the tower's lower levels hold a shopping mall and restaurants, making it easy to combine the view with a meal. Because the building sits right between Sakuragicho and Minatomirai stations, it works well as a first or last stop on a Minato Mirai walking route, bookending time spent at the Red Brick Warehouse or Cosmo World nearby.

2-2-1 Minatomirai, Nishi-ku, Yokohama
