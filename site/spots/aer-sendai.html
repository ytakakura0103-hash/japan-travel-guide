AER

Sendai

AER rises 145.5 meters beside Sendai Station, one of the tallest buildings in the Tohoku region, and splits its use between retail, offices, and public space across its height: the first four floors hold a shopping complex, the middle floors house public and small-business facilities, and the upper floors above that are largely offices and clinics. For visitors, the building's standout feature sits right at the top.

The 31st floor holds a free observation terrace, open daily except New Year's Day, giving sweeping views over Jozenji-dori, the Sendai Castle ruins, and the mountains around Zao, with the Pacific Ocean visible on especially clear days. Unlike paid observation decks elsewhere in Japan, there's no admission charge here, making it one of the best value viewpoints in the city for a skyline photo or simply a break from walking.

Combining a browse through the lower-floor shops with the trip up to the 31st-floor terrace makes for a fuller visit than treating the building as purely a shopping stop or purely a viewpoint. Its direct pedestrian-deck connection to Sendai Station makes it one of the easiest stops in the city to fit into a travel day, requiring no more than a short covered walk from the station itself.

Chuo, Aoba Ward, Sendai
