Kushida Shrine

Fukuoka

Kushida Shrine has watched over the old Hakata merchant district for well over a thousand years and is best known today as the spiritual home of the Hakata Gion Yamakasa festival. A full-size, intricately decorated Yamakasa float stands on near-permanent display inside the grounds (it usually comes down briefly each June for maintenance), so visitors who miss the actual July festival can still study the level of craftsmanship up close.

The compact grounds also hold a centuries-old ginkgo tree and several smaller shrines tucked into the corners, worth a slow lap rather than a quick photo stop. Entry is free and the shrine stays open from early morning to late evening, which makes a first-thing visit an easy way to see it before the tour groups pass through later in the day on their way to Kawabata Shopping Arcade or Canal City next door.

If your trip lines up with early July, the actual Hakata Gion Yamakasa festival culminates here with a dawn race of teams hauling heavy floats through the streets. It is spectacular, but expect serious crowds and closed roads around the shrine for several days beforehand, so budget extra time if visiting that week.

1-41 Kamikawabatamachi, Hakata Ward, Fukuoka

Open in Google Maps

5-minute walk from Gion Station (Kuko Line), Exit 2, or 2 minutes from Kushida-jinja-mae Station on the Nanakuma Line
