Sendai PARCO & PARCO 2

Sendai

Sendai PARCO has anchored the area near Kokubuncho-dori for years with a fashion-forward retail lineup typical of the PARCO brand, but the complex expanded significantly with the July 2016 opening of PARCO 2, a second building connected directly to Sendai Station's west exit pedestrian deck. Rather than duplicating the original store, PARCO 2 was deliberately built around a different concept, branded internally as shopping for adults who've thought it through, keeping apparel to only around thirty percent of its tenants in favor of lifestyle goods, zakka, food, and a cinema.

That shift toward a broader lifestyle offering, rather than a pure fashion focus, set PARCO 2 apart from typical PARCO stores elsewhere in Japan, and it includes several tenants making their first appearance anywhere in the Tohoku region. Together, the two buildings give Sendai PARCO a wider age range and shopping style than either would cover alone.

PARCO 2's direct station connection makes it one of the most convenient shopping stops for visitors working around a train schedule, while the original Sendai PARCO closer to Kokubuncho suits a more deliberate shopping trip combined with a walk through the nightlife district nearby.

Chuo/Kokubuncho area, Aoba Ward, Sendai
