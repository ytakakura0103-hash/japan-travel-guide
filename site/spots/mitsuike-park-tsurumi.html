Local Pick

Mitsuike Park

Yokohama

Laid out around a trio of connected ponds that give the park its name, meaning three ponds, Mitsuike Park has quietly built a reputation among Yokohama residents as one of the city's best cherry-blossom spots without ever making it onto most visitor itineraries. Several hundred cherry trees of different varieties are planted through the grounds, which means the bloom season here stretches out longer than at single-variety hanami spots elsewhere in the city.\n\nOutside cherry-blossom season, the park still functions as a genuinely pleasant green space, with wide lawns, a Japanese garden section, a small zoo-adjacent children's area, and walking paths around the water that draw joggers and dog walkers from the surrounding Tsurumi neighborhoods. It is large enough that even on a busy weekend you can find a quiet corner away from the crowds near the main entrance.\n\nGetting here takes a bit more effort than the central Minato Mirai attractions, which is precisely why it stays a locals' park rather than a tourist stop. If your visit lines up with cherry-blossom season, generally late March into early April, it is worth the detour for a hanami experience closer to how Yokohama residents actually spend the holiday.

6-1 Terao, Tsurumi-ku, Yokohama

Open in Google Maps

About 15 minutes by bus from JR Tsurumi Station, or roughly a 20-minute walk from Tsurumi-Odemachi area.
