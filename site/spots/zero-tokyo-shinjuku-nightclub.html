ZERO TOKYO

Tokyo

ZERO TOKYO occupies three basement levels, B2 through B4, of the Tokyu Kabukicho Tower, split across five distinct areas rather than a single dance floor, giving it more range than most Kabukicho clubs in terms of moving between different music and atmosphere without leaving the building. The same space doubles as Zepp Shinjuku by day, a proper concert venue, so the room itself is built to full live-music production standards rather than a converted bar.

That dual identity, live house by day, nightclub after dark, means the sound system and stage setup are a notch above what a typical club room offers, something audiophile-minded visitors will likely notice. Its location inside one of Kabukicho's newest landmark buildings also puts it in the middle of the district's densest concentration of entertainment and dining under one roof, useful for combining a club night with dinner or other Kabukicho Tower attractions beforehand.

As with the rest of Kabukicho, the walk in from either station exit passes through heavy signage and tout activity, so the same standard advice applies: go straight to the venue and ignore unsolicited approaches from people on the street claiming to offer a better deal elsewhere.

Tokyu Kabukicho Tower B1-B4F, 1-29-1 Kabukicho, Shinjuku City, Tokyo

About 7 minutes on foot from JR Shinjuku Station's east exit, or 2 minutes from Seibu-Shinjuku Station's main exit.
