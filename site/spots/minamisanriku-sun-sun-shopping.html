Minamisanriku Sun Sun Shopping Street

Sendai

Minamisanriku was among the towns hit hardest by the 2011 tsunami, and this shopping street, designed by the well-known architect Kengo Kuma, was built on land deliberately raised by around ten meters as part of the town's rebuilding effort. Around two dozen small wooden shops, using local cedar and traditional construction methods with deep overhanging eaves, sell fresh seafood, local produce, and souvenirs in a layout meant to recreate some of the street-level community feeling the town lost.

Browsing the shops is free, and meals of fresh local seafood are reasonably priced. A memorial park commemorating the disaster sits close by, adding a more reflective note to the visit.

Because Minamisanriku sits off the main Sendai-Matsushima tourist route, it draws a modest, steady stream of visitors rather than crowds, giving the shopping street a genuinely unhurried feel. It works well paired with a stop at the adjacent memorial for a fuller picture of the town's recovery.
