Japan Monkey Park

Nagoya

Japan Monkey Park occupies a wooded hillside above the Kiso River, combining a genuinely significant zoological institution with a fairly conventional Japanese amusement park under one admission ticket. The zoological half, the Japan Monkey Centre, holds one of the largest primate collections on earth, with dozens of species and several hundred individual animals spanning macaques, gibbons, lemurs and great apes from Asia, Africa and the Americas.

Its best-known attraction is Monkey Valley, where visitors can watch Japanese macaques wash sweet potatoes in a running stream, a learned behavior first documented in wild macaque populations decades ago and now considered an example of animal culture being passed between generations. On the amusement park side, a day pass typically covers a set number of rides, including roller coasters, a Ferris wheel and gentler attractions for younger children, with a pool in summer and an ice rink in winter rounding out the seasonal offerings.

It is a good match for families who want more active entertainment than a castle or museum visit offers, without leaving the Inuyama area.

26 Kanrin, Inuyama, Aichi

About a 5-minute bus ride from the east exit of Inuyama Station on the Meitetsu line.
