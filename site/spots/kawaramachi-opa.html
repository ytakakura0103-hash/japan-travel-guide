Kawaramachi OPA

Kyoto

Kawaramachi OPA opened in November 1998 as a fashion and lifestyle building aimed squarely at a younger customer base, roughly teens through thirties, spread across eight floors and a basement with more than a hundred shops concentrated toward women's fashion on the lower levels. A Tower Records outlet and a basement food court with options like Sweets Paradise round out the mix, giving it a more casual, mall-like character than Kyoto's traditional department stores.

The OPA brand itself has shrunk considerably across the Kansai region in recent years, and as of early 2026, Kawaramachi OPA is one of only three OPA-branded stores still operating in the area, alongside two locations in Kobe, after Osaka's long-running Shinsaibashi OPA closed in January 2026 after thirty-one years. That makes the Kyoto location something of a holdout for a brand that once had a wider footprint across the region's shopping scene.

Individual tenants inside cycle in and out fairly regularly, typical of a youth-fashion building chasing current trends, so the specific shop lineup on any given visit may differ from what's listed online. Its location directly at Shijo-Kawaramachi puts it within a few minutes' walk of Takashimaya, Kyoto BAL, and Nishiki Market, making it easy to fold into a broader shopping loop through the area.
