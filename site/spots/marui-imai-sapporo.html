Marui Imai

Hokkaido

Marui Imai's roots trace back to 1872, when it began as a sundries stall called Imai Shoten, becoming a kimono drapery in 1874 and eventually a full department store in 1916, making it Hokkaido's oldest department store by a wide margin. At its peak the chain operated seven branches across the prefecture; today only two remain, the Sapporo main store covered here and a smaller Hakodate branch, both now part of the Isetan Mitsukoshi group alongside Sapporo Mitsukoshi nearby.

The Sapporo store spans two connected buildings, the Odori Building and the Ichijo Building, splitting roughly between women's fashion, cosmetics, and accessories in one and menswear, luxury boutiques, and home goods in the other. That division gives the store a more deliberately organized shopping experience than a single undifferentiated building, useful for visitors with a specific category in mind.

Its central Odori location puts it within easy walking distance of Odori Park, Sapporo Mitsukoshi, and Sapporo PARCO, making it simple to combine with a broader day of shopping in the area. As the region's oldest department store, it also carries a certain historical weight for visitors interested in Hokkaido's retail history beyond the shopping itself.

Odori, Chuo Ward, Sapporo
