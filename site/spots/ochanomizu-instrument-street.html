Ochanomizu Instrument Street

Tokyo

Ochanomizu Instrument Street runs along Meidai-dori, a stretch lined with dozens of independent guitar, bass, and instrument shops that has made this corner of Chiyoda City a genuine hub for working musicians rather than a tourist-oriented shopping street. Vintage guitars, new instruments across a wide range of budgets, sheet music, and specialty repair shops sit side by side, and it's common to hear scattered practicing drifting out from shop doorways as customers test instruments before buying.

Because it caters primarily to musicians rather than casual browsers, the street sees relatively few tourists despite sitting just a short walk from Akihabara, making it a quieter, more specialized alternative for visitors interested in something other than anime and electronics shopping. A casual walk down the street and a glance into a few shop windows takes about twenty to thirty minutes; anyone seriously shopping for an instrument, especially vintage or higher-end pieces, should expect to spend considerably longer trying instruments out.

There is no fixed opening or closing pattern across the whole street since each shop operates independently, though most keep standard daytime retail hours and some close on specific weekdays, so checking a specific shop's hours in advance is worth doing if you have one in mind. Weekday afternoons tend to be quieter and give more room to browse without competing with other customers at instrument-testing counters.

Kanda-Surugadai area, Chiyoda City, Tokyo
