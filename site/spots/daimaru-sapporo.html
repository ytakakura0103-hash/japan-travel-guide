Daimaru Sapporo

Hokkaido

Daimaru Sapporo opened in 2003 alongside JR Tower and holds a distinction none of the city's other department stores can claim: it's the only one directly connected to Sapporo Station, making it the most convenient stop for anyone arriving or departing by train. Spread across eight floors and four basement levels, its aisles run roughly three meters wide, noticeably broader than the more traditional layouts at Marui Imai or Sapporo Mitsukoshi, giving the store a more open, easier-to-navigate feel.

Its tenant list leans into a mix of standard department store fare and specific draws worth knowing about: a Pokemon Center Sapporo brings pop-culture appeal alongside the usual fashion and cosmetics floors, and a dedicated shime-parfait cafe taps into Sapporo's distinctive after-dinner dessert culture. The eighth-floor restaurant zone offers a range of dining options for a meal without leaving the building.

Given its direct station connection, Daimaru Sapporo works well as a first or last stop on a Sapporo visit, whether killing time before a train or picking up a final souvenir on the way out of the city. Its basement food floor is worth a look even for visitors not planning to shop the fashion floors above.
