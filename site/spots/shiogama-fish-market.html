Shiogama Fish Market

Sendai

Shiogama has long been one of Japan's most significant tuna landing ports, and the fish market built around that trade functions first as a genuine wholesale operation and second as a place tourists can wander and eat. Vendors sell fresh and processed seafood at stalls spread across the market floor, while several sushi counters and small restaurants inside or near the building turn the catch into breakfast and lunch the same day it arrives.\n\nLocals point to Shiogama's improbably high number of sushi restaurants per capita as a point of civic pride, a direct byproduct of the port's steady supply of fresh fish, and eating a sushi breakfast here is a genuinely different experience from sushi almost anywhere else in the region, given how directly it connects to the boats a few hundred meters away.\n\nPrices for market food range from cheap snack-style seafood to a full sushi breakfast that costs more but is well regarded as good value for the quality. The market is busiest and best in the morning, when the widest range of fresh catch is still available; arriving before mid-morning is worth the earlier start, and a weekday visit avoids the bigger weekend crowds.

3-27 Shiodome, Shiogama, Miyagi
