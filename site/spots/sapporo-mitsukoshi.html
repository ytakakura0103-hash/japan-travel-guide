Sapporo Mitsukoshi

Hokkaido

Sapporo Mitsukoshi sits almost directly at Odori Station, a minute's walk at most, and carries the national Mitsukoshi brand's reputation for luxury goods into Hokkaido's retail scene, distinguishing it from the more locally rooted Marui Imai nearby. Fashion, jewelry, and watch floors lean toward established international and Japanese luxury labels, giving the store a more upscale character than a general department store.

Its basement food hall is a particular draw, spread across two levels and packed with ramen counters, sushi, and sweets, a dense concentration of options that works as well for a quick meal as for gift shopping. As with most department store depachika in Japan, it's worth a stop even for visitors not planning to browse the fashion floors above.

Being part of the Isetan Mitsukoshi group, alongside sister company Marui Imai just down the street, gives visitors an easy opportunity to compare Sapporo's two major department stores directly, both within a few minutes' walk of Odori Station and Odori Park.

Odori, Chuo Ward, Sapporo
