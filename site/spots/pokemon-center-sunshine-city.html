Pokemon Center Sunshine City

Tokyo

The Pokemon Center inside Sunshine City is one of the largest official Pokemon retail stores in Japan, spread across a sizeable footprint on one of the complex's floors and stocked with a far wider range of plush toys, stationery, apparel, and collectible cards than the smaller Pokemon Center outlets found in other shopping centers. Certain items and regional plush designs are exclusive to specific store locations, so collectors sometimes make a point of comparing this branch against others in Tokyo or Osaka rather than assuming the stock is identical everywhere.

A quick browse takes twenty to thirty minutes, but dedicated fans and anyone shopping for a specific figure or card set should expect to spend closer to an hour working through the shelves, especially during new merchandise launches tied to a game or anime release, when lines can form at the entrance before opening. Weekday mornings shortly after the store opens are the most comfortable time to shop without crowding; weekends, especially afternoons, draw families and collectors in much larger numbers.

The store does not charge admission, unlike some Pokemon-themed attractions elsewhere, so it's an easy add-on to a visit to Sunshine City's aquarium or planetarium rather than a separate destination. Popular limited-edition items can sell out during a visit, so checking online for current stock or new releases beforehand is worth doing if there's something specific on your list.
