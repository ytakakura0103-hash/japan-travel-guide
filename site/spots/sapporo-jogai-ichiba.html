Sapporo Jogai Ichiba (Central Wholesale Market Outer Market)

Hokkaido

Sapporo's Central Wholesale Market handles the city's real seafood and produce trade, and the outer market (jogai ichiba) that grew up around it is where that trade becomes browsable for the public. It's considerably larger and more sprawling than Nijo Market downtown, with rows of stalls selling crab, uni, salmon roe, melon, and prepared foods, plus several kaisen-don restaurants serving the catch straight from the neighboring stalls.

Because it sits outside the central sightseeing loop in a working commercial district rather than near the main hotels, it draws a genuinely mixed crowd of chefs restocking, locals doing weekly shopping, and visitors, which keeps the atmosphere closer to a functioning market than a curated food hall. Some stalls open very early to serve the wholesale trade, so mornings are the liveliest and freshest time to visit.

It's a bigger, less polished experience than the more centrally located markets, and that's part of its appeal for anyone who has already done the standard Sapporo seafood-market stop and wants a less staged version.

Winter crab season and summer melon season are the two clearest reasons to time a visit, though there's fresh seafood worth eating here year-round.

Kita 11-jo, Nishi 21-chome, Chuo-ku, Sapporo, Hokkaido 060-0011

Open in Google Maps

About 10 minutes by taxi from Sapporo Station, or take the subway to Nijuyonken Station and walk about 5-10 minutes.
