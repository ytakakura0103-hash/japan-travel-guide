Onomichi Hondori Shopping Street

Hiroshima

Hondori runs in a long, mostly straight line parallel to the waterfront through the older part of Onomichi, and much of it is covered, giving it a slightly faded, retro character shaped by the port town's history as a shipping and trade hub going back to the medieval period. The arcade mixes still-operating traditional shops selling local goods with a fair number of vacant or converted storefronts, a visible sign of the town's shrinking population that has also made it attractive to independent cafes, secondhand bookstores, and small galleries moving into low-rent space.

Onomichi has a literary reputation in Japan, having been the setting or home for several well-known writers and filmmakers, and Hondori and its side alleys retain something of that atmosphere, with narrow lanes branching uphill toward the temple district and its many stone stairways. The street is a natural base for exploring the town on foot, since it connects easily to both the harbor and the lower stations of the Onomichi Temple Walk and Neko no Hosomichi cat alley.

Casual food options along the street include Onomichi's distinctive style of ramen, as well as smaller cafes serving coffee and simple lunch sets, making it easy to combine shopping with a meal break.

Tsuchidocho, Onomichi, Hiroshima Prefecture
