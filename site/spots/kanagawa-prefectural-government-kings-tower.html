Kanagawa Prefectural Government Building (King's Tower)

Yokohama

Rebuilt in 1928 after its predecessor was destroyed in the 1923 earthquake, this building still functions as the prefectural government office, which means most of its interior is off-limits to casual visitors, but its exterior, and particularly the roughly 49-meter tower capped with a stepped, pagoda-influenced top, is the real attraction. Together with the Customs building's Queen's Tower and the Port Opening Memorial Hall's Jack's Tower, it forms what locals call the Three Towers, tied to a local tradition that spotting all three from one vantage point brings good luck.\n\nBecause it remains an active government building, opening hours for any public areas are limited and it is best treated as an exterior architecture stop rather than a museum visit. It is easiest to appreciate as part of a short loop taking in all three towers, since the fun of the site is partly the local legend around viewing them together.\n\nThe surrounding Nihon-odori street was itself Japan's first Western-style boulevard, laid out after the 1866 fire cleared much of the original settlement, so the walk between the towers also passes through one of the city's oldest planned streets, worth a slower look beyond just the buildings themselves.

1-1 Nihon-odori, Naka-ku, Yokohama
