Cup Noodles Museum Yokohama

Yokohama

This museum tells the story of Momofuku Ando, the inventor of instant ramen and later the Cup Noodle, through exhibits that are more playful than a typical corporate museum, including a recreation of the shed where he experimented with the original recipe. The main draw for most visitors is the My CUPNOODLES workshop, where you design your own cup graphics and choose from a wall of soup bases and toppings to create a one-of-a-kind noodle cup to take home.

That workshop requires a separate paid ticket bought on-site or in advance, and it fills up during busy periods, particularly school holidays and weekend afternoons, so arriving close to opening time gives the best chance of a same-day slot. There is also a noodle-making workshop for younger children and a food court themed around instant noodle dishes from around the world.

Given the museum's popularity with families and school groups, weekday late mornings tend to be calmer than weekends. It sits an easy walk from the Red Brick Warehouse and World Porters, so it fits naturally into a half-day loop of the Shinko waterfront district, and the building itself, with its open central atrium, is worth a look even for visitors skipping the workshops.

2-3-4 Shinko, Naka-ku, Yokohama 231-0001
