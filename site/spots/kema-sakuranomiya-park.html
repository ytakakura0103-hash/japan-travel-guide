Kema Sakuranomiya Park

Osaka

Running along the Okawa River just north of central Osaka, this promenade is planted with roughly several thousand cherry trees in a long, continuous line, making it one of the most heavily visited hanami spots in the city each spring. During the blossom season, typically late March into early April depending on the year, the riverbank fills with picnic groups, food stalls, and boats offering short rides along the water beneath the flowering canopy, and the atmosphere is lively rather than quiet.

Outside blossom season, the park settles into a much calmer role as a riverside walking and cycling route, popular with joggers and locals out for a stroll, with views across the water toward the Osaka Mint building and the city skyline beyond. A full walk along the promenade takes roughly an hour at an easy pace, though most visitors simply pick a stretch to walk or sit rather than covering it end to end.

During peak cherry blossom weekends, arriving early in the morning is the most reliable way to find a good spot along the riverbank before the crowds build through the afternoon and into the evening, when the trees are sometimes lit for nighttime viewing. There's no admission fee to walk the park, though vendors selling food and drink during blossom season charge their own prices.
