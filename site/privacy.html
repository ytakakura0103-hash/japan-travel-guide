Privacy Policy

Curious City is written and run by one person (see About) as a personal, non-commercial project. This page explains what data gets collected when you visit, and what choices you have about it.

What this site does (and doesn't) collect

Curious City doesn't have user accounts, logins, or forms that collect personal information, and it doesn't run ads or affiliate links. Beyond standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited — kept for basic security and troubleshooting), no other data is collected by this site, and no advertising or tracking cookies are set.

Your rights

If you're in the EEA or UK, you have rights under the GDPR/UK GDPR to access, correct, or delete any personal data associated with you. Since this site doesn't hold accounts, profiles, or advertising cookies, there's very little personal data to begin with — mainly the basic server logs described above. For any request, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site changes. Check back occasionally if you have concerns.
