Privacy Policy

Curious City is written and run by one person (see About). This page explains what data gets collected when you visit, and what choices you have about it.

Cookies and advertising

This site displays ads through Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may set cookies or use similar technology on your device to serve ads and measure their performance, and, where you've given consent, to personalize the ads you see based on your browsing activity.

You can see and adjust how Google personalizes ads for you at Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of personalized advertising more broadly through aboutads.info (US) or youronlinechoices.eu (EU).

Your privacy choices (EEA, UK, and Switzerland)

If you're visiting from the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you'll see a consent prompt the first time you load this site, before any advertising cookies that aren't strictly necessary are set. You can allow or decline personalized advertising there, and change your choice at any time — look for a "privacy settings" or "manage cookies" link, usually in the site footer.

Declining doesn't stop ads from showing; it just means the ads you see won't be based on your browsing activity elsewhere.

What else this site does (and doesn't) collect

Curious City doesn't have user accounts, logins, or forms that collect personal information. Beyond standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited — kept for basic security and troubleshooting) and the advertising cookies described above, no other data is collected directly by this site.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site are affiliate links (clearly marked "[PR]"). If you book or purchase through one of these links, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This doesn't affect which places are listed or how they're described.

Your rights

If you're in the EEA or UK, you have rights under the GDPR/UK GDPR to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of any personal data associated with you, and to object to its use for advertising. Since this site doesn't hold accounts or personal profiles, most of these requests would concern advertising cookies — see the sections above for how to manage those directly. For anything else, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site changes — for example, once a consent management tool is added ahead of running ads to EEA/UK visitors, or if analytics tools are introduced. Check back occasionally if you have concerns.
